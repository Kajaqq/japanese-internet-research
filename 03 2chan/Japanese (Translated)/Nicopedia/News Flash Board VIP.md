## 1. The News Flash (VIP) Board (ニュース速報(VIP)板)

The **News Flash (VIP) Board** (often just called *VIP*) is historically the most populated and representative message board on 2channel, established on June 18, 2004.

Originally, VIP was created as a quarantine zone. The standard "News Flash" board had implemented a "trash thread repulsion feature" (a system to filter out spam or off-topic posts), and VIP was built to catch all the garbage threads that got filtered out. Simply put, it was born as a digital dumping ground. Although the repulsion feature was quickly abolished, VIP instantly took on a life of its own and reigned supreme as the most highly active board on 2channel.

Despite having "News Flash" in its name, it has absolutely nothing to do with news. It functions as a "no rules, anything goes" general chat board. Furthermore, while "VIP" normally means "Very Important Person," it was given to this board as an ironic insult toward the "trash" users who were quarantined there.

### The "VIPPER" Identity
Users of the VIP board call themselves **VIPPERs**. 

Unlike the users of the standard News board who shamelessly view themselves as internet elites, the default persona of a VIPPER is a "low-spec human being"—typically self-identifying as a NEET (Not in Education, Employment, or Training), a virgin, and poorly educated. Because of this, they are generally perceived as idiotic (for better or worse). It is a running joke that VIPPERs cannot read anything longer than three lines, giving birth to the classic slang, "Too long, summarize it in three lines" (長い、三行で - the Japanese equivalent of *tl;dr*).

They are highly energetic and reactive. There is an old saying that "wherever VIPPERs pass, not a single blade of grass or tree is left standing." 

In the past, VIPPERs frequently organized "charges" or "raids" (突撃), swarming threads on other specialized boards and leaving massive amounts of spam like, "I have come from VIP" (VIPから来ますた). Because of this, older, more isolated 2channel communities absolutely despised VIPPERs.

Perhaps because they proudly named themselves VIPPERs, they have a bizarre sense of elitism and tribalism. Self-proclaimed veterans and newcomers constantly fight. However, because the board is completely anonymous, it is impossible to tell who is actually a veteran, leading to many newcomers faking veteran status.

Though massive in numbers, they are largely seen as mob forces who show up to fuel internet fires, blog flamewars, and "festivals" (online riots). They lack real independence or leadership. As the saying goes: *"Terrifying as an enemy, but completely unreliable as an ally."*

## 2. VIP Quality (VIPクオリティ)

In its early days, VIP was a chaotic paradise. Out of this Genesis-like chaos was born countless "Legendary threads," "God threads," and "Trash threads."

Around the mid-2000s, boosted by phenomena like the *Densha Otoko* (Train Man) boom and the spread of broadband, 2channel hit its peak user count. VIP expanded rapidly and within a few years was recognized as the face of 2channel. The sheer momentum and wealth of original memes that came out of the board birthed the phrase **"VIP Quality"** (VIPクオリティ). For a VIPPER, having a thread or meme labeled "VIP Quality" was the highest form of praise.

Many famous ASCII Arts (AA) and internet slang terms heavily used on Nico Nico Douga actually originated in VIP. Examples include the running character `⊂二二二（ ＾ω＾）二⊃ Boon` and sentence-ending particles like `~dao` (だお). 

> **Cultural Context: Shoko Nakagawa (Shokotan)**
> The article explicitly notes: *"Keep in mind, these did NOT originate from Shokotan."* Shoko Nakagawa (a famous otaku idol) frequently used VIP slang on her highly popular mainstream blog. This led the general public to believe *she* invented the slang, which endlessly infuriated the VIPPERs who actually created it.

However, around 2006, the user base began a slow decline. As internet-connected devices became cheaper, a massive influx of "newcomers" (shinzan) diluted the culture, leading to a drop in quality. Today, the board is mostly a chaotic mess of argument threads and endless serialized discussion threads, retaining almost none of its former glory. Even so, it remains the most accessed board on 5channel (formerly 2channel).

## 3. The Love-Hate Relationship with Nico Nico Douga

In the earliest days of Nico Nico Douga, the user base was comprised almost entirely of 2channel News board users and VIPPERs. They are the ones who established the cynical, meme-heavy direction of Niconico's culture today.

However, as Niconico opened its account registration to the general public (during the "Gamma" era), normie internet users who didn't understand VIP's harsh etiquette (or its inside jokes) began flooding Niconico. Friction immediately sparked.
VIPPERs labeled these new users **"Nico-chu"** (ニコ厨 - Niconico Addicts). Whenever a Niconico-related thread was posted on VIP, it would be instantly filled with insults, trolling, and mockery. 

> **Cultural Context: The "Nico-chu" Stigma**
> To a VIPPER, a "Nico-chu" is a cringeworthy kid who takes internet memes too seriously, doesn't understand the unwritten rules of anonymous posting, and loudly brings Niconico culture into spaces where it doesn't belong.

This created a reverse-flow phenomenon. While Niconico was overcrowded, VIP was losing users. Young Niconico users started migrating to VIP (often because they watched "2channel Summary" videos on Niconico). This terrified and annoyed VIPPERs, causing them to vehemently reject any topic related to Niconico.

While the older 2channel users from the standard News board often pat themselves on the back saying, *"We raised Nico Nico,"* VIPPERs genuinely hate Nico-chus and will blindly attack them.

That being said, most VIPPERs don't actually hate Niconico *as a platform*. A common sentiment is, *"I watch Nico Nico Douga with the comments turned off, but I hate Nico-chus."* Regardless of whether they actively use it, almost every VIPPER has a Niconico account. They still occasionally organize "Erotic Video Hunting Festivals" on the site or raid Niconico Live streams, proving that the two communities are never truly severed.

Today, Nico-chus and VIPPERs fight like cats and dogs, but outsiders view them as two sides of the same coin, sharing a symbiotic relationship. Modern Niconico users shouldn't underestimate just how massively VIPPERs influenced the Niconico they enjoy today.

## 4. VIP Today

*   **Age Demographic:** The age range has stretched massively, from elementary school kids to 70-year-old men, though the majority are between middle school age and their 40s/50s.
*   **Matome (Summary) Sites:** The internet is now flooded with "2ch Summary Blogs" that scrape VIP for content. Because users now post specifically trying to get featured on these blogs, the organic quality of the board has plummeted.
*   **Smartphones:** The rise of smartphone posting means those who remember the "old days" of PC-based VIP culture are now a severe minority.

## 5. Notable VIP Inventions (Selected Related Terms)

*Because of VIP's massive influence on otaku culture, several major internet phenomenons were born here. Of particular note to Vocaloid fans:*

*   **VIPPALOID (Kasane Teto / Yokune Ruko, etc.):** 
    In 2008, as Hatsune Miku and Vocaloid were exploding in popularity on Niconico, VIPPERs decided to play a massive April Fool's prank on the "Nico-chus." They pooled their skills to draw professional-looking character art, write fake lore, and create a fake website for a "New Official Vocaloid" named **Kasane Teto** (重音テト). They successfully tricked thousands of Niconico users. Ironically, users later gave Teto a real voice using the freeware synthesizer UTAU. Today, Kasane Teto is entirely embraced by the Vocaloid community and is one of the most famous virtual singers in the world—all starting as a VIPPER troll project.

*   **First Coil Shock (第一次コイルショック):**
    When an official Pokémon popularity poll was held online, VIPPERs banded together to artificially inflate the votes for Magnemite (Coil in Japanese) just to ruin the rankings for popular Pokémon like Pikachu.
    
*   **Inazuma Eleven Popularity Poll Incident:**
    A similar trolling operation where VIPPERs hijacked a children's anime popularity poll to vote a completely irrelevant background character to first place.
