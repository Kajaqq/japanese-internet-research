# News Flash Board (ニュース速報板)
This is a translation from #source/nicopedia, for the wikipedia page, check [[News Flash Board|News Flash Board]]

The **News Flash Board** (ニュース速報板, *Nyūsu Sokuhou Ita*) is one of the many message boards found on the giant anonymous textboard forum, 2channel (now 5channel).

It is a "proud and noble board" where supposedly *only* super-elites—graduates from former Imperial Universities, earning over 10 million yen a year, and standing over 180cm tall—are permitted to post. *(Note: This is a long-running, deeply sarcastic inside joke).*

---

## Overview

The board was established in 1999 as 2channel's hub for current events. In its early days, it literally served as the center of 2channel. It was the driving force behind numerous internet "festivals" (matsuri).

> **Cultural Context: The Tashiro Festival (田代祭り)**
> A "matsuri" (festival) in 2channel slang refers to a massive, coordinated internet flash mob or trolling campaign. The "Tashiro Festival" of 2001 is one of the most legendary: 2channel users banded together to mass-vote Masashi Tashiro—a disgraced Japanese comedian who had recently been arrested—to the #1 spot in *TIME Magazine's* "Person of the Year" online poll.

Later on, the board lost many users due to the massive rise of the **VIP Board** (News Flash VIP). However, it maintained a solid standing as a refuge for users who couldn't stand the chaotic, hyper-energetic atmosphere of VIP. Compared to VIP, whose user base is primarily teenagers and people in their early 20s, the content of the News board suggests a slightly older demographic, mostly in their late 20s to 40s.

While it is fundamentally a board for discussing news, today, it is effectively an "anything goes" board. As long as the original poster (referred to as **>>1**) includes a news source link in the first post, the thread can be about anime, manga, games, voice actors, or anything else. "Fishing" (clickbait/troll) thread titles or punchline titles are extremely common, and these threads frequently devolve into casual chatting completely unrelated to the news.

> **Cultural Context: >>1 (Resu-anchor)**
> On 2channel, users reference other posts using the `>>` symbol followed by the post number. `>>1` refers to the original post that started the thread.

The board has a pseudo-rivalry with the **News Flash + (Plus)** board. However, while the "+" board actually discusses news seriously, users on the standard News board mock the news, derail conversations, and chat casually—in other words, the users are highly unserious. If you come in with a stiff attitude and post long, serious arguments, you will likely be told to "Go to +".

Because the 2channel administrators (like founder Hiroyuki and admin "Fox") treated this board as an experimental testing ground, its features changed constantly. Consequently, the rise and fall of new memes on the board is dizzyingly fast, and many famous Japanese internet memes originated or were popularized here.

In terms of posting volume, it usually ranks between 2nd and 5th across all of 2channel (competing with live-reaction boards, News+, and the idol-focused board). It has about half the traffic of the #1 board, VIP. However, whenever a major incident happens in a specialized hobby board, the standard phrase is still, *"Go make a thread on the News board about it."* For many, it remains the true "center" of 2channel.

### Earthquake Speed
There is a famous saying: *"When an earthquake hits, go to the News board."* They are legendary for how fast earthquake threads are created—it is completely normal for a thread to go up *while the ground is still shaking*. Yet, no matter how fast a thread is posted, it is a running joke for users to heckle the poster with, *"Too slow!"* 

Additionally, no matter where the earthquake occurs—even if the epicenter is the middle of Tokyo—it is a traditional board custom to reply with *"Doko ina?"* (どこいな - short for "Where in the countryside is this?").

### The 2012 Exodus
In early January (2012), the board was struck by the "Stealth Marketing Riot." Most threads ceased to function, and a massive wave of users migrated to the **Kenmou** (嫌儲 - Anti-Profit) board. 

> **Cultural Context: The Ste-ma Riot and Kenmou**
> 2channel users realized that "Matome" (Summary) blogs were scraping 2ch threads and heavily manipulating narratives for ad revenue (Stealth Marketing). Furious that their anonymous posts were being monetized, thousands of News board users fled to the "Kenmou" board, a space specifically dedicated to hating anyone who profits off internet culture. 

Later that same month, the board took further catastrophic damage from a self-inflicted spam script called "Let's go, old News."

## Nico Nico Douga on the News Board

Threads linking to Nico Nico Douga news articles frequently pop up on the board, and they are almost always filled with comments bashing or criticizing Niconico. 

Despite claiming to be an elite group of tall, highly educated, high-income earners, for some reason, the users on this board never have girlfriends and don't even own clothes to wear outside. 

In reality, threads about Niconico have been created on the News board since the platform's earliest inception (roughly two days after Nico Nico Douga (Temp) launched in 2006). It is highly likely that many News board users possess very small Niconico account ID numbers (proving they were among the very first people to register for the site).

In short, the Niconico-bashing on the News board is just **self-hatred (同族嫌悪)** and being **Tsundere**.

*Note: The board is commonly abbreviated as "Nyūsoku" (ニュー速) or "ν-soku" (using the Greek letter Nu).*
