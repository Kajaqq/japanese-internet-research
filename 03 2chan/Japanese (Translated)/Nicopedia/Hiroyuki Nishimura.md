**Hiroyuki** is the nickname of **Hiroyuki Nishimura** (西村博之, Nishimura Hiroyuki; born November 16, 1976), founder and former administrator of “2channel,” one of Japan's largest collections of message boards. The source describes its administration and operating rights as having been transferred to a Singaporean company.[^tn-hiro-perspective]

Born November 16, 1976, in Kanagawa Prefecture and raised in Tokyo. His father worked for the National Tax Bureau.

He graduated from Tokyo Metropolitan Kitazono High School and the Faculty of Letters at Chūō University. In high school, Hiroyuki once bluntly asked a teacher, “Why do we have to study mathematics?” and, according to the article, won the argument.

He was well known as a fixed-handle user on Amezou—indeed, something close to a troll. However, because he diligently managed the board and replied to posts, people gradually warmed to him.

He is said to have founded 2channel while at university, although the details remain unclear. According to one account, he was studying abroad when it was founded, and there were hopes that operating on an overseas server might overcome the legal problems Amezou faced.

Niconico users and 2channel users call him **“Hiroyuki,” “God,”** and **“Maroyuki.”** In the user-broadcast community he is called **“Java Man.”** ~~In many cases, people use the widely recognized nickname “Tarako” (“cod roe”).~~

Although his face is now widely known, until he was interviewed about the Nishitetsu bus hijacking, people thought he looked like the famous “creepy otaku” ASCII-art face. In fact, that ASCII art had originally been created as “Hiroyuki's face.”

Nicknames that vary the spelling of “Hiroyuki,” such as “Maroyuki” and “Piroyuki,” apparently originated in the “Questions for Hiroyuki-kun” thread that he created on July 29, 1999. In post #60 someone asked, “Can I try calling you Piroyuki?” He replied in #65, “Piroyuki or Paroyuki is fine. Yes.”

He is the former administrator of the huge message-board service **2channel** and a director of **Brazil, Ltd. (Mirai Kensaku Brazil)**. He was also a director when **Niwango Co., Ltd.** was established, but resigned on February 18, 2013.

He sometimes produces Hatsune Miku music and posts videos; see **Tarako-P**.[^tn-hiro-p]

A child at heart who loves games, forever nineteen. He is an **Umaibō enthusiast**, with spicy cod roe as his favorite flavor. As a result, people who hear of his fondness for Umaibō often bring him the snacks whenever they meet him.

He receives so many that he apparently appreciates regional varieties and other Umaibō flavors he rarely gets to eat.

At the end of 2007 his favorite was **Jagarico**. In his words, **“Jagarico is a drink.”**

He also apparently likes thick-cut potato chips and buys them by the case.

Between *Takenoko no Sato* and *Kinoko no Yama*, he somewhat prefers the latter, supposedly because it contains more chocolate.

His position nevertheless seems to be, “If you want chocolate, wouldn't it be better just to eat a chocolate bar?”

As an elementary-school student, he named his ruler “Lone Wolf” and treasured it. He got angry when someone broke it.

On April 20, 2014, he announced his marriage on Twitter. The marriage itself had been registered on the 19th.

As of 2018, he lives in France.[^1]

## Lawsuits and compensation

As 2channel's former administrator, he has faced many lawsuits concerning the site. He has lost most of them through proceedings in his absence, leaving him liable for large damages. However, he has no intention of paying. Since, as discussed below, he has paid his taxes, his defiant stance extends only to civil matters.

On damages, “Hiroyuki Keeps Losing Lawsuits: Total Compensation Around ¥400 Million?”[^2] gives figures attributed to research by the *Yomiuri Shimbun*: approximately ¥58 million in compensation and coercive payments of ¥880,000 per day for failing to comply with provisional court orders, bringing the accumulated total to approximately ¥434 million.

According to *Why I Abandoned 2channel*, he thinks everything through logically and investigates thoroughly; presumably, then, he has confirmed that the existing legal system poses no problem if he does not pay. Even if future legal amendments made this criminally punishable, they would not ordinarily apply retroactively.[^3][^tn-hiro-law]

On December 20, 2012, the Tokyo Metropolitan Police referred his case to prosecutors on suspicion of aiding a violation of the Anti-Drug Special Provisions Act.[^4] There had been indications nine months earlier that this referral was coming. For details, see the 2ch drug-posting controversy. In March 2013, prosecutors decided not to indict him.[^5]

Incidentally, in his writing Hiroyuki says that he was a delinquent in high school and committed several crimes. Before creating 2ch he also created a message board called “True Accounts! How to Make Traffic Violations Disappear and Avoid Paying Fines,” a title seemingly calculated to anger the police. The article takes this as evidence of strong hostility toward the police and other public authorities.

## Undeclared income and the 2channel information leak: August 2013 onward

On August 24, 2013, it emerged that Hiroyuki had failed to declare ¥100 million in income, and media outlets reported it widely.

The substance of the reports was that, of approximately ¥350 million in 2channel advertising revenue, the National Tax Bureau considered about ¥100 million transferred to Packet Monster to be Hiroyuki's income, and therefore judged that he had failed to declare ¥100 million.

2channel received approximately ¥350 million in advertising revenue between 2009 and 2012. This revenue moved from Tokyo Plus Co., Ltd., of which Hiroyuki was president, to Packet Monster. Of the ¥350 million, approximately ¥200 million-plus was treated as Hiroyuki's income and approximately ¥100 million as Packet Monster's income.

Although he did not pay the damages discussed above, he apparently did pay taxes, declaring the ¥200 million-plus as income. However, because the National Tax Bureau regarded Packet Monster as a shell company, it determined that money paid to the company also constituted his personal income.

He has already paid the taxes on that ¥100 million: ¥30 million.

Three days before this news, on August 21, a major personal-information leak occurred on 2channel, becoming public on August 26. For details, see the 2channel personal-information leak incident.

On August 26 his blog temporarily became inaccessible (it can now be viewed), and his Twitter account name abruptly changed, among other strange developments. He explained that it had been hijacked, although some suggested that he staged this himself to escape scrutiny over the two incidents above.

## Conflict with the new 2ch administrator, Jim: February 2014 onward

The 2channel personal-information leak destroyed confidence in “●,” a source of revenue for the site.[^tn-hiro-maru]

The person affected was Jim Watkins, president of Race Queen, which operated 2channel and received revenue from “●.” He is referred to below as Jim.

Jim had long been involved with 2channel as administrator of the Pink boards. After Hiroyuki dissolved Packet Monster and transferred 2channel to Race Queen—a transfer Hiroyuki subsequently denied—Jim took over operation of the entire service and received the “●” revenue.

With that source of income gone and 2ch's continued existence potentially at risk, Jim was furious. On February 19, 2014, he dismissed the former operating team, whom the source blames for the information leak, along with Hiroyuki, who remained involved behind the scenes.

On that same day, Hotto Link, which had held an exclusive commercial-use agreement since October 2012 for “big data” consisting of information posted on 2channel, sold 80,000 of its own shares. J-CAST News reported suspicions of insider trading because of the timing. The report also prompted accusations that Hiroyuki might have passed personal information to Hotto Link. Hotto Link denied the report and issued a statement saying it would consider litigation depending on subsequent developments.

## Controversy over the launch of 2ch.sc: April 2014 onward

After being driven out of 2channel, Hiroyuki created **2ch.sc** on April 11, 2014. In effect a mirror of 2channel, it automatically crawled and reproduced posts from 2ch.net, going so far as to replace the wording “Reproduction Prohibited” in replies with “＼(^o^)／.”

Jim and the new 2channel operators, along with Kenmō and NanJ users who supported the ban on reproduction, strongly objected, triggering another major controversy. For details, see 2ch.sc and the “fake 2ch” controversy. Matters gradually settled down. Although 2channel took steps against crawling, it could not prevent it completely, and 2ch.sc continues to exist in the article's present.

During this controversy, Hiroyuki also got married, as noted above, and wrestled sumo at the Grand Sumo Chōkaigi tournament. As typically Hiroyuki as ever, one might say…

## The dispute with Jim and becoming 4chan administrator: late 2014–2015

After Hiroyuki was expelled from 2channel, he and Jim each claimed rights to it, but months passed without either side budging. Meanwhile, Hiroyuki traveled to North Korea and reportedly encountered a refusal of his return home, drank with ZUN at Niconico Chōkaigi 2015, and appeared on *TV Tackle*.[^tn-hiro-korea]

Then, in September 2015, he became the new administrator of the overseas anonymous imageboard **4chan**. Former administrator moot commented that Hiroyuki was “the grandfather of 4chan.” This is said to be because 4chan took inspiration from Futaba Channel, which had itself originated as an offshoot of 2channel.

After becoming its new operator, Hiroyuki said in a 4chan Q&A session, “I have sued Jim, the current 2channel administrator,” and “The trial begins in November.” The dispute thus appeared headed to court in November 2015, but Jim's side requested a postponement, which was granted; it seemed likely to be some time away.
