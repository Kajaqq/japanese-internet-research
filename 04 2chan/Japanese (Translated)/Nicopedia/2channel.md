# 5channel

**【About 5ch】 5channel is Japan's largest anonymous bulletin board site. 【Let's all discuss】**

## 1: Overview

**2006/12/12(Tue) 10:29:01 ID:K+Mnｆ/kx0**

The previous thread fell off, so I'm making a new one.

5channel is Japan's largest-scale anonymous bulletin board site. Commonly known as "5chan" or "5ch". It was created in 1999 as "2channel" by Hiroyuki Nishimura, commonly known as Hiroyuki. 

From its inception to the present day, extremely dense information exchanges on every conceivable field take place here. Its catchphrase is: *"A massive cluster of bulletin boards broadly covering everything from 'Hacking' to 'Night Snacks'."* 

> **Cultural Note:** "Night snacks" (夜のおかず - *yoru no okazu*) is classic Japanese internet slang for pornography or masturbation material.

Because it is anonymous, it features everything from casual posts without social pleasantries to genuine, unfiltered opinions without any pretense. It has been featured by the mass media as the most influential site in Japan's internet society. It can be said to be the authentic pioneer of a massive, uniquely Japanese internet community.

The culture and slang that originated and developed from 5channel amount to an enormous number, and they continue to grow today. The minimum rule proposed by the admin of 5channel is *"Let's stop causing trouble for others."* However, because anyone can easily use the site, the fact that this rule isn't always followed has become a recurring problem.

It also shares a vast number of similarities with Niconico Douga.

## 2: Relationship with Niconico Douga

**2006/12/12(Tue) 10:29:10 ID:K+Mnｆ/kx0**

Niconico Douga was once developed and operated by Niwango, Inc. Actually, the director of this company was Hiroyuki himself, the creator of 2channel.

When Niconico Douga (Beta) was launched, it was first introduced on 2channel and Hiroyuki's blog. In other words, 2channel residents were used as beta testers. As a result, 2channel residents made up almost all of the early users of Niconico Douga, using it effectively as a sister site.

Furthermore, it has strong ties not just with Niconico Douga, but with Niconico Pedia (*Niconico Daihyakka*) as well. At the time of its establishment, Niconico Pedia was operated by *Mirai Kensaku Brazil* (Future Search Brazil), which was also the advertising agency for 2channel. The director of this company was also Hiroyuki. This is why the Niconico Pedia message boards share so many functional and cultural similarities with 5channel's message boards.

Even the event that could be called the origin of the *Niconico Chokaigi* (Niconico Super Conference) was once held on 2ch.

To sum it up, the relationship between Niconico Douga and 2channel is like that of brothers, sisters, or close relatives.

## 3: 5channel Terminology & Copypastas

**2006/12/12(Tue) 10:29:10 ID:K+Mnｆ/kx0**

An extremely large number of internet terms originated from 2channel and are now used all over the internet. In addition to terminology, ASCII Art (AA), copypastas, and kaomoji (emoticons) originating from 5channel are incredibly popular. Furthermore, words like "Riaju" and "wwww" have a massive number of users even outside internet circles.

> **Cultural Note:** 
>
> * **Riaju (リア充):** Someone who is fulfilled (*ju*) in their real life (*ria*). The English equivalent is a "normie". 
> * **wwww:** The Japanese equivalent of "lol", deriving from *warau* (to laugh). A string of w's looks like grass growing, which later birthed the modern slang "Kusa" (草 - grass).

## 5: The Transfer of 2channel

**2006/12/12(Tue) 10:30:46 ID:K+Mnｆ/kx0**

2channel became Japan's largest electronic bulletin board site, but on January 2, 2009, Hiroyuki transferred 2channel to a Singaporean company, "PACKET MONSTER INC." Along with this, the Whois information for `2ch.net` was also changed to "PACKET MONSTER INC."

## 6: From 2channel to 5channel

**2006/12/12(Tue) 10:31:08 ID:K+Mnｆ/kx0**

In August 2013, an incident occurred where the personal information of users of the paid service "5channel Viewer" (commonly known as **●**) was leaked.

> **Cultural Note:** **●** (Maru) was a premium subscription on 2ch that allowed users to bypass IP bans, read old archived threads, and hide their IP addresses. Its database breach was a massive scandal.

Jim Watkins, the administrator of ●, and the 2channel administration clashed over how to handle the aftermath. On February 19, 2014, Watkins expelled the 2channel administration and became the new admin himself. Following this, on April 11, Hiroyuki established `2ch.sc` (a clone site). As a result, 2channel and Hiroyuki became bitter adversaries.

According to Watkins' side, they suffered various forms of harassment from Hiroyuki and announced that safe and comfortable operation would be difficult under the "2channel" brand. Consequently, they changed the site name to **5channel** and the domain to `5ch.net`.

## 9: Restrictions (Kisei)

**2006/12/12(Tue) 10:32:22 ID:K+Mnｆ/kx0**

To prevent massive spamming by bots, host restrictions (blanket IP bans) are often implemented. However, this causes even completely innocent users who have nothing to do with trolling to be restricted and entirely unable to post. 

In 2008, dynamic IP users of asahi-net were permanently banned. SoftBank BB was fully restricted for months in 2009, and major hosts like KDDI and OCN have faced posting bans since 2010. Situations have even occurred where "Jippura"—a board where people are supposed to be able to post even during a ban—ends up restricted as well.

> **Cultural Note:** "Jippura" (じっぷら) is the nickname for the News & Weather Live++ board. Historically, it was used as a sanctuary or refugee camp for users caught in the crossfire of massive IP bans.

## 11: Related Items (Slang & Lore)

**2006/12/12(Tue) 10:33:19 ID:K+Mnｆ/kx0**

* **1001:** 2ch threads have a strict 1000-post limit. Post 1001 is a system message stating the thread is full.
* **Aboon (あぼーん):** The term used to replace text in deleted posts. It mimics an explosion or "poof" sound effect.
* **Copypasta Blogs (Matome Sites):** Blogs that scrape and curate 2ch threads, highly controversial among users.
* **Bathroom Graffiti:** A derogatory, self-deprecating nickname for 2ch itself.
* **Kisaragi Station:** A legendary 2ch urban legend about a haunted, non-existent train station.
* **Train Man (Densha Otoko):** A famous true (?) story of an otaku saving a woman on a train and getting dating advice from a 2ch thread. It was adapted into a hit movie and TV drama.

## 12: 5channel Threads Documented on Niconico Pedia

**2006/12/12(Tue) 10:34:23 ID:K+Mnｆ/kx0**

*A selection of legendary 5ch threads that became so famous they have their own Niconico articles:*

* **VOCALOID Main Thread:** (The birthplace of much of the early Vocaloid fandom discourse and producer culture!)
* **Crappy Game of the Year (Kusoge of the Year):** An annual, highly formalized review thread roasting the worst video games.
* **"Hey, anyone who can compose music, come here a sec":** A famous VIP board thread that birthed many viral internet songs.
* **Sharekowa:** Short for "Stories so scary they aren't a joke," a legendary repository for horror board stories.
* **Demon Lord: "Become mine, Hero!" / Hero: "I refuse!":** The original roleplay thread that spawned the official light novel and anime *Maoyuu Maou Yuusha*.
* **"I got tricked and sent to the Dam board again":** A classic bait-and-switch link prank, similar to Rickrolling, redirecting users to the obscure Civil Engineering board.
* **My wife's cooking tastes terrible:** A highly dramatic, famous series of threads on the Marriage board detailing spouses dealing with dangerously bad cooking.
