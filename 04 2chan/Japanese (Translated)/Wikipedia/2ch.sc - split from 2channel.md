# 2channel (2ch.sc)

**2channel** (*Nichanneru*) is an electronic message board (anonymous message board) established in April 2014 by **[[Hiroyuki Nishimura|Hiroyuki Nishimura]]**, with the domain name **2ch.sc**.[^1] It is called “2channel” or “2chan.”

This article generally calls it “2ch.sc.”

## Overview

The boards and interface of 2ch.sc are almost identical to those of [[2channel Hub|2channel (2ch.net)]], now [[5channel]], referred to below as “5ch.net.” Furthermore, it regularly crawls 5ch.net and copies data such as posts unchanged. For this reason, some describe 2ch.sc as a copy site of 5ch.net.[^2][^3]

Whereas 5ch.net had decided by March 2014 to prohibit reproduction of posts by affiliate-advertising blogs and similar sites, hereafter “affiliate blogs,” 2ch.sc has permitted reproduction of its contents since its launch.[^4][^5] Replies copied from 5ch.net have “.net” appended to their IDs.

## History

For details, see History of 2channel and 5channel § Dispute over administrative authority.

See also [[Hiroyuki Nishimura|Hiroyuki Nishimura]].

## Relationship with 5ch.net

5ch.net has stated that it does not exchange information with 2ch.sc.

On April 12, 2014, reports suggested that data from 5ch.net might be being copied using a crawler originating from Sakura Internet, which managed 2ch.sc's servers. Several 5ch.net servers went down that day.[^5][^6]

In response, 5ch.net reportedly blocked all crawlers from 2ch.sc and installed a program displaying “Reproduction prohibited” instead, temporarily filling 2ch.sc with that message. Subsequent observation showed 5ch.net posts again appearing unchanged on 2ch.sc. However, J-CAST News reported that 2ch.sc had not commented on whether this was being done by crawler or manually, leaving the details unknown.[^5]

These specifications result in the following display behavior:

- Posting directly to 2ch.sc causes reply numbers to diverge from those on 5ch.net.
- On 2ch.sc, replies copied from 5ch.net have “.net” appended to their IDs.
