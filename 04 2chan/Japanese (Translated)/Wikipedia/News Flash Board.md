# News Boards — 2channel and 5channel

[[2channel Hub|2channel]] > List of 2channel boards > **News (2channel and 5channel category)**

This article lists the **News-category** [[2channel Hub|2channel (2ch.net)]], [[2ch.sc - split from 2channel|2channel (2ch.sc)]], and [[5channel]] message boards.

The anonymous message-board services 2channel and 5channel have News as one of their categories. It groups boards concerning various kinds of news, including News Flash Plus, News Flash, and Entertainment/Sports News Flash Plus. All Plus boards operate a reporter system: only reporters with cap codes may create threads. This is called a fully reporter-controlled system; see reporter cap codes for details.[^tn-news-reporter]

Breaking Headlines is a message board displaying threads created on news-related boards in newest-first order. Posting is not possible, so strictly speaking it is not a board.

### News Flash board

News Flash handles fast-breaking news. Common abbreviations are **ν速** (*Nyūsoku*) and **N即**. Its users are called **News Flash residents** (*Nyūsoku-min*).

Existing since 2channel's early days, it is “a message board for discussing breaking news.” It is also known as the birthplace of many memes, and the famous Tashiro Matsuri centered on it. It was the parent board from which many others emerged, including Live Commentary Channel and News Flash VIP.

In its early and middle periods, many regulars also participated in specialist boards. Because posting was far more frequent here than on those boards, highly sophisticated reply battles flourished, particularly late on weekday nights, among users called the “late-night group” or “professionals.” During the September 11 attacks, posters identified Osama bin Laden as the mastermind soon afterward. There were also numerous posts apparently by people connected with well-known incidents, according to the source.

Until the mid-2000s, often called News Flash's golden age, Internet-related legislation had barely developed, in the source's account. Many threads concerned child sexual abuse material and illegal downloading. Users also tried to trick others into following links to browser-crashing pages and graphic images. The article consequently credits well-intentioned users here with the Internet's first practice of “checking link destinations.” Users also created a site called Chō Uploader (“Super Uploader”), but it became a lawless area for exchanging illegal files from the outset and soon closed. At the time, the concepts of smartphones and even social-networking services were scarcely present. Information from across the Japanese Internet accumulated around 2channel, so earthquakes and major breaking news often brought concentrated traffic to News Flash and caused server outages. Particularly after earthquakes, apparently nearby users would immediately create threads, earning it the occasional description “the world's fastest earthquake news.”[^tn-news-sourceclaims]

In 2004, users called on other boards to rise against the administrators and FOX ★ in what they called a “holy war.” The administrators subsequently used News Flash as a testing ground for various experiments. After [[News Flash Board VIP|News Flash (VIP)]] opened, its post count temporarily fell below 2channel's top ten. It began recovering around 2009; in 2011 daily posting reached record levels and it increasingly ranked first.

However, from January 2012, resentment toward 2channel roundup sites prompted large numbers of users to migrate to News Flash (Kenmō). It fell not merely outside the top ten but below one hundred, reaching around 120th–130th in February that year.

Anti-script measures came to require BE and a Ninpōchō level of at least 3 even to post. Although the population is small, the board functions.

#### Display of anonymous names

Until 2007 the default was “We Interrupt This Program, but I Am Anonymous.” The name field then used a random anonymous name plus the prefecture of posting, such as “Convenience Store (Tokyo).” From March 29, 2008, “prefecture” was replaced with “specialty product of the prefecture of posting,” for example “Convenience Store (monja),” where the previous “Tokyo” became “monja.” Whether in response to criticism or not, it returned to the previous prefecture display almost a month later, on April 26, 2008.[^1] The specialty-product change was also introduced on other boards displaying the prefecture of posting, including the Earthquake board. The Earthquake board returned to the original display earlier than News Flash. The displayed specialty did not necessarily correspond to the user's actual prefecture; exceptions included:

- (Alabama) — Unsupported ISPs such as DION, now au one net.
- (dion army) — Posts from au one net.
- (Connecticut) — Posts from NTT DoCoMo mobile phones.
- (catv?) — Unidentifiable ISPs.

These are examples.

### News Flash Plus board

News Flash Plus, hereafter **Nyūsoku Plus**, handles general news across a wide range, from society, politics, international affairs, and economics to Internet topics and local news. Established on October 17, 2001, it differs from News Flash in being fully reporter-controlled: only “reporters” with cap codes may create threads. Abbreviations include “Nyūsoku+,” **ν速+**, **N速+**, and simply **+**.

Threads appear quickly after news is reported by the mass media, but in principle cannot be created without a mass-media source. It need not be a source published on a media outlet's website: photographing or scanning the media item and uploading the image is also accepted.

In the source's present, a **four-day rule** applies: once four days, or 96 hours, have passed since the first thread was created, no further continuation thread may be created.

Threads about South Korea, North Korea, and China were said to be restricted because dedicated boards such as East Asia News Plus exist. Over time those restrictions have become nominal, and the current situation is effectively “anything goes.”

### News Discussion board

A board for discussing news. Originally, once a topic on boards such as [[#News Flash Plus board|News Flash Plus]] reached its third thread or beyond, discussion was supposed to move here. In practice this was ignored, and many threads here are created for in-depth discussion independently of News Flash Plus.

### News Live Commentary Plus board

Its main uses are live commentary on news programs and continuation of News Flash Plus topics from thread ★3 onward. It is abbreviated “Jippura.” Unusually among news-category boards, live commentary is permitted. Its official name is **News & Weather Live Commentary Plus Plus @ 2ch**.

Under its local rules, in principle only cap-code holders can create threads. However, because it is “under experimentation,” people without ★ cap codes sometimes manage to create threads by chance. ★ holders are expected to “act like ★ holders.” Alteration, fabrication, ideological agendas, and personal grudges are strictly prohibited.

It originally belonged to both News and Live Commentary Channel, but was removed from the latter in April 2014.

### Entertainment/Sports News Flash Plus board

**Entertainment, Music, and Sports News Flash Plus**, hereafter **Geispo Plus**, is a fully reporter-controlled board for general entertainment news. It covers everything from entertainment, music, and sports to Internet topics, local sports, and even festivals.

#### Celebrity reporters

A “celebrity reporter” system allows self-described celebrities connected with entertainment and sports to become reporters and create threads themselves. Principal reporters include:
- Mai Fujiko — a gravure idol calling herself an “ugly idol.”
- Yūichirō Nagashima ☆ Jien-otsu — cosplay kickboxer.
- Pyocotan — “poop-type” manga artist.
- Yumi Nanao — former gravure idol and member of KEIRIN promotional girl group LOVE9.
- Haruna Anno — gamer idol.
- Takayasu Katō — president and representative director of Jaleco Co., Ltd.
- Masashi Tashiro — former entertainer.

#### Abiru rule

During the scandal sparked by Yū Abiru's appearance on Coming Doubt, multiple media outlets published successive articles, causing a proliferation of Abiru threads on Geispo Plus, reportedly reaching an “effective ★60.” In 2005 FOX ★, a 2ch administrator cap-code holder, introduced a provisional “Abiru rule”: only one Abiru thread could be created; its post had to direct users to liveplus; and no next thread could be created until the previous one had fallen into the dat archive.

### East Asia News Plus board

**East Asia News Plus** handles news about East Asia, including China, North Korea, and South Korea. One of the News Flash Plus family, it previously belonged to the News category. Only reporters with cap codes, whose posting names end in ★, may create threads; see cap codes for details. Its official name is **East Asia News Flash Plus**, abbreviated **Tōa Plus**.

Users call South Korea, China, and North Korea “Specific Asia”. News covers those countries—the Republic of Korea, People's Republic of China, and Democratic People's Republic of Korea—and the wider East Asian region. News Flash Plus has a division of responsibility under which it handles only major news about those three countries. Stories principally concern East Asian countries, ranging beyond recent incidents to politics, economics, history, culture, entertainment, and international affairs. News about the Republic of China (Taiwan), Russia, and Southeast Asian countries is also occasionally covered.[^tn-news-asia]

Before the board was established, these stories were handled on News Flash Plus. As North Korea's abductions of Japanese citizens and Chongryon's involvement became increasingly clear, according to the source, story numbers grew and thread discussions often became heated; apparently this led to the dedicated board's creation.

In response to South and North Korean anti-Japanese actions and statements, the article describes it as a base for Japanese anti-Korean, anti-North-Korean, or racist speech. Its default anonymous name places the “Shobōn” face representing Japan between “Nidā” and “Shinā,” representing Korea and China, expressing a worldview of “Japanese people oppressed by China and Korea.”

### Lively News Plus board

Officially “Lively News Plus @ 5ch.” It uses a reporter system—only applicants passing screening during occasional recruitment rounds may create threads—and serves the following two purposes:

- A place for demoting and retraining reporters from other reporter-controlled boards, especially News Flash Plus, whose conduct is problematic but does not warrant dismissal.
- A place where new reporters can practice for a while and become accustomed to the role.

An instructor system was introduced on May 17, 2007, to train newcomers. Instructors have φφ ★ cap codes, denoting assistant bureau chief, and guide new reporters. When a newcomer graduates from this board, an instructor receiving thanks from a majority of graduates may serve again. Those serving five terms receive a φφφ ★ bureau-chief cap code. The board is fully reporter-controlled.

#### Circumstances of its creation

- On November 2, 2004, the group responsible released video of a young Japanese man being killed in Iraq, sharply increasing 2ch traffic from people seeking information. See Killing of a Japanese Young Man in Iraq.
- Late on November 4, rumors circulate of the death of Palestinian Chairman Arafat, who actually dies on the 11th. Another surge in 2ch load is anticipated and countermeasures begin to be considered.
- At that time, action is being considered against several News Flash Plus reporters creating unreasonably large numbers of threads. With load already likely to rise, dealing with them is urgent.
- However, they have fans, and dismissing them seems harsh. They are therefore demoted to the “second string,” made reporters on a dedicated second-string board, and allowed to create as many threads as they like there.
- By coincidence, all the demoted reporters and those volunteering to join them have animal names in their handles. Someone observes that it will be like “Animal News Plus,” leading to the name “Energetic Kids' Animal News Plus @ 2ch,” board ID wildplus. The name thus means a board where animals write news, rather than one for news about animals.
- November 5, 2004 — The board is founded on live14, which happens to retain a reporter database from earlier board moves.
- It subsequently moves live26 → news22.

Unlike most earlier boards split off from News Flash to segregate threads or users, this unusual board is intended to segregate the reporters, who would ordinarily belong to the operational side.

It also becomes a training ground: new reporters are to practice here for a while, with their activity assessed before promotion to the first string.

### Painful News Plus board

Painful News Plus, hereafter **Itai Plus**, is a fully reporter-controlled board for foolish or so-called DQN news.[^tn-news-itai]

Its local rules explicitly assign “that sort of news” and “pathetic news” here rather than to News Flash Plus. Restrictions on reporters are very loose: any thread is acceptable if it is “painful news”; sources may be somewhat old because the board name does not say “breaking”; and blogs may be sources. It was established on January 18, 2004, on a whim of 2channel administrator [[Hiroyuki Nishimura]]. The default anonymous name comes from a stock phrase in bank-transfer fraud, then called “It's me, it's me” fraud, prevalent when the board was created.

### Moe News Plus board

Moe News Plus is a fully reporter-controlled news board for otaku-related topics, which often span a broad range. It may also handle connected incidents and phenomena involving both two-dimensional fictional material and three-dimensional real-world subjects.

Unusually for the News category, it has a mascot character, created shortly after the board's birth and accepted by its users. Although rarely seen as of 2007, it can be found in the randomly displayed banners used on Moe News Plus. It was used extensively as the board mascot in the third All-Boards Popularity Tournament in 2008; the board was eliminated in the first main-round contest. An animated GIF in the banner explains the founding guidelines and ideals followed from April 2005 to the end of 2006, before the operating policy was revised.

#### History

- April 13, 2005 — Opens.

- March 2007 — The scope of permitted information is redefined, strengthening its character as a comprehensive board for otaku-related topics.

### My News board

Handles personal news, soliloquies, and similar material. Its official name is “My News Flash @ 2ch.”

An anything-goes board, its posts cover many genres, levels, and viewpoints: genuinely personal matters, passing impressions, relatively sensible opinions, material that would offend rigid-minded readers, and things an administrator would immediately delete on boards outside 2ch.

#### History

- December 8, 2001 — As 2ch's growing population makes the proliferation of junk and worthless threads on News Flash conspicuous, the board is established for segregation, asking users to post private rather than public news here. Additional News Flash boards have numbers 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6; because 6 already exists, this one is named “News Flash 7.” Its nickname “Seven” comes from this.

- 2002, February 8 — The previous subtitle “My News Flash” becomes its official name.

### News Nostalgia board

News Nostalgia discusses previously reported news rather than current developments. Threads concern past events that attracted public attention, including entertainment and political news, but famous criminal cases are particularly common.

### Stupid News board

A board for trivial news, effectively transformed into a board for baiting, jokes, and general chat. Its official name is “Stupid News @ 2ch.”

Soon after its creation, users ask how it differs from My News Flash. The administrators explain that My News Flash is for freely talking about what one wants without expecting replies, while Stupid News is for posting freely, including general chat.

#### History

The board is abruptly created around 22:24 on August 8, 2002. According to “Ima Iru Hito” (“Person Here Now”), who sets it up, News Flash has quite a few silly threads, but continually deleting them would be harsh, so this is provided as somewhere to post stupid news freely.
- August 8, 2002 — Founded on the tmp server.

- 2004, December 29 — Banner logo changes to a JavaScript system displaying five new designs.
- 2009, July 7 — Banner logo changes to 24 new designs.

> [!info] Cultural context and translator's notes
> This source covers the whole News category, despite this vault file's narrower filename. “Residents” are regular users; “migration” describes users moving between boards. Board histories combine passages with different reference dates, including 2007, 2012, and the category count as of May 2023. They have not been rewritten as a description of 2026. Thread ★3 or ★60 means the third or sixtieth continuation thread on a topic. Cap-code markers such as ★ and φφφ are retained as identifying symbols. “Dat” refers to thread data and, in phrases such as “falling into dat,” a thread leaving the active list. The original uses strongly worded descriptions of groups and makes historical claims without citations in several places; those are source characterizations, not independent findings of this translation. Empty board sections, source navigation links, and the two source references remain present.

[^tn-news-reporter]: “Reporter” (記者) is a role authorized to create news threads on these boards, not necessarily a professional journalist. “Fully reporter-controlled” restricts thread creation; the description does not say ordinary users cannot reply. Cap codes authenticate privileged posting names, shown here with ★.
[^tn-news-sourceclaims]: The claims that Internet legislation had “barely developed,” that the board first checked links, and that it offered the “world's fastest earthquake news” are the source's broad historical assertions. Their wording and attribution are preserved without turning them into verified historical conclusions.
[^tn-news-asia]: “Specific Asia” (特定アジア) is the term the source attributes to these users for South Korea, China, and North Korea. The later paragraph explicitly discusses anti-Korean and racist speech. These names and caricatures are reported as part of that board culture rather than used as neutral descriptions of national groups.
[^tn-news-itai]: 痛い (*itai*) literally means “painful”; here it characterizes cringe-inducing, foolish, or embarrassing news. DQN is the source's derogatory slang label, retained because replacing it with an English demographic category would distort its usage.
