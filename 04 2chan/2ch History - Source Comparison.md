# 2ch History: Do the Sources Agree?

A comparison of the vault's notes on 2channel history.
The sources agree on the broad story but disagree on a number of dates and causes, and some of the disagreements come from sources copying each other.
## What all sources agree on

- Ayashii World's closure (Sept 3, 1998), confirmed by the Ayashii pages themselves.
- Amezou's thread-floating board (Sept 6, 1998) absorbed Ayashii's refugees.
- Hiroyuki founded 2channel in 1999 as Amezou's "second channel". History of Ayashii World - Part 1 also records May 30, 1999.
- Futaba Channel was founded on Aug 30, 2001 during a 2ch crisis.
- 4chan was founded on Oct 1, 2003, built on Futaba's code.
- Nishitetsu bus hijacking, May 2000, as 2ch's breakthrough.
- Hiroyuki transferred 2ch to Packet Monster in January 2009.
- The personal-information leak happened in August 2013.
- Jim Watkins took control on Feb 19, 2014.
- 2ch became 5channel on Oct 1, 2017.
- Hiroyuki became 4chan's admin in September 2015.
