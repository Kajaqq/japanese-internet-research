## Japan: Ayashii World, Amezo and 2channel (1996–1999)

# Ayashii World
## 21.08.1996  

A man named *Masayuki Shiba* opens a **Registration-less Bulletin Board** called **Ayashii World** (あやしいワールド / Strange World), a WWW continuation of a NiftyServe *Home Party BBS* service he opened in the summer/autumn of 1995 to distribute a controversial video game. 
The new Strange World runs on proprietary anonymous BB code by venture webhost *Digital Eden*, and Shiba adheres his site to the then dominant **Japanese Underground Internet Culture**.
Underground or "UG" sites were the hangouts of an anarchic scene of internet punks spread across USENET and Grassroots BBS where content control was essentially inexistent: Warez, spam, trolling, defamation and even (then legal) CSEM are posted without second thought, mostly free of the political baggage of their [western Hacker brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacker_culture#Ethics_and_principles). 
Eden closes and SW hops through several free Bulletin Board services before settling on a fork of CGI Rescue's Perl script [SimpleBBS (*簡易ＢＢＳ*)](https://web.archive.org/web/19981206135748/http://www.ask.ne.jp/%7Erescue/support/arrange.html). By 1997, Strange World was one of the dominant sites in the Underground scene, receiving attention from Hacking magazines, [protagonising a few controversies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_child_murders) and being linked to [a hacking attack that made the news](https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm23147061). 
An early troll group named *Guess*,(Gesu?) involved in [several notable crapflood attacks](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%BE%B2%E6%B0%B4%E7%9C%81%E3%82%AA%E3%82%A6%E3%83%A0%E3%82%BD%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B0%E4%BA%8B%E4%BB%B6), had its start in SW. 

By 1998 the websight was being cited by mainstream magazines as the most prominent Underground network, Shiba had built a personality cult and [similar BBs open under the "あやしいわーるど＠" prefix](https://web.archive.org/web/19990210074826/http://www.geocities.com/Paris/Cafe/7893/). At this point, users begin to experiment with the limits of single thread textboard format and develop the first [Kaomoji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaomoji) derived copy & paste memes like the [Gikohahaha Cat `(,,ﾟДﾟ)`](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%82%AE%E3%82%B3%E7%8C%AB), marking the starting point of a distinct **Anonymous Internet Culture**. Around March 1998, *Guess* leading figure *Alice Liddell* was doxed and humilliated as backlash for having released a SimpleBBS spamscript called PerlDUKE. Alice swore *revenge* and harassed Shiba until he shut Strange World down in September 3, 1998.[^7]

# Amezou

## 1997, August 5 
A High school teacher and tech hobbyist opens the first (large) [Bulletin Board link portal](https://web.archive.org/web/20200125032210/http://history.amebbs.com/1997/1997_0805_n002main-ame.htm) called **Amezo's Links** (あめぞうリンク / *Amezou Rinku*) (n.b. link date is wrong and likely dates August 1998). Under the motto ["A silent protest against people who say information on the Internet is boring"](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%81%82%E3%82%81%E3%81%9E%E3%81%86) 

Amezo aims to facilitate word of mouth news sharing and integrates the Strange World in its rotation. The site achieves modest popularity and by March 6 1998 opens a SimpleBBS forum called *Breaking News*. As a link portal, it becomes the focal point of its word of mouth process and the Japanese netizen's first choice for suggestions [inside and outside the UG scene](https://web.archive.org/web/20140812203741/https://internet.watch.impress.co.jp/www/wtoday/backno/981028/).

## 1998, September 6 
A few days after Alice chased Shiba off the net, Amezo is struck by a [flood of SW refugees](https://web.archive.org/web/20070907114556/https://history.amebbs.com/1998/199809061413_sokuho.htm) and responds by switching to a recently released Perl script called [*resBBS*](https://web.archive.org/web/19981205221858/http://www2q.meshnet.or.jp/%7Eterra/cgi/), a threaded BB. By January they had transformed it into the first **Floating Thread Bulletin Board** (rechristened **Textboard** in the west) called *amezo.cgi*, where new posts **bump** threads to the top. Amezo continues opening Bulletin Boards, his "kitchen sink" approach to forums combined with the disarray of the SW community [makes the site mainstream](https://web.archive.org/web/20040704041645/http://homepage3.nifty.com/ttakeda/special/), outgrowing the Underground scene and surpassing Yahoo JP as Japan's largest Bulletin Board. This leads to [non-stop shitposting, trolling, humoristic namefield impersonations, real life meet ups and severe site-loading problems](https://web.archive.org/web/20160304043940/http://history.amebbs.com/).

# 2channel
##  1999, May 10 
- Entrepreneur and Amezo regular *Hiroyuki Nishimura* opens [*Channel 2*](https://github.com/bibanon/bibanon/wiki/2ch-History), more popularly known as **2channel** (2ちゃんねる / *Ni Channeru*). Claiming pedigree from Amezo as the "first channel", 2ch was a perl clone coded derived from amezo.cgi, the also unnamed *read.cgi*. It later receives several backend changes such as the feature of opting-out of bumping threads through **sage**, letting staff members via **capcodes** and the implementation of a third-party script for unique password protected names called **tripcodes**. The Amezo userbase sees 2ch's announcement as their thunder being stolen and Hiroyuki gets shitposted and impersonated non-stop, but despite this, Amezo and Hiroyuki were always on good terms.

## 1999, June 19 
Amezo starts to collapse under the weight of its own success. The amezo.cgi script cannot take their mainstream popularity and there's simply too many boards. Posting becomes restricted, boards fail to load. To make matter worse, the *whistleblower* (callout and gossip) board opened a few months prior was reported to their webhost over one or several a discrimination scandals and [forced to close](https://web.archive.org/web/20060430232709/http://resistance333.web.fc2.com/1-1.htm), followed by other hosts dropping the site due to ToS violations. In August an html exploit wreaks havoc and hackers vandalize entire boards. By September, Amezo appears to lose his account and disappears. What's left of the site shuts down by October or November. With all the competition gone, 2ch becomes the de facto center of Japan's internet culture. Aided by a couple [viral controversies](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9D%B1%E8%8A%9D%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AC%E3%83%BC%E3%83%9E%E3%83%BC%E4%BA%8B%E4%BB%B6#%E4%BA%8B%E4%BB%B6%E3%81%AE%E5%BD%B1%E9%9F%BF) (and a growing rivalry with the remnants of the Amezo and Strange World networks after its discovered or claimed that Hiroyuki had a hand in Amezo's collapse, which inspires 2ch's *tantsubo* (spittoon) logo), by the end of 1999, 2channel had become Japan's largest news discussion website.


# Something Awful and ADTRW (1999–2001)

## 1999, November 16 
 Amateur writer *Lowtax* starts a comedy blog called **Something Awful**. It explodes in popularity, prompting the creation of a *vBulletin* forum by 2000. [The forum overtakes the blog and becomes the largest gathering of content creators on the western internet.](https://github.com/bibanon/bibanon/wiki/Something-Awful)
 
## 2001, May 14 
Tired of seeing it flood GBS, Lowtax creates an Anime subforum titled [*Anime Death Tentacle Rape Whorehouse*](https://web.archive.org/web/20010607011103/https://forums.somethingawful.com/forumdisplay.php?forumid=27) (later shortened to **ADTRW**). The forum speeds up next year, creating an involved community and [coining the ZOMG meme.](https://web.archive.org/web/20030603165407/http://www.sylphex.com/ZOMG_FAQ.txt) Lowtax's (and his inner circle's) disposition towards ADTRW was never very high and only got lower with time, leading to the *othering* of board regulars and periodic purges over their sense of humour.[^8]

# Futaba Channel and the first imageboards (2001–2002)

##  2001, August 30 
[2channel is in danger of dying from a very similar problem to the one that killed Amezo.](https://github.com/bibanon/bibanon/wiki/2ch-History#unix-and-2chan-and-the-august-2001-crisis) 
While a C++ rewrite of the codebase was in the works, users created a bunker 2chan.net, officially named *Channel Two Leaves*, popularly known as **Futaba Channel**.

## 2002, March 12 
Futaba implements a custom fork of [*GazouBBS*,](https://web.archive.org/web/20011225220533/http://php.s3.to:80/bbs/bbs3.php) a Floating Thread BB script made on PHP known by westerners as an **Imageboard**, where a thread's opening post may attach a file. The spartanly named [***Futaba script***](https://web.archive.org/web/20030129152139/https://www.2chan.net/script/) improves Gazou by limiting the number of threads instead of posts, having old threads **pruned** as new ones are made. In the coming years, imageboards become wildly popular among *Anime Otaku* and even in the English speaking internet.[^9]

# Studio ADTRW, Raspberry Heaven and world2ch (2002–2003)

## 2002, November 6 

ADTRW regulars *\[NPH\]*, *Inpheaux*, *mdl* and *Uziga* form **Studio ADTRW**, fangroup dedicated to anime fansubbing and manga scanlating. On November 6 they open an official website, [adtrw.org](https://web.archive.org/web/20021208061739/http://www.adtrw.org/), with a forum, wallpapers page, [videogame mods](https://web.archive.org/web/20030207122111/http://mods.adtrw.org/index.php?p=faq), IRC `#adtrw` on irc.aniverse.com (later irc.mircx.com, irc.pyoko.org, and irc.synirc.org), torrent releases and personal blogs. Studio ADTRW absorbs a handful dying fangroups, developing a small network of friends that don't completely overlap with Something Awful.[^10]

## 2002, November 16 
Studio ADTRW member and manager at (also ADTRW's) SADCHUB DC++ hub *Hannibal* [^10] and acquaintance *IchibanMuffin* [create a spin off DC++ hub](https://web.archive.org/web/20021208112620/http://dc.obso.net/~hannibal/raspberry/) called **Raspberry Heaven** (After the [ending of *Azumanga Daioh*).](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kWsxg3uNMt4) after SADCHUB's host *obso* bans anime porn. In May, hannibal moves to his own domain at [raspberryheaven.net](https://web.archive.org/web/20030625201305/http://www.raspberryheaven.net/). By the end of 2003, the hub had accrued over 100 active members and the IRC-DC++ bridged channel `#raspberryheaven` (on irc.mircx.com [^11] and later irc.pyoko.org) imposes itself as ADTRW's everyday lounge, with forum top posters doubling as RH regulars. Raspberry Heaven starts out an anime & manga sharing hub doubling as one of Studio ADTRW's release venues, but it becomes apparent most regulars dabbled in web design and programming.[^12]

## 2003 
The 2chan.net appreciation thread is created on ADTRW (ref IOU), the collision point between the eastern and western Internet cultures. 2chan's rapid-fire image sharing becomes wildly popular on ADTRW and RH, making it one of the fastest threads on the forum and [spawning fan videos](https://eye.swfchan.com/flash.asp?id=4381&n=SA_loves_2chan.swf).
## 2003, March 3 
The first English language textboard is created: [**world2ch**](https://github.com/bibanon/bibanon/wiki/world2ch), made by *RIR6*, a 16 years old 2channel poster who grabbed an "english friendly" fork of 2ch called [eng2ch](https://web.archive.org/web/20190809130015/http://eng2ch.s22.xrea.com/) after the original project folded. To his surprise, it picks up. It's userbase consists of a mix of Japanese and English users, the only time that this happens for any considerable amount of time. Where it was first promoted is unknown, but there was some modest overlap with the fauna of Something Awful. In June, English users would convince the owner to make an imageboard. He makes two, officially becoming the first western imageboards. (n.b. Oekaki script imageboards appeared even earlier than world2ch, but those were not anonymous)
## 2003 Circa June/July 
15 years old anime fan from New York City named **Christopher "moot" Poole** joins `#raspberryheaven` IRC through ADTRW.[^12]
