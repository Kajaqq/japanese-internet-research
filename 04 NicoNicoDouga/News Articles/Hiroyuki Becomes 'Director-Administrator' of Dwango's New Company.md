# Hiroyuki Becomes "Director-Administrator" of Dwango's New Company

On 14 November, Dwango founded Niwango, a new company that will offer online information services for mobile phones.[^tn-niwango] It has brought in Hiroyuki Nishimura, administrator of 2channel, as a director.[^tn-hiroyuki] Dwango says the company "will work on creating new services not bound by the conventional framework of mobile phone content."

*(Photo: Hiroyuki Nishimura)*

The new company has capital of ¥90 million, 75.1% of it put up by Dwango. Seiji Sugimoto, general manager in charge of Section 2 of Dwango's Business Development Department, has become its representative director.[^tn-sugimoto] Mirai Kensaku Brazil, which runs "2channel Search", and the content producer CELL also took stakes.[^tn-partners]

Dwango has so far built services for the official sites of each mobile carrier, but the new company will specialize in services for general sites, the so-called *katte saito* ("unofficial sites").[^tn-katte] Together with Niwango, the Dwango group will build a platform for free content services funded by advertising and aim to expand its business for general sites.

To raise the quality of its services, among other things, it has brought in Nishimura, administrator of one of Japan's largest bulletin-board sites, as "Director-Administrator" (取締役管理人).[^tn-title] Nishimura is also a director of Mirai Kensaku Brazil.

Earlier this year, Dwango drew attention by featuring ASCII-art characters born on 2channel in a TV commercial.[^tn-aa]

> [!info] Cultural context and translator's notes
> This is a short news item from ITmedia Mobile, 14 November 2005. It is worth reading for what it does *not* say: Niwango is presented as a mobile email and advertising venture, and nothing hints that, thirteen months later, the same company would launch Nico Nico Douga (12 December 2006). The follow-up interview, [[What Is Dwango Starting with Hiroyuki]], explains the original mail-based plan. For the companies involved, see [[Niwango]], [[Dwango]], [[Mirai Kensaku Brazil(Future Search Brazil)]] and [[Dwango Contents(CELL)]]; for Hiroyuki, see [[Hiroyuki Nishimura]]. The photo caption of Nishimura had no image in the clipping and is kept as a placeholder.

[^tn-niwango]: Niwango (ニワンゴ) would later become the operator of Nico Nico Douga, launched in December 2006. According to the follow-up interview, the "Ni" stands for Nishimura, not for the "2" of 2channel. See [[What Is Dwango Starting with Hiroyuki]].
[^tn-hiroyuki]: Hiroyuki Nishimura (西村博之, known online as ひろゆき *Hiroyuki*) founded 2channel in 1999 and was its administrator (管理人 *kanrinin*) until 2009. See [[Hiroyuki Nishimura]] and [[2channel (2ch.net)]].
[^tn-sugimoto]: Seiji Sugimoto (杉本誠司) went on to head Niwango through the Nico Nico Douga years. His interview is in [[What Is Dwango Starting with Hiroyuki]].
[^tn-partners]: Mirai Kensaku Brazil (未来検索ブラジル, "Future Search Brazil") was a small company linked to Hiroyuki that ran search and other services for 2channel; CELL was a Dwango-affiliated content production company. Both are covered in the vault's Nicopedia translations.
[^tn-katte]: Japanese mobile internet in the i-mode era was split between carrier "official sites" (公式サイト), listed on the carrier's menu and paid for through carrier billing, and 勝手サイト (*katte saito*, literally "sites doing as they please"), independent sites reached by typing a URL and usually funded by ads. Dwango had made its money from paid official content, above all ringtones (着メロ *chaku-mero*).
[^tn-title]: 取締役管理人 combines the corporate title 取締役 (*torishimariyaku*, company director) with Hiroyuki's 2channel title 管理人 (*kanrinin*, site administrator). It is a joke title that plays on his online fame.
[^tn-aa]: 2channel's ASCII-art (AA) characters, such as Mona and Giko, were well known by 2005. The article does not say which characters appeared in Dwango's commercial; check before citing a specific one.
