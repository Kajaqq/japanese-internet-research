# Niwango Strangely Left Out of the "Miku Miku ni Shite Ageru" JASRAC Registration Affair

On Nico Nico Douga, which Niwango runs,
"Mikku Miku", sung by Crypton's Hatsune Miku, made with YAMAHA's VOCALOID technology,[^tn-miku]
was registered with JASRAC by Dwango without permission from all the various parties,[^tn-jasrac]
and Nico Nico Douga users protested, and Crypton reacted too,
and when Dwango apologized, suspicions came up that it had been distributing the song as a *chaku-uta* without permission...[^tn-chakuuta]

Huh? Wait, what about Niwango?[^tn-niwango]

[^tn-miku]: 「みくみくにしてあげる♪【してやんよ】」 ("I'll Miku-Miku You♪ (For Reals)"), by the producer ika, was posted on Nico Nico Douga in September 2007 and became one of the first big Hatsune Miku hits. "Mikku Miku" (みっくみく) is its catchy refrain and nickname. Hatsune Miku is a VOCALOID voicebank sold by Crypton Future Media, built on Yamaha's VOCALOID singing-synthesis engine.
[^tn-jasrac]: JASRAC is Japan's main music copyright collecting society. Registering a song with it puts its licensing under JASRAC's control, which many Nico users feared would stop free derivative works. The registration was made through Dwango's music publishing arm with the artist listed as "Hatsune Miku", a software product, without clear consent from the creator or Crypton. Dwango apologized and called it a mistake, and the episode fed a long debate about the rights of amateur VOCALOID creators.
[^tn-chakuuta]: 着うた (*chaku-uta*) were full-audio ringtone clips sold for mobile phones, Dwango's core business at the time; see [[What Is Dwango Starting with Hiroyuki]].
[^tn-niwango]: 蚊帳の外 (*kaya no soto*, "outside the mosquito net") in the title is an idiom for being left out of something. Niwango ran Nico Nico Douga, but the dispute was handled by Dwango and its music business.
