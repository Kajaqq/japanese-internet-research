**Niwango, Inc.** (株式会社ニワンゴ, *niwango, inc.*) is the former Japanese internet company that originally operated Niconico Douga.  
Corporate Registration Number: 8011001067664.

---

## Overview

Niwango was established as a collaborative joint venture funded by three corporate entities: **DWANGO Co., Ltd.**, **Mirai Kensaku Brazil LLC**, and **CELL Co., Ltd.** 

The letter **"Ni"** in *Niwango* was taken directly from the surname of 2channel founder **Ni**shimura Hiroyuki (popularly known simply as *Hiroyuki*). The official corporate mascot character was also named **Niwango** (a stylized black cartoon creature with an oversized grin).

The Representative Director and President throughout its operational history was **Seiji Sugimoto**.

Hiroyuki held the unique corporate title of **"Director Administrator"** (*Torishimariyaku Kanri-nin*) from the firm's founding. However, on February 18, 2013, he formally resigned his directorship, citing "personal reasons."

On October 1, 2015, Niwango, Inc. was formally absorbed and merged into its majority owner, DWANGO Co., Ltd. (with Dwango continuing as the surviving legal entity), and Niwango was officially dissolved.

---

## Chronological History

- **November 14, 2005:** Founded as a joint venture company.
- **January 16, 2006:** Public beta launch of the free mobile email search portal service, **"Niwango."**
- **January 15, 2007:** Niconico Douga version upgrade to **(β)**; Niwango officially assumes role as the service's operating company.
- **November 13, 2014:** Dwango announces its acquisition of all outstanding minority shares, making Niwango a wholly owned subsidiary.
- **February 28, 2015:** The original mobile email portal service "Niwango" is officially discontinued.
- **October 1, 2015:** Niwango, Inc. is legally dissolved and absorbed into DWANGO Co., Ltd.

---

## Primary Business Activities

- Planning, software development, and operation of video-sharing platform **Niconico Douga**.
- Planning, architecture development, and management of proprietary video-upload server **SMILEVIDEO**.
- Information broadcast and delivery services utilizing mobile email, web protocols, and PC internet platforms.
- Advertising delivery, sponsorship sales, and advertising agency operations tied to mobile email and internet portals.

## Cultural Context & Terminology Guide: Niwango, Inc.

- **The Portmanteau Name:**  
  The name **Niwango (ニワンゴ)** is a direct portmanteau blending **Ni**shimura (西村) and D**wango** (ドワンゴ). It celebrated the high-profile alliance between Dwango's corporate tech empire and 2channel's eccentric creator, Hiroyuki Nishimura.
- **The "Email Portal" (Niwango's Forgotten Original Product):**  
  Niwango was **not** originally founded to build a video site! In 2005, years before the iPhone or mobile Safari arrived in Japan, Japanese feature phones ("galapagos phones") had primitive web browsers with expensive per-megabyte data fees, but mobile phone carriers offered completely free, unlimited text-based carrier email (`@docomo.ne.jp`, etc.).  
  Niwango was invented as a clever "email-based search engine bot." A user added `niwango@...` to their contacts and emailed it single-word commands like *"weather Tokyo"*, *"train delay"*, *"fortune"*, or *"wiki Einstein"*. Within seconds, Niwango's automated server parsed the text and emailed back a condensed reply. The service became a massive viral hit among Japanese high school students in 2006.
- **How Niconico Ended Up at Niwango:**  
  When Dwango engineers developed the experimental Niconico Douga comment overlay prototype in late 2006, it was legally perilous: it scraped YouTube's video files, consumed vast bandwidth, and violated copyright laws. Rather than risk the publicly listed parent company Dwango Co., Ltd. on the Tokyo Stock Exchange, management spun the controversial project out under the unlisted subsidiary **Niwango**, which was already known for counter-cultural internet experiments and associated with Hiroyuki.
- **"Director Administrator" (取締役管理人):**  
  On 2channel, Hiroyuki never called himself "CEO" or "President"; he famously titled himself simply the **Kanri-nin (管理人)**—the "site administrator," "building janitor," or "caretaker." When Niwango incorporated, they created the whimsical hybrid title *Torishimariyaku Kanri-nin* ("Director Caretaker") specifically for Hiroyuki, marrying formal Japanese corporate law with 2channel message board culture.
- **SMILEVIDEO:**  
  In February 2007, YouTube blocked Niconico from streaming YouTube videos through its player. Faced with total shutdown, Dwango and Niwango rapidly engineered their own internal video hosting server in just a few days, branding the upload server as **SMILEVIDEO**. From 2007 until it was seamlessly consolidated into Niconico Douga in the 2010s, all video files on Niconico were officially hosted and managed by SMILEVIDEO.
- **The Mascot Creature:**  
  The company mascot, *Niwango-kun*, was a minimalist, blobby black character with two white eyes and a wide, open triangular grin, often drawn alongside *Nico Nico Terebi-chan* on error pages and promotional merchandise.
