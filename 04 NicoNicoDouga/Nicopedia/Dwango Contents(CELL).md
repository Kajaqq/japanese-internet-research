**Dwango Contents Co., Ltd.** (株式会社ドワンゴコンテンツ) is a former Japanese digital venture company that operated in digital content production, television broadcast media, and video software development.

Among its prominent employees was creative producer **Foo-san**.

---

## Overview

Within the Niconico Douga ecosystem, the company was responsible for:

- Designing and producing **Video Deletion Notice screens** and **Hourly Time Chime Advertisements** (*jihou*).
- Planning, staging, and managing the **International Nico Nico Film Festival** (*Kokusai Niconico Eigasai*).
- Production, stage direction, and talent casting for **Niconico Live broadcasts** and **Nico Nico Ichiba in-store retail events**.

During the "Non-Engineer Doubling Campaign: 100 Recruits!" talent drive held in the summer of 2008 in conjunction with Dwango, CELL's crucial operational duties behind Niconico were formally revealed to the public. The company's staff members are also explicitly credited in Niconico's anniversary end-roll credits.

---

### Brief History

- **May 2001:** Founded originally as **CELL LLC** (有限会社CELL) after current Representative Director & Chairman Daisuke Yokosawa was commissioned by Dwango to compose polyphonic mobile ringtones (*chakumero*). The company became intimately involved in Dwango's expanding mobile ecosystem. Reorganized into a joint-stock corporation (**CELL Co., Ltd.**) in January 2003.
- **2004:** Tomoyuki Hara, an experienced executive running a television production firm, was appointed president. Established a dedicated broadcast and video software division, contributing to the production of major commercial television broadcasts—including Fuji TV's flagship music variety show *HEY!HEY!HEY! MUSIC CHAMP*—as well as corporate TV commercials.
- **Early 2011:** Formally became a corporate subsidiary of Dwango. (The share transfer agreement was approved by Dwango's board on January 27, and share transfer finalized on February 21).
- **March 29, 2011:** Senior Executive Officer Toshiya Sagara was appointed Representative Director & President. Tomoyuki Hara transitioned to Director on the same date.
- **January 1, 2013:** Inherited portions of Dwango's portal operations and live event production divisions, officially renaming the corporation to **Dwango Contents Co., Ltd.**
- **October 1, 2014:** Legally dissolved and absorbed into parent firm DWANGO Co., Ltd. (with Dwango continuing as the surviving corporate entity).

## Cultural Context & Terminology Guide: Dwango Contents (CELL)

- **The Creative Arm of Niconico:**  
  While Dwango provided the raw software engineering and server infrastructure, and Niwango served as the administrative publisher, **CELL (later Dwango Contents)** provided the eccentric creative soul, video production, and variety-show sensibilities that defined early Niconico Douga.
- **Daisuke Yokosawa (Founder):**  
  Yokosawa began as a sound designer composing ringtones for Dwango's mobile services. He went on to become one of the most important creative executives in Dwango's history: the master planner and chief executive director behind **Niconico Chokaigi** (the massive physical convention filling Makuhari Messe every year) and *Nico Nico Live*.
- **The Infamous "Jihou" (時報 - Hourly Time Chimes) & "Jihou Onna":**  
  In the early years of Niconico (2007–2010), when server capacity was severely strained late at night, Niconico implemented automated hourly notifications at times like 12:00 AM, 1:00 AM, and 2:00 AM.  
  - Without warning, an unskippable audio chime accompanied by a banner would take over the entire video player, chiming: *"It is now 2:00 AM! Good boys and girls should get ready for bed!"* or announcing commercial brand campaigns.  
  - For free users, it interrupted whatever song or anime they were watching. The professional female announcer who recorded these chime prompts was universally nicknamed by the community as the **Jihou Onna (時報女 - "The Hourly Chime Woman")**. Producing these chimes and selling commercial ad slots for them was one of CELL's signature duties.
- **"Deleted Video" Splash Screens (削除動画):**  
  When a video was taken down due to copyright infringement or violations of terms of service, Niconico did not simply return a standard 404 error. Instead, the player displayed stylized, humorous placeholder screens—such as a cartoon illustration of the mascot being dragged away or comedic apology cards. These memorable placeholder assets were created and animated by the team at CELL.
- **The "Non-Engineer Doubling Campaign" (非技術者倍増計画):**  
  In mid-2008, Niconico was growing at a pace that pure tech engineers could no longer manage alone. The site was evolving into an entertainment media empire requiring copyright clearing, event production, variety shows, and community moderation. Dwango and CELL ran a celebrated, self-deprecating hiring campaign specifically seeking "100 Non-Engineers"—drawing eccentric talent, animators, event organizers, and subculture writers into Dwango's corporate fold.
- **Foo-san (Yohei Hirose):**  
  CELL was the corporate home of **Foo-san**, Niconico's beloved staff sound engineer. Aside from his famous maintenance harmonica performances, Foo-san served as a creative producer directing live programs, hosting comedy events, and acting as the cheerful, transparent bridge between management and the chaotic userbase.
