# First-Generation Teto (初代テト)

> Original Source: [初代テト - ニコニコ大百科 (Nico Nico Pedia)](https://dic.nicovideo.jp/a/%E5%88%9D%E4%BB%A3%E3%83%86%E3%83%88)

**First-Generation Teto** (*Shodai Teto*) is **the "someone" who, in an alternate timeline, might have become Kasane Teto.**  
This figure is often referred to by aliases like **"Doriemon"** (ドリル + ドラえもん, *Drill-emon*), or reverently addressed by the community as **"Shodai-sama"** (*Her Ancestral Majesty*).

---

## Overview

> **477: Anonymous VIPPER: 2008/03/30 (Sun) 01:30:39.49 ID:FfROale50**  
> *This is what you guys meant, right?*

Kasane Teto (originally codenamed *Teto Petensson*) was initially an imaginary "fake VOCALOID" dreamed up by users on 2channel’s VIP board as an elaborate April Fool's Day prank for 2008.  
After the character's base profile attributes were determined via board *anka* (random post-number prompts), the thread moved to the visual design phase. The very first image dropped into the thread was this "First-Generation Teto."

True to the chosen *anka* prompt, the image featured drill-shaped pigtails—plastered onto a character modeled directly after classic, Nobuyo Oyama-era **Doraemon**.

Remarkably, the user who created and posted this First-Gen Teto image was none other than **"Sen" (線)**—the very same artist who would go on to illustrate Kasane Teto’s final, iconic official design.  
In a sense, one could easily argue that this bizarre entity stands as the defining symbol of Teto’s chaotic and eccentric origin story.

### About the Person in the Original Photo

The image of First-Gen Teto was a composite based on a photograph of a real cosplayer dressed in a somewhat cursed Doraemon costume: a cosplayer known as **Peperoncino X**.  
Later on, when the official Kasane Teto management circle *TwinDrill* launched their website, they noted that Peperoncino X had graciously given his blessing for the photo to be immortalized as "Shodai-sama."

The official blog post documenting this approval concluded with the following line:

> *"Please note that while neither Sen nor TwinDrill owns the intellectual rights to this image, we ask that you continue to show it love and cherish it just as you do Kasane Teto."*

...Wait, are we *seriously* supposed to cherish this thing?

## Cultural Context & Slang Guide

### 1. "Anka" (安価)
**Anka** (short for *ankā* / "anchor") is a popular game mechanic on Japanese imageboards. A thread creator posts a prompt and lets an arbitrary future post number decide the answer. 
For example:
> *Thread OP: ">>15 decides her hairstyle!"*  
> *Post 15: "Drill twin-tails!"*  
> *Thread OP: ">>30 decides her age!"*  
> *Post 30: "31 years old!"*

This is how Kasane Teto ended up with such absurd initial lore: a 31-year-old chimera whose favorite food is French bread, possesses drill pigtails, and has wings. 

### 2. "Doriemon" (ドリえもん) and Nobuyo Oyama
When artist **Sen (線)** read the *anka* prompt for "drill twin-tails," they opened an image editor and slapped drill pigtails onto a photo of an infamous, slightly scruffy bootleg-looking Doraemon cosplayer (Peperoncino X). 
- **Doriemon:** A portmanteau of **Drill** (*doriru*) + **Doraemon**.
- **Nobuyo Oyama (大山のぶ代):** The legendary voice actress who voiced Doraemon from 1979 to 2005. Calling something "Nobuyo Oyama style" invokes the classic, vintage retro-anime aura of Doraemon.

### 3. "Teto Petensson" (テトペッテンソン)
Before her surname was finalized as *Kasane* (重音, "layered sound"), the pranksters nicknamed her **Teto Petensson**. This was a multi-layered pun referencing:
1. *Tetopepetenson*, a catchy surreal folk song popular on NHK’s *Minna no Uta*.
2. **Peten** (ペテン), the Japanese word for a swindle, con, or scam.

### 4. "Subspecies" (亜種 - Ashu)
In Vocaloid fandom, **subspecies** (*ashu*) refers to fan-made derivative characters created by the community. You are likely familiar with Vocaloid subspecies like **Akita Neru** (the tsundere yellow-haired girl representing board trolls) and **Yowane Haku** (the depressed white-haired girl representing failed music creators). 
The joke in the article (*"A subspecies... really...?"*) expresses disbelief that an absurd meme-within-a-meme like First-Gen Teto actually inspired its own fan derivatives!
