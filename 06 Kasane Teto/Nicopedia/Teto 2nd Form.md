**Teto's Second Form** (*Teto Daini Keitai*) refers to an eccentric ability originally recorded in Kasane Teto’s 2channel profile lore, said to trigger whenever she encounters a mouse or rat.

---

## Overview

When Kasane Teto’s fictional profile was being constructed via anonymous prompts (*anka*) on 2channel's VIP board in March 2008, a user submitted a proposal for her "Second Form." (Although the poster subsequently tried to retract the suggestion, the thread had already locked it in via the *anka* rules, and the setting was honored out of respect for board tradition):

- **Trigger condition for Second Form:** Encounters a mouse/rat.
- **What happens in Second Form:** The drill portions of her twin-tails begin to spin.  
*(What her full transformed appearance looks like was left officially undefined—an ambiguity that granted creators total artistic freedom).*

On Nico Nico Douga, however, video animators and producers have largely discarded the mouse requirement, allowing Teto to trigger her Second Form at will.

## Cultural Context & Slang Guide: Teto's Second Form

### 1. The Doraemon Connection (Fear of Mice)
Teto's "Second Form" being triggered by mice is another direct parody of **Doraemon**. In the classic manga, Doraemon's robot ears were bitten off by robotic mice while he was asleep, leaving him permanently terrified of even the tiniest mouse. 

When 2channel was building Teto's profile, users gave her an identical phobia: encountering a mouse causes her drill pigtails to activate and spin violently out of panic.

### 2. "It's Pretty Much This Guy's Fault" (だいたいこいつのせい - Daitai Koitsu no Sei)
A ubiquitous, tongue-in-cheek video tag on Nico Nico Douga. It is stamped on ground-zero videos that unexpectedly launched a massive meme, an enduring inside joke, or a widely accepted community headcanon that completely derailed a character's original canon. 

Here, it refers to **LamazeP** (the famous Vocaloid producer behind *PoPiPo*), who created an early MMD video depicting Teto with black bat wings and an animated chain tail, single-handedly turning her into a mischievous succubus in community consciousness.

### 3. "Doing a Gevanni" (ジェバンニが一晩でやってくれました)
*Gevanni* (or *Jevanni*) is classic Japanese internet slang originating from Tsugumi Ohba’s manga **Death Note**. In the climax, the investigator Stephen Gevanni somehow manages to copy an entire complex Death Note journal by hand in a single night with superhuman perfection. 

On 2channel and Niconico, whenever a creator, animator, or programmer creates a ridiculously high-quality, complex video or 3D model overnight in response to a trend, users comment in awe:  
> **「ジェバンニが一晩でやってくれました」** (*"Gevanni pulled an all-nighter for us!"*)

### 4. "JK" in 2channel Slang
In the text, the author writes: *"That's way too Gevanni, JK."*  
While "JK" is commonly short for *Joshi Kōsei* (female high school student) in real-world Japanese, on 2channel textboards it is internet shorthand for:  
> **常識的に考えて** (*Jōshiki-teki ni Kangaete*) = *"Thinking about it logically"* / *"Obviously."*
