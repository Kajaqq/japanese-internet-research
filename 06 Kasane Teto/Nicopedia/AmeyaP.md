# [Ameya P](https://dic.nicovideo.jp/a/%E9%A3%B4%E5%B1%8Bp)
AmeyaP is the developer of the "UTAU" vocal synthesis tool .

## overview

AmeyaP is the developer of " UTAU ". On Nico Nico Douga , he posts cover songs using UTAU , including Kasane Teto , as well as experimental videos using other human-powered VOCALOIDs. 
He also has a small number of works that use VOCALOID.
Currently , while continuing to update UTAU, he also works on adjusting the original sounds for libraries such as Kazune Mako , Amane Luna , and Defoko. 
In comments on his blog , he often positions libraries and posted videos as by-products of development .

### Origin of the name
The name comes from his blog's username, "Ameya/Ayame ."

### Audio Library

While there are works that use the Kugimiya voice library (known as Kugimiyaroid ), AmeyaP himself has expressed concerns about refraining from using Kugimiya's voice and has now shifted to using voices such as Kasane Teto , the newly released Kazune Mako , and Amane Luna.
