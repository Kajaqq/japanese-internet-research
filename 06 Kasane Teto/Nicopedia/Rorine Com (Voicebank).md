**Rorine Com** is a voice library for the singing voice synthesis tool UTAU. At present, anyway. However, its existence predates UTAU itself.

---

## Overview

Describing Rorine Com simply as "a character created for the singing voice synthesis tool UTAU based on a freely distributed male voice sample pack" does not tell the whole story. The background is detailed below.

From autumn 2007 onward, following the release of Hatsune Miku, VOCALOID flourished immensely on Niconico Douga.  
Around that time, some individuals wondered: *“If we use the time-honored techniques of audio MADs, couldn't we make voices sing freely even if they don't exist as commercial VOCALOIDs?”* And so they tried.  
This marked the birth of **Jinriki VOCALOID** (Human-Powered VOCALOID).

On December 1, 2007, the user **LOLI.COM** uploaded a video titled *"How to Get Hatsune Miku for Free"* (the original video was lost due to an account ban, and distribution is currently hosted elsewhere). His idea was simple: he recorded roughly 8 musical pitches for each of the Japanese 50 syllables using his own voice and distributed them as raw audio data. By simply stitching and arranging these files on an audio timeline, anyone could make him sing at will.  
This library of recorded phonemes—~~pointlessly~~ extensive for its time—later became the catalyst for garnering spotlight in certain creative circles.

Despite marketing itself as a "Free Hatsune Miku," it was pure meme material: it was a male voice, had no character artwork, was not a standalone singing program but just a pile of raw audio data, and actually putting it to use required painstaking Jinriki VOCALOID construction skills.

Nevertheless, videos utilizing the audio pack soon began appearing on Niconico. The dozens of videos published were tagged not only with `Rorine Com`, but also playful tags like `Legendary Rorine Com Master` and `Victims of LOLI.COM`.

On December 15, 2007, **Ameya-P** also uploaded a video using Rorine Com (a revised version is currently uploaded).


Then, on January 11, 2008, Ameya-P uploaded an instructional guide on how to use Rorine Com, along with a custom program designed to simplify what was previously a brutally tedious manual process.


Ameya-P continued developing this "Jinriki VOCALOID support tool" to generate singing tracks directly from sets of audio files. During testing and development, Rorine Com, *Kugyuroid*, and *AquesTalk* were used as test sound sources. On March 6, 2008, a functional build of the software was publicly released. That software was subsequently named **UTAU**. The default sound source synthesized using AquesTalk came to be known as **Defoko** (Utane Uta).

Looking back, Rorine Com can arguably be considered the missing link connecting VOCALOID, Jinriki VOCALOID, and UTAU. (The version of Rorine Com available today is a re-release.)

Though Rorine Com lacked official character visual art for a very long time, an official design was finally created on September 3, 2009—one year and nine months after release.

## Rorine Com's Profile

- **Name:** Rorine Com (炉利音コム / ろりねこむ)
- **Voice Provider (CV):** LOLI.COM
- **Gender:** Male
---

## Rorine Com on Niconico Douga

- *Note:* "Rorine Com" refers to the set of WAV files released as freeware starting in December 2007 by **LOLI.COM**, a creator famous on Niconico Douga for the *Mirishira Series*. These samples are included as a voice library in *Rorine Com II*, but all copyrights remain with LOLI.COM. (Redistribution is carried out with explicit permission from LOLI.COM.)
