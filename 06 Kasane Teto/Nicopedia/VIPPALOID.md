**VIPPALOID** refers to a group of fictional Vocaloids born predominantly on Japanese anonymous textboards (most notably 2channel's VIP board), or an UTAU unit composed of their voicebanks. 

In a narrow sense, the term denotes the trio of **Kasane Teto**, **Yokune Ruko**, and **Namine Ritsu**, or the quartet formed by adding **Sukone Tei**. In a broader sense, it can encompass any fictional Vocaloid or UTAU voicebank originating from 2channel’s News4vip board, Seisaku Sokuhō VIP, or related message boards.

---

## Overview

As of November 2010, the following four characters are universally recognized as the core VIPPALOIDs:  
*(Dates in parentheses indicate when their respective prank campaigns took place)*

- **Kasane Teto (重音テト)** (April 2008)
- **Yokune Ruko (欲音ルコ)** (November 2008)
- **Namine Ritsu (波音リツ)** (October 2009)
- **Sukone Tei (健音テイ)** (June 2010)

Additionally, the following characters closely tied to the core four are frequently treated as VIPPALOIDs. During the *"VIPPALOID Festival 2010"* held on October 25, 2010, numerous videos featuring their UTAU voicebanks were submitted:

- **Kasane Ted (重音テッド):** A character representing Kasane Teto with her UTAU gender factor (`g-flag`) set to +30. However, Ted’s name, design, and settings did not originate directly on VIP.
- **Rook (ルーク):** A character created from a backup male voicebank prepared when the development of Yokune Ruko’s male voicebank encountered delays.
- **Ouka Miko (櫻歌ミコ):** A character created by repurposing the vocal samples of the alternate voice candidate who was not chosen when Namine Ritsu was transitioned into an UTAU voicebank.

Furthermore, the following characters are occasionally grouped under the broader VIPPALOID umbrella:

> **Aino Hate**, **Scott**, **Kotone Tsuru**  
> **Hinomoto Oniko**, **ISIS-chan**, **Sukone Teiru**, **Cool (UTAU)**, **Akune Kin**, **Chitose Rifu**, **Den'on Hibiki**, **Omoine Tsubasa**, **Jerusalem (UTAU)**, **Sorane Mako**, and many more.

When VIPPALOID was first formed as an UTAU singing unit, it consisted solely of three members: Kasane Teto, Yokune Ruko, and Namine Ritsu. However, following the arrival of Sukone Tei and the increasing inclusion of Kasane Ted and Ouka Miko in group videos, the meaning of the word shifted. Originally referring specifically to "hoax Vocaloid characters born on News4vip," it evolved into a broader term for characters with deep ties to 2channel, or the recognized quartet of Teto, Ruko, Ritsu, and Tei.

Using the 3D animation software *MikuMikuDance* (MMD) alongside custom 3D models, these characters can be animated to dance, act, and perform in dramas. Note that although they share a birthplace, **their intellectual property holders and managing volunteer organizations are completely separate and independent**. Each character has distinct terms of service and usage guidelines—exercise due care when creating derivative works.

### Orthographical Variations
Variations of the name include **VIPPERLOID**, as well as casing variations such as **Vippaloid**, **Vipperloid**, and **VIPERloid**. There is no single official capitalization rule. However, because both Nico Nico Pedia and the Pixiv Encyclopedia index the term as **VIPPALOID**, using that exact spelling is recommended when tagging videos and artwork.

### Subcategories and Classifications

- **VIPPALOID Three Girls (VIPPALOID三人娘, *VIPPALOID Sannin Musume*):**  
  *Kasane Teto, Yokune Ruko, Namine Ritsu*  
  The foundational unit of VIPPALOID. (The trio of 2channel-born characters Akita Neru, Yowane Haku, and Kasane Teto is separately referred to as the "Derivative Three Girls" / 亜種三人娘). Historically, "VIPPALOID" originally referred strictly to these three. The Pixiv Encyclopedia article tagline *"By the way, not a single one is a girl"* stems from this trio (see cultural notes). Beyond UTAU, all three have expanded into modern speech synthesis engines like TALQu and COEIROINK; with rich voice assets available, they are widely used for comedic skit theater (*chaban gekijō*) as well as singing.
- **VIPPALOID Four Girls (VIPPALOID四人娘, *VIPPALOID Yonin Musume*):**  
  *Kasane Teto, Yokune Ruko, Namine Ritsu, Sukone Tei*  
  The core canon of VIPPALOID. Because all four profiles were determined via community *anka* (forum anchors), their personalities are eccentric, highly distinctive, and charming. Universally recognized as VIPPALOIDs by the UTAU community, this lineup enjoys widespread mainstream acceptance.
- **Chōka-roid (釣果ロイド, "Catch-loids" / "Prank-loids"):**  
  *Kasane Teto, Yokune Ruko, Namine Ritsu, Sukone Tei, Aino Hate, Kotone Tsuru, Den'on Hibiki*  
  An UTAU unit classification strictly defined by characters that began life as message-board hoax Vocaloids designed to troll the internet. Unlike broader definitions, this category has a crystal-clear boundary. Characters like Ouka Miko, ISIS-chan, and Kasane Ted are excluded by definition. Each started as a fictional Vocaloid created in an *anka* thread to prank "Nico-addicts" (*Nico-chū*), but gained established designs, voice providers, and standalone teaser sites before being repurposed into functional UTAU voicebanks after the prank concluded.
- **VIPPALOID X-Members / VIPPALOID X-Girls (VIPPALOID〇人 / VIPPALOID〇人娘):**  
  A flexible naming convention where "〇" represents a number (e.g., "5-Member VIPPALOID"). Frequently used in video titles to clarify: *"While many VIPPALOID characters exist, this video/artwork specifically features only these X members."* Note that depending on the creator, a "5-person VIPPALOID" lineup might mean (Teto, Ruko, Ritsu, Tei, Ouka Miko), or it might mean (Teto, Ruko Male, Ruko Female, Ritsu, Tei).

## Crypton Future Media and VIPPALOIDs (Chōka-roids)

Publishing fake announcements for non-existent Vocaloids on spoof websites designed to mimic official corporate pages causes immense disruption and confusion for Crypton Future Media. Consequently, organizers behind most prank-born VIPPALOIDs (especially the *Chōka-roids*) sent formal written letters of apology to Crypton.

- Yokune Ruko's team apologized on Shitaraba textboard on July 13, 2010.
- Namine Ritsu's team apologized on Parsoku VIP on July 6, 2010.
- Sukone Tei and Kotone Tsuru both published official announcements on their websites confirming that Crypton had formally granted permission for their continued derivative activities:
  - Sukone Tei: June 19, 2014, *Sukottei Channel (Tentative)* — Important Announcement
  - Kotone Tsuru: June 25, 2015, *Kotone Tsuru Official* — Important Announcement

How does Crypton view these message-board hoax Vocaloids? Crypton treats them as a legitimate offshoot of **CGM (Consumer-Generated Media)** culture. In an April 2010 Q&A regarding whether derivative illustrations of characters other than Kasane Teto could be uploaded to Piapro, Crypton stated:

> *"Provided that the original copyright holders submit or explicitly permit the submission of their works, they are welcome. As a company, we warmly welcome onto Piapro characters created by artists who share our philosophy of accelerating CGM culture."*  
> *(Quoted from the official Piapro blog: `https://blog.piapro.net/2010/04/qa-2.html`)*

Because characters like Akita Neru and Yowane Haku were born from 2channel threads prior to Kasane Teto, Crypton views hoax Vocaloids as another creative manifestation of CGM culture. 

**However, posing as an official entity to announce a fake commercial Vocaloid constitutes fraudulent misrepresentation and phishing. In legal terms, this can be prosecuted under Japanese law as Obstruction of Business by Fraudulent Means (偽計業務妨害罪) or Defamation of Credit (信用毀損罪). Formally apologizing to Crypton and obtaining explicit permission for ongoing activities is an absolute legal necessity. Above all, because it borders on illegal conduct, orchestrating phishing-style hoax Vocaloid pranks should not be attempted.**

Kasane Teto in particular enjoys a warm, cooperative relationship with Crypton Future Media: Crypton publicly celebrates her birthday, produces official collaboration merchandise, and developer wat (Crypton’s chief vocal synthesis engineer) even cooperated in technical experiments testing Teto's UTAU voicebank inside the VOCALOID3 development environment.

Regarding Piapro submissions: Piapro requires explicit authorization from the character's copyright holder. Characters whose official sites prohibit Piapro submissions (such as Chitose Rifu) or whose creators have not registered authorization (such as ISIS-chan and Den'on Hibiki) cannot be posted to Piapro.

## Comprehensive Roster of VIPPALOID Characters

Because opinions differ on what qualifies as a VIPPALOID, this article defines a candidate character as meeting at least one of the following criteria:
1. Born on 2channel or related message boards;
2. Historically treated as a VIPPALOID in community media; OR
3. A recognized `g-flag` pitch-variant character of an existing VIPPALOID.

*(Note: Chibi/deformed parody derivatives such as Teto-nee, Sukone Teika, and Hateteyanyo are treated here as supporting mascots and are excluded from this primary roster table).*

### Edge Cases and Classification Difficulties
- **Kasane Ted:** Born organically on Nico Nico Douga as a pitch-shifted derivative of Teto; he did not originate on 2channel VIP. The first video on Niconico to use the tag "VIPPALOID" (*"[MMD] Kasane Teto's Song / VIPPALOID: Kosmos, Cosmos"*) appeared on February 2, 2010, whereas Kasane Ted's debut video (*"Made Kasane Teto (g+30) sing Kichiku Megane"*) dates back to October 2008. The creator of the first VIPPALOID unit likely did not plan on including `g-flag` derivatives. Nevertheless, Ted is so frequently grouped with them that his inclusion is left to personal interpretation.
- **Aino Hate:** Born on 2ch VIP, but her name lacks the traditional "〇-ne" (音, sound) naming pattern, and her profile was not bizarrely unhinged, leading many users to feel she lacked the chaotic "VIPPALOID identity."
- **Rook, Ouka Miko, Omoine Tsubasa, Shimazaki Eberhard:** Prominent figures like Ouka Miko and Rook originated on *Seisaku Sokuhō VIP*, not *News4vip*. If they count, should 4chan’s Omoine Tsubasa count? Should Shimazaki Eberhard, born in a Nico Nico Pedia wiki thread, count?
- **Scott vs. Gōon Nero:** Scott (born on VIP Service) explicitly claims the VIPPALOID title, whereas Gōon Nero (born on Seisaku Sokuhō VIP) explicitly disavows it on the grounds of not being created as a troll hoax.
- **ISIS-chan vs. Jerusalem (UTAU):** ISIS-chan was conceived to combat terrorism by hijacking search engine suggestions with cute moe imagery. Featuring her alongside Jerusalem (UTAU)—a character modeled after Islamic militants—causes severe conceptual and political friction.
- **Kyo'on Bochi vs. Akita Neru:** Kyo'on Bochi began and ended as a VIP troll without ever receiving a voicebank. If unvoiced characters qualify, should Akita Neru and Yowane Haku—classic 2ch characters who also lack proprietary synthesis voicebanks—be classified as VIPPALOIDs?

## Cultural Context & Terminology Guide

This section explains the unique Japanese message board culture, internet pranks, and community taxonomy that gave rise to VIPPALOIDs.

---

### 1. Etymology: "VIPPER" + "Vocaloid" = VIPPALOID
- **VIPPER (ビッパー):** A denizen of 2channel's chaotic *News4vip* (ニュース速報(VIP)) board, notorious for trolling, creative flash mobs, and hoaxes.
- Combining **VIP** (or **VIPPER**) with **Vocaloid** created the portmanteau **VIPPALOID** (alternatively spelled *VIPPERLOID*).
- While initially an umbrella term for fake Vocaloids concocted to troll the internet on April Fools' Day, it evolved into an official classification for the resulting UTAU voicebanks.

### 2. The Concept of "Chōka-roid" (釣果ロイド)
- **Tsuri (釣り):** Japanese internet slang for trolling, baiting, or "fishing" for gullible victims.
- **Chōka (釣果):** A fishing term meaning "the catch" or the haul of fish caught during an outing.
- **Chōka-roid (釣果ロイド):** A witty pun combining *Chōka* (the haul of trolled victims), *Tsuri* (trolling), and *Vocaloid*. It refers strictly to characters that began life as an actual online phishing prank before transitioning into working singing voicebanks.

### 3. The Core VIPPALOID Roster and "Not a Single One is a Girl"
The foundational group was the **"VIPPALOID Three Girls"** (*VIPPALOID Sannin Musume*): Kasane Teto, Yokune Ruko, and Namine Ritsu. A famous running gag documented on the Pixiv Encyclopedia notes: *"By the way, not a single one is a girl"* (ちなみに女の子は一人もいません). This refers to the bizarre anka-generated canon of each character:
- **Kasane Teto:** Officially a **31-year-old chimera** (humanoid with bat wings and drill hair).
- **Yokune Ruko:** Officially a **197-cm (6'6") hermaphrodite / intersex individual** (90% male, 10% female).
- **Namine Ritsu:** Officially a **6-year-old cross-dressing boy** (*otokonoko*), weighing 25 metric tons with built-in missile pods.

When the group expanded into the **"VIPPALOID Four Girls"**, they added:
- **Sukone Tei:** Canonically a violent, silver-haired **yandere** schoolgirl who despises Hatsune Miku, murders rivals with a box cutter, and harbors a delusional romantic obsession with Len Kagamine.

### 4. Legal Realities: Spoofing Crypton Future Media
- Fabricating a fake website that copies the branding, layout, and copyright notices of Crypton Future Media to announce a fake Character Vocal product constitutes serious legal violations in Japan:
  - **Obstruction of Business by Fraudulent Means (偽計業務妨害罪, *Gikei Gyōmu Bōgai-zai*):** Causing a business operational distress through deception.
  - **Defamation of Credit (信用毀損罪, *Shin'yō Kison-zai*):** Damaging the commercial trust and public reputation of a brand.
- When the pranks went viral, the organizers sent formal written apologies to Crypton. Crypton's leadership (notably CEO Hiroyuki Ito) graciously recognized them as an expression of **CGM (Consumer-Generated Media)** culture, allowing Teto, Ruko, and Ritsu onto their creative platform, Piapro, provided their creators explicitly permitted it.

### 5. Message Board Ecosystems in Japan
- **2channel / 5channel (2ch / 5ch):** Japan's largest anonymous BBS network. *News4vip* was its primary creative shitposting board, while *News4plus* was a news discussion board (where Akita Neru originated).
- **Seisaku Sokuhō VIP (制作速報VIP):** An independent offshoot textboard created specifically for collaborative creative endeavors (character design, music, doujin projects) without the chaotic trolling and spam of 2channel. Characters like Rook, Ouka Miko, and Chitose Rifu were born here.
- **Open2ch (おーぷん2ちゃんねる):** A copyright-free open board clone of 2channel launched in 2012, where Akune Kin was created.
- **4chan (`/vip/`):** The English-language imageboard created Omoine Tsubasa as an international answer to 2channel's VIPPALOIDs.

### 6. Notable Satirical & Sociopolitical Characters
- **Hinomoto Oniko (日本鬼子):** In 2010, anti-Japanese posts on Chinese message boards frequently used the historic ethnic slur *Riben guizi* (日本鬼子, literally "Japanese devil"). 2channel users responded with brilliant subversion: *"If they call us Japanese devils, let's design a super cute anime girl named Hinomoto Oniko!"* The moe character went viral, turning an insult into fan art, and later received an official UTAU voicebank.
- **ISIS-chan (ISISちゃん):** In February 2016, 2channel users organized a "Google-bombing" campaign against ISIS terror propaganda (*サジェスト汚染*, suggestion pollution). By flooding search engines and Twitter with a cute, green-haired moe girl who loves melons and wields a knife only to slice fruit, they successfully disrupted online terrorist recruitment algorithms. She was subsequently given an UTAU voice library.
- **NEET Voiceroids:** Unemployed, self-proclaimed "NEET" forum posters on 2channel who recorded their own mundane speaking and singing voices to create authentic "NEET-loids."
- **The Cellular Answering Machine Trio (Den'on Rusu, Tele, Maho):** Novelty voicebanks crafted entirely from the automated answering machine prompts of Japan's big three mobile carriers: au (*rusu* = answering machine), NTT docomo (*tele* = telephone), and SoftBank (*maho* = smartphone / *sumaho*).

### 7. The UTAU "g-flag" and Gender Derivatives
- In UTAU's resampling algorithms, the **`g` parameter** shifts vocal formants up or down. 
- Lowering or raising the gender parameter on female voicebanks creates realistic male, boy, or child vocals. The community took these parameter shifts and spun them into independent fanon characters (such as Kasane Ted, Sukone Teiru, and Rukuna), often giving them unique MMD 3D models and depicting them as siblings or lovers to the original VIPPALOIDs.
