**Mayo Oyamano** (小山乃 舞世 / *Oyamano Mayo*) is the human voice provider (*CV / voice source*) of Kasane Teto.

---

## Overview

Mayo Oyamano is the voice provider behind Kasane Teto. She is a multi-talented creator: a 2channel VIPPER, vocal manipulation artisan, digital illustrator, *utaite* singer, and video editor. Within the community, she is affectionately nicknamed **"Yamanon"** (やまのん).

Beyond recording Teto's character voice, her contributions to the franchise are energetic and wide-ranging, including official illustrations, original song compositions, and vocal tuning (tuning Teto's UTAU voicebanks with master precision).  
Chronologically, she had just graduated from middle school (around age 15) when Kasane Teto was created in late March 2008. On November 30, 2023, she reached age 31—the exact canonical age assigned to Teto herself.

Outside of her personal blog, she frequently drops by 2channel VIP boards and community hubs. As a vocalist, she possesses the remarkable ability to switch seamlessly between Teto's stylized synthetic character voice and her own natural singing voice.

---

## Regarding Her Name

In the original VIP thread where Kasane Teto was fabricated, her voice actress credit was jokingly written in Kanji as **小山 乃舞世**. Read straightforwardly, this looks like **"Nobuyo Oyama"** (*Oyama Nobuyo*), the famous voice of Doraemon.  
However, in follow-up threads, she playfully posted: *"Honestly, read it however you like, lol,"* meaning fans could pronounce it as either *Oyamano Mayo* or *Oyamano Buyo*. (Her fixed forum handle [*kotehan*] on 2channel was simply **"Oyamano"** [小山乃]).

*(Note: For international audiences, the pronunciation has since been formally standardized as **Mayo Oyamano** [おやまの まよ]).*

---

## Regarding Her Voice

Kasane Teto's vocal bank was constructed by mechanically pitch-shifting and processing audio samples recorded by Mayo herself.  
Regarding this process, Mayo has candidly remarked:  
> *"It sounds so completely different from my real speaking voice that when I hear Teto, I don't feel like 'Oh, that's me singing, lol' at all."*

She strongly emphasizes that Teto's vocal delivery is an independent character voice. She is conscious of drawing a clear line between the two, wishing to ensure that Teto fans are neither confused into believing Teto is her raw singing voice nor disappointed by any perceived discrepancy with her natural vocal timbre.  
In her cover of ryo (supercell)'s *"Black★Rock Shooter,"* she showcased this duality by singing the lead vocal part in her processed "Teto voice" while performing the backing harmony lines in her natural singing voice.

In addition to musical singing samples, she has recorded numerous spoken voice lines (*serifu*) for Teto, which have delighted fans in story videos. A famous example is the short video *"Older Little Sister"* (*Toshiue no Imouto*), where the canonically 31-year-old Teto slowly walks toward the screen and whispers *"Onii-chan, daisuki"* ("I love you, big brother")—a concept fans described as delightfully scandalous yet thoroughly heartwarming.

## Cultural Context & Slang Guide: Mayo Oyamano

### 1. The Nobuyo Oyama Pun: How Her Name Came to Be
When 2channel VIP was building the fake VOCALOID prank on March 30–31, 2008, every detail was designed as an elaborate parody of the real Vocaloid voice providers (like Saki Fujita for Hatsune Miku and Asami Shimoda for Rin/Len). 

Because the thread had already made the running gag that Teto was modeled on Doraemon ("Doriemon"), the pranksters listed her voice provider with the Kanji **小山 乃舞世**. 
- Look closely at the Kanji: **小山** (*Oyama*) + **乃舞世** (*No-bu-yo*).
- It is a transparent visual anagram and pun on **大山のぶ代** (**Nobuyo Oyama**, the iconic voice actress of Doraemon)!

When real-life teenager Mayo stepped forward to actually record the voice lines, she embraced the pseudonym, arranging the reading as **Mayo Oyamano** (*Oyamano Mayo*).

### 2. "Kotehan" (コテハン)
Short for *Kotei Handoru Nēmu* (**Fixed Handle Name**). On anonymous Japanese message boards like 2channel, virtually everyone posts under the default name "Anonymous" (*Nanashi-san*). Users who deliberately attach a consistent nickname or tripcode to build a personal identity are called *kotehan*. Mayo posted under the fixed handle "小山乃" (Oyamano).

### 3. "Toshiue no Imouto" (年上の妹 - The Older Little Sister)
In anime subculture, the *imouto* (little sister) archetype is almost universally younger than the protagonist. However, because Teto’s 2channel *anka* profile arbitrarily designated her age as **31 years old** (while retaining the visual appearance of a teenage girl), the community embraced the paradoxical concept of the "Older Little Sister." The phrase playfully captures the uncanny comedic clash between her mature canonical age and her cutesy anime antics.
