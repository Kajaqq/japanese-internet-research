> *"This is Charlie, commencing the Teto Offensive!"*

The **Teto Offensive** (*Teto Kōsei*) refers to a sustained military-style offensive campaign underway across Nico Nico Douga since April 1, 2008.

*(For the historical military campaign during the Vietnam War known as the "Tet Offensive," please consult Wikipedia or external historical literature via the upper-right link).*

---

## Phase One

On March 30, 2008, Operation *"Let's build a fake Vocaloid and troll the Nico-addicts"* was formally submitted on 2channel's VIP board. The proposal received immediate authorization. Racing against a tight 48-hour deadline, VIP mobilized using a rapid design doctrine known as "Anchor Decision" (*anka*). On April 1 of that year, VIP unleashed their fully primed combat asset, **"Kasane Teto" (Object 04)**, directly into the Niconico theater. Deployed as a diversionary strike (troll bait / *tsuri*), Teto achieved tactical outcomes far exceeding original strategic expectations.

---

## Phase Two

Upon completing her designated mission, Teto was scheduled for decommissioning and destruction. However, she was covertly recovered by a VIP case officer and transferred to the custody of UTAU. Field reports suggest this extraction occurred in response to impassioned pleas from software developers who lamented the premature scrapping of an asset as extraordinarily well-crafted as Teto (Object 04). Through technical cooperation with UTAU—which had already established an independent operational doctrine—Kasane Teto was endowed with new acoustic capabilities and assigned to re-infiltrate the interior of Nico Nico Douga (during this process, her serial designation was updated to **Object 0401**).

---

## Phase Three

Concealed beneath the deep-cover disguise of a "Vocaloid Parody," Teto began penetrating Niconico while keeping her true strategic intentions strictly classified. Although the full operational picture has not been disclosed, intelligence confirms the use of methods of questionable legal standing under standard conventions of war: weaponry unrecognized by standard military doctrine (such as drill-shaped twintails and French bread baguettes) and the donning of a military uniform intended to sow visual confusion among theater observers.

---

## Phase Four

Silently expanding her sphere of influence before the general viewership of Nico Nico Douga could grasp the situation, Teto seized key tactical infrastructure, including footholds within the Niconico VOCALOID Hall of Fame and the Weekly Vocaloid Ranking (Vokaran), and continued widening her operational gains. 

According to Theater Intelligence Division assessments, the probability of the entire Niconico platform being completely "Tetto-teto-ed" (subjugated to Teto) was deemed relatively low in the near term. However, the presence of partisan combat units conducting sustained penetration-and-assault operations alongside Kasane Teto was verified through the interception of advertising transmissions (*koukoku tsūshin*), resulting in a situation where no room remains for complacency.

---

## Phase Five (Current)

Subsequently, disparate partisan factions began coalescing, eventually organizing into a unified mobile strike formation known as the **Teto Offensive Force** (テト攻勢隊). On October 10, 2009, this force launched their first full-scale offensive. Furthermore, on that exact date, Teto was upgraded with a continuous-sound (VCV / *renzokuon*) voicebank engine. Galvanized by this enhancement, the offensive tempo has accelerated with each passing day. 

While this surge in hostile activity has settled into a temporary stalemate, conditions remain ripe for fresh assaults to erupt at any moment, and it is no exaggeration to state that the theater is hovering right at the critical threshold.

#### Operation Overview
The objective was daring and bizarre: capture all four random promotional showcase slots displayed on the top page of Nico Nico Douga.

### Operation X'mas (Tentative)

> ***"As a representative of listeners everywhere: to all UTAU uploaders, here is a message of heartfelt daily gratitude."***

- **Operational Proposal:** Delta  
- **Participating Units:** Charlie, Delta, Echo, Chimera, Fox, Dark  

#### Background
December 2009 brought the recurring festive season: the festival of lovers, by lovers, for lovers—commonly known as "Christmas." In 2009, following the viral autumn explosion of Hatsune Miku’s anthem *"Normies Explode!"* (*Riajū bakuhatsu shiro!*), an aggressive anti-Christmas sentiment had spread across the 2525 Theater.  
Amidst this cynicism, one combatant reflected: *"Isn't Christmas supposed to be fun? If not, let's at least bring a little joy to someone."* Thus began Operation X'mas (Tentative).

#### Operation Overview
Originally intended as a solo rogue operation by Delta—who "used to love Christmas and probably still does"—the concept received unanimous backing from other units, escalating into a full-scale Teto Offensive campaign.  
The plan: assault "hidden gem videos that touched someone's heart but remained largely unknown." Expanding beyond Teto-only videos from Operation 10.10, the force unleashed an indiscriminate carpet-bombing run across anything related to UTAU.

#### Codename Origin
Unlike the organic naming of Operation 10.10, the codename for this campaign was hotly debated. Six candidates were considered, until Delta made the executive call to designate it "Operation X'mas (Tentative)."  
Intelligence reveals hidden layers behind this moniker:
1. The "(Tentative)" was made permanent because they couldn't agree on a final title—a Copernican leap of logic: *"Why not just leave it (Tentative) forever? lol"*.
2. In English, Christmas is written as "Christmas" or "Xmas" without an apostrophe. The apostrophe was deliberately inserted to evoke an anime cowlick (*ahoge*).

#### Tactical Characteristics
The primary objective was not conquering hostile factions, but delivering festive vitality to the UTAU sector as an expression of gratitude. Despite being an indiscriminate carpet-bombing run, it was planned with unprecedented precision.  
Furthermore, all advertising transmissions were standardized with the prefix: *"This is [Callsign], ..."* to signal unified force coordination.

#### Battle Damage Assessment
On the Nico Nico Koukoku front page, the "UTAU" and "Kasane Teto" tags became the largest trending tags, capturing half of the Daily Advertisement Rankings. Because the attackers were purely focused on spreading seasonal cheer, suppressing them was straightforward.  
However, like dazzling illuminations on a winter avenue, the UTAU directory was blanketed in gleaming gold and silver borders. Caught amidst stalled reorganization, the 2525 Theater Defense Regiment failed to prevent the holiday decorations.

#### Aftermath
Shopkeepers along the commercial avenue welcomed the campaign as a major economic boom, sending Teto Offensive approval ratings soaring while theater defenses suffered a catastrophic loss of public trust.  
Following the operation, an illustrated PV for Mimi-Robo-P’s classic song *"Hirahira"* was produced in homage, officially immortalizing the words "Teto Offensive" (*Teto Kōsei*) within the video itself.  
While theater forces plotted a New Year counter-ambush against depleted partisan ranks, Callsign Sierra unexpectedly intervened with heavy rearguard support, causing the ambush to collapse.  
Taking responsibility for repeated tactical blunders, the 2525 Theater Defense Command sacked the regiment commander, placing the UTAU sector directly under the Supreme Commander's personal control.

### Operation Modest Monument

> ***"It was truly so much fun. Always, and forever, thank you so very much."***

- **Operational Proposal:** Delta  
- **Participating Units:** Charlie, Delta, Echo, Oscar, Dark, Sierra  

#### Background
In Summer 2009, during the 3rd MMD Cup, Charlie, Delta, and Echo formed a coordinated front that laid the groundwork for the (Former) Teto Offensive Group. Following the tournament's closing ceremony, they launched an offensive referred to in their own words as "Our Award Ceremony."  
In February 2010, the 4th MMD Cup concluded, prompting a successor strike: Operation Modest Monument.

#### Operation Overview
Targeting UTAU-related submissions in the 4th MMD Cup, the force swarmed every entry that had impressed them. It was a classic ambush, striking right when contest organizers, participants, and viewers had breathed a sigh of relief.

#### Codename Origin
Named by Delta. Commemorates the collaborative video from the 3rd MMD Cup that served as the foundational "monument" for the Teto Offensive Force.

#### Tactical Characteristics
The timing of the strike was crucial. Aiming to "gently lay a flower just when everyone thought it was over," the force deliberately delayed the strike until the day following the closing ceremony—close enough to remain relevant, yet late enough to catch defenses completely off-guard.

#### Battle Damage Assessment
Although defenses anticipated an eventual strike, its exact zero-hour defied all tactical predictions. Caught completely flat-footed, numerous videos sustained heavy promotional strikes. However, due to delays in the closing ceremony, rapid defensive regrouping, and coordinated intelligence counter-measures that successfully confused Delta prior to H-hour, overall damage was kept well below worst-case estimates. The offensive was suppressed far quicker than previous campaigns, and high command assessed the outcome as a defensive victory.

#### Aftermath
*Currently under active investigation.*

## Cultural Context & Terminology Guide

This article is an iconic example of late-2000s Niconico Douga **mock-military roleplay** (*chūnibyō* / military satire). Below is a guide to the specific platform mechanics, Japanese internet slang, and community memes being parodied.

---

### 1. The Core Pun: "Tet Offensive" vs. "Teto Offensive"
- **The Historical Event:** The **Tet Offensive (テト攻勢)** of 1968 was a famous surprise military campaign launched by the North Vietnamese Army and Viet Cong during the Vietnam War, timed to the Lunar New Year holiday (*Tết*).
- **The Fandom Meme:** Because Kasane Teto's name is spelled **テト (Teto)**, fans and video promoters dubbed their coordinated promotional blitzes on Niconico the "Teto Offensive." The article adopts the straight-faced tone of a formal military intelligence dispatch from an opposing army trying in vain to halt her spread.

### 2. The Fictional "2525 Theater Defense Command"
- In Japanese internet culture, numbers are often read through phonetic wordplay (*goroawase*):
  - **2 (ni) - 5 (co) - 2 (ni) - 5 (co) = Nico Nico.**
- Therefore, the **"2525 Theater Defense Command"** (第2525戦域防衛司令部) is the fictional military headquarters charged with defending the website Nico Nico Douga from being overrun by Kasane Teto and her devoted fanbase.

### 3. Nico Nico Koukoku (ニコニ広告) & "Advertising Transmissions"
- **Nico Nico Koukoku:** Niconico’s official paid promotional feature. Users purchase points (using real money or tickets) to advertise their favorite videos.
- **Carpet Bombing (絨毯爆撃):** When dedicated fans spend tens of thousands of points to boost a batch of niche videos simultaneously.
- **Gold & Silver Borders (金枠・銀枠):** When a video receives a substantial number of advertising points within a set timeframe, Niconico rewards it by wrapping its thumbnail in an illuminated **silver** or **gold** border on search pages and category rankings.
- **Advertising Transmissions (広告通信, *Koukoku Tsūshin*):** When promoting a video via Nico Nico Koukoku, users can attach a brief customized text message that displays publicly alongside their donation. The "combat transmissions" quoted in this article are the literal promotional messages submitted by real Niconico users.
- **The "Callsigns":** Charlie, Delta, Echo, Fox, etc., were the actual handles or public monikers of heavy-spending Teto super-fans who coordinated their point dumps to push UTAU songs into Niconico's daily rankings.

### 4. Famous Tropes & Platform Slang
- **Nico-chū (ニコ厨):** Derogatory/self-deprecating slang for obsessive Niconico video addicts (combining *Niconico* with *chūdoku* / addiction or *chūbō* / middle-school brat).
- **"Tetto-teto ni sareru" (てっとてとにされる):** A wordplay parodying Hatsune Miku's legendary 2007 debut track *"Miku Miku ni Shite Ageru♪"* ("I'll Miku-Miku You," meaning to charm/brainwash you into a Miku fan). To be "Tetto-teto-ed" means to have your mind completely conquered by Kasane Teto.
- **Vokaran (ボカラン):** The universal community shorthand for the *Shūkan VOCALOID Ranking* (Weekly Vocaloid Ranking), the most influential chart program on early Niconico.
- **"Normies Explode!" (リア充爆発しろ！, *Riajū bakuhatsu shiro!*):** A ubiquitous 2channel meme slogan directed at couples flaunting their romantic lives (*riajū* = "people fulfilled in real life"). In late 2009, a viral Hatsune Miku song titled *"Riajū bakuhatsu shiro!"* became Niconico's unofficial anti-Christmas anthem. Delta’s "Operation X'mas" was specifically launched as a wholesome counter-measure to spread positive cheer to underappreciated UTAU musicians instead.
- **Kikisen (聞き専):** Literally "listening-only." Net slang referring to dedicated fans who do not produce creative content (music, art, or videos) themselves, but passionately support the community by watching, commenting, sharing, and sponsoring creators.

### 5. *Teto-Teto-san* and the "Flag Battles"
- ***Teto-Teto-san* (テトテトさん):** A beloved, long-running fan-made hand-drawn comedy anime series created by animator **Kimura-P**, depicting the chaotic daily lives of Kasane Teto, Defoko, Momone Momo, and 2channel ASCII art characters like Dokuo.
- **Flag Battles (フラッグ戦):** Because new episodes were eagerly awaited events, top Teto promoters turned uploads into a sport: competing to see who could detect the newly published video first and drop the very first paid advertising transmission. The winner claimed the "Flag" for that episode.

### 6. The "Bravo Yamada" Taboo
- The roster notes that **Callsign Bravo** is permanently retired due to **"Bravo Yamada" (ブラボー山田)**.
- Bravo Yamada was an infamous, notorious troll and eccentric live streamer on early Niconico. His name became a running joke across the platform—a cursed persona that nobody wanted to invoke or be associated with, leading the group to skip the phonetic letter "B" entirely.

### 7. Emoticon `ｍ９（－Ⅹ－）`
- `m9` is the classic 2channel ASCII emoticon depicting a hand pointing an accusatory finger directly at the viewer (the `m` represents four curled fingers and `9` represents the extended index finger and thumb).
- Combined with `（－Ⅹ－）`, it depicts a stern, pursed-lip commander giving a blunt military directive.
