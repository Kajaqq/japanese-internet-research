# Niconico

**Niconico Douga** (ニコニコ動画) is a Japanese video-sharing service known for comments that scroll across the video. It has been run by **DWANGO** since December 12, 2006 (originally developed by its subsidiary **Niwango**). Its user base grew from displaced 2channel users during the January 2007 closure crisis, and it became the home of Vocaloid/UTAU, MAD videos and Nicopedia, the source of most translations in this vault.
