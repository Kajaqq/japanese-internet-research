# 4chan

**4chan** was founded on **October 1, 2003** by **moot**, then 15 and a regular of the `#raspberryheaven` DC++ hub and Something Awful's ADTRW forum. It was built as an English-language version of Futaba Channel (2chan), copying its software. [[Hiroyuki Nishimura|Hiroyuki]] of 2channel later took over as administrator (2015).
