# Futaba Channel (2chan)

**Futaba Channel** (ふたば☆ちゃんねる, "2chan") is a Japanese anonymous **imageboard** that began in 2001, separate from 2channel. Its board software and layout were copied directly for 4chan in 2003, making it the parent of Western imageboard culture.
