# Kasane Teto (重音テト)

**Kasane Teto** began as a fake Vocaloid made up in an *anka* (anchor) thread on 2channel's **VIP board** for **April Fools' Day 2008**. After the joke, the community turned her into a real singing voice with **UTAU**, Ameya-P's free voice-synthesis tool. She became one of the best-known UTAU characters, and later got official SV/VP voicebanks.
