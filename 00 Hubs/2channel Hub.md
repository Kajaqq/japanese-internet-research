# 2channel (2ch / 5channel)

**2channel** (2ちゃんねる) is Japan's largest anonymous textboard, founded in 1999 by **Hiroyuki Nishimura** (ひろゆき). It began as a refuge for users of the troubled Amezou World. A dispute with the later operator Jim Watkins led to the **2ch.sc** split in 2014, and the original site was renamed **5channel** in 2017. Boards such as **News Flash (VIP)** gave rise to many memes, including [[Kasane Teto Hub|Kasane Teto]].

> [!note] Naming
> "2chan" in English usually means **Futaba Channel**, not 2channel. The vault's `2chan/` folder covers both.
