**Mirai Kensaku Brazil LLC** (有限会社未来検索ブラジル, *Future Search Brazil*) is a Japanese information technology and web services company.

---

## Overview

Founded on April 10, 2003. Corporate Number: 4011002028025.

The company developed the open-source Japanese full-text search engine **Senna**, operates the dedicated **2channel Search** engine, and manages backend infrastructure for the early internet virtual currency **Moritapo**.  
It also collaborated on the backend search development for *Livedoor Blog Search* (though it does not handle operations).

Mirai Kensaku Brazil was the original creator and operating company of this encyclopedia, **Nico Nico Pedia**. However, in early July 2017, Brazil handed over the encyclopedia business to Dwango subsidiary *Daihyakka News Inc.* Following the handover, Brazil continued to be contracted to operate the off-site "Pedia Deletion Request Board." In December 2017, an integrated directory and report system ("Nico Nico Pedia: Deletion Request BBS") was built directly inside Nico Nico Pedia, leading to the formal closure of the external deletion board.

Hiroyuki Nishimura is one of the company's directors.

In the past, the company also served as the primary advertising agency for 2channel. [1]  
*(Chronology of 2channel advertising brokers: Jingumae.org → Tokyo Access, Marionette Corporation [for i-mode] → Mirai Kensaku Brazil).*

In January 2016, the corporate office relocated adjacent to Suehirocho Station in Akihabara, Tokyo.

The company name was inspired by Terry Gilliam's 1985 dystopian sci-fi cult film, *Brazil* (released in Japan under the translated title *Mirai Seiki Brazil* [Future Century Brazil]). [2]

## Related Services & Software

- **Nico Nico Pedia (ニコニコ大百科):** The collaborative encyclopedia for Niconico culture. (Originally created and operated by Brazil; transferred to Daihyakka News Inc. in July 2017).
- **Pedia Deletion Request Board:** An external forum formerly used to submit privacy and copyright deletion claims for Nico Nico Pedia articles. Closed after being integrated directly into the encyclopedia.
- **Moritapo / Morita Portal (モリタポ):** A proprietary digital micropayment and virtual currency system used across 2channel-affiliated platforms.
- **p2:** An ASP/web-based dedicated 2channel browser. Enabled users to browse and post to 2channel boards remotely through proxy servers; rendered unusable in March 2014 following the server takeover.
- **MOGERA (モゲラ):** An indie web gaming and browser game showcase portal.
- **Kossori Enquete (コッソリアンケート):** A lightweight, anonymous user polling and survey tool ("Kossori" means secretly or quietly).
- **2channel Search:** A dedicated search engine for 2channel message boards. Like *p2*, it became inoperable in March 2014.
- **Blomag (ブロマガ):** Niconico's blog-magazine publishing platform. Brazil participated in the development and management of its "Major Media Corporate Plan." [3]
- **2channel (2ちゃんねる):** Brazil previously served as the authorized data distributor, scraping raw board data and licensing structured 2ch feed APIs to third-party developers. However, in March 2014, American server host Jim Watkins seized control of 2channel's servers and domain, locking out Brazil and Hiroyuki. Since then, Brazil has been unable to acquire 2channel data feeds. *(See coverage: "President Dumps Large Volume of Stock Prior to 2ch Takeover Announcement; Company Flatly Denies Insider Trading Allegations" – J-CAST News).*
- **Tarako Tataki (タラコたたき):** A browser-based parody Flash game ("Whack-a-Tarako") created as a running joke teasing Hiroyuki's internet nickname.
- **MiyouTV:** A network television recording and collaborative viewer system.

## Cultural Context & Terminology Guide: Mirai Kensaku Brazil

- **The Corporate Vehicle of the "2channel Clique":**  
  *Mirai Kensaku Brazil* (often simply called **Brazil** or **Razil**) was founded by Naozumi Takenaka, Hiroyuki Nishimura, and their close software circle. While Dwango provided the corporate scale and server hosting for Niconico, Brazil served as the nimble, eccentric "hacker shop" that built experimental web applications and monetized 2channel behind the scenes.
- **Why "Brazil"?**  
  The company took its name from Terry Gilliam's satirical cult film *Brazil* (1985), which depicts an absurd, surreal bureaucracy ruled by clunky technology and paperwork. The company's internal tools, domain name (`razil.jp`), and irreverent corporate ethos were deliberate nods to the film.
- **Moritapo (モリタポ): An Early Virtual Currency:**  
  Long before Bitcoin or modern microtransactions, Brazil introduced **Moritapo** in the early 2000s. Users bought Moritapo with real money or earned it by filling out surveys (*Kossori Enquete*). It was spent across the 2channel ecosystem: paying to search massive multi-gigabyte thread archives, purchasing access passes to bypass posting bans (*2ch Ronin*), and tipping creators.
- **"p2" and Dedicated Browsers (専ブラ - Senbura):**  
  Browsing 2channel via standard web browsers was frustrating due to aggressive anti-spam IP bans, board crashes, and primitive layouts. Japanese developers built specialized software called "Dedicated Browsers" (*senbura*). Brazil created **p2**, a server-side web-based browser where Brazil's servers downloaded the threads and rendered a clean, mobile-friendly interface for the user. It allowed users to bypass school or workplace network filters and post without revealing their personal IP addresses.
- **The March 2014 2channel Coup (The Jim Watkins Incident):**  
  2channel's physical servers were hosted in California by an American company owned by Jim Watkins (NTec / San Francisco). In February/March 2014, Watkins abruptly locked Hiroyuki and Brazil out of the server farm and domain registry, claiming Hiroyuki had defaulted on server maintenance bills. Watkins seized 2channel, installed himself and his son as administrators, and stripped Brazil of its lucrative advertising and data-distribution rights. Hiroyuki countersued, set up a rival clone called *2ch.sc*, and Watkins eventually rebranded 2ch.net to *5channel (5ch)* in 2017 to dodge trademark lawsuits.
- **"Gunyarakun ★" (グニャラくん ★):**  
  The pseudonym of a brilliant eccentric software engineer who worked at Brazil. Gunyarakun single-handedly coded the backend architecture of **Nico Nico Pedia** in 2008. The star mark (**★**) next to his name is a traditional badge of honor originating on 2channel, where volunteer moderators and server administrators were designated with a "★" attached to their tripcode or username.
- **"Tarako" (タラコ):**  
  **Tarako** (salted cod roe) was the universal 2channel nickname for Hiroyuki Nishimura, coined because 2ch users mockingly felt his thick lips resembled plump cod roe. The game *Tarako Tataki* ("Whack-a-Tarako") mentioned in the article was a playful piece of internal self-mockery coded by Brazil's programmers.
