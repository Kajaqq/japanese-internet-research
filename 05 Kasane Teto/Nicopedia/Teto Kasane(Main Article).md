**Kasane Teto** (重音テト) is a fictional Vocaloid born from an *anka* (anchor) thread on 2channel's VIP board...

...Or at least, that is how she started. Following April Fools' Day 2008, her story took an unexpected turn.

## Overview

### UTAU Voice Library: Kasane Teto

| Basic Information | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Voice Provider** | Mayo Oyamano (小山乃舞世) |
| **Character Designer** | Sen (線) |
| **Nico Nico Debut** | April 6, 2008 |
| **Voice Gender** | Female Voicebank |
| **Library Types** | CV (単独音, *Tandokuon*) / VCV (連続音, *Renzokuon*) |

Kasane Teto was originally conceived as a practical joke parodying Crypton Future Media's "Character Vocal Series" (the product line that spawned Hatsune Miku and Kagamine Rin/Len). In particular, her official teaser site was built as an April Fools' prank on April 1, 2008, by volunteers from a 2channel VIP thread. A careful look at the site's URL and content revealed an elaborate assortment of jokes and gags hidden throughout.

Following the prank, an actual voice library for Kasane Teto was released for use in the freeware singing voice synthesizer "UTAU." This marked the very first user-developed UTAU voice library. As long as users had UTAU and Teto's library installed, anyone with a bit of technical skill could make her sing. As her voicebank was gradually updated and improved, a vibrant creative community blossomed around her, and she went on to become the undisputed flagship representative of UTAU.

Although born from a mere joke, on April 1, 2010, Crypton Future Media and Teto's official management circle, **TwinDrill**, issued a joint statement officially recognizing Kasane Teto as a character born from the Vocaloid creative movement. In tandem with this statement, Teto's usage guidelines were updated. *(Note: This did not mean copyright was transferred to Crypton.)*

This landmark agreement officially allowed users to submit Kasane Teto illustrations to Crypton's content platform, Piapro (see announcements from Crypton and TwinDrill).

Creativity around Teto continued unabated, later spawning a speech-specialized voicebank for the TALQu engine. Years passed, and in April 2023:

Kasane Teto was officially released as a commercial software vocal for **Synthesizer V**.  
TwinDrill expressed their wish that creators continue to love and use Teto across all platforms—whether UTAU or Synthesizer V—viewing every version as the same beloved Teto tailored to individual artistic expression.

## Regarding the Change in Numbering

Originally, the red serial tattoo on Teto’s left shoulder was **"04"**. However, once April Fools' Day concluded, the creators realized that leaving it as "04" would cause confusion and trouble for Crypton Future Media. It was promptly altered to **"0401"**.

The initial choice of "04" instead of "03" was actually a design blunder caused by a VIPPER misunderstanding: they mistakenly assumed Kagamine Rin was 02 and Kagamine Len was 03 (in reality, Rin and Len were sold together as CV02). Although the real CV03, Megurine Luka, was not announced until early 2009, Crypton's official website had already noted that Character Vocal Series 03 was in development.

The new number, **0401**, represents the date of her birth prank: April Fools' Day (April 1st).

## Regarding the Change of Logo

The original "Kasane Teto" logo was deliberately styled to imitate the font and layout of Crypton's Character Vocal Series logos as part of the April Fools' bait.

However, as Teto’s mainstream fame grew, it became clear that using a logo that could deceive casual audiences into believing she was an official Crypton product (like Hatsune Miku) was untenable. TwinDrill decided to create a completely new official logo through an open community contest featuring submissions, public voting, and final jury selection.

On the night of December 13, 2009, during a Nico Nico Live broadcast hosted by TwinDrill, the winning new logo was officially unveiled.

## Kasane Teto on Nico Nico Douga

While Kasane Teto originated from an *anka* thread on 2channel's VIP board, numerous community interpretations and non-anka tropes took root on Nico Nico Douga:

- **Bokukko (僕っ娘):** Using the typically boyish first-person pronoun *boku* (僕). This likely stems from her signature line using the second-person *kimi* (君), but she has used *boku* since her earliest videos (Wanai-P's videos are believed to be the earliest source).
- **Wings:** A popular theory once held that Lamaze-P started the wing trope in *PoPiPo*, but bat/chimera wings actually appeared even earlier.
- **Tail:** Possessing a tail that looks identical to a wallet chain. This originated from Sen’s official character art: looking closely, a wallet chain inexplicably pokes out from under her skirt. Fans joked, *"Wait, that's actually a tail!"* MMD modeler Nanami codified this idea into 3D models, cementing it as canon.
- **VIPPER Slang:** True to her roots, depictions of Teto speaking classic VIP internet slang (such as ending sentences in `お` or making `(^ω^)` faces) are very common.

While there is no rigid dogma that says Teto *must* be depicted in a certain way, pondering "what makes Teto feel like Teto" is part of the fun:
- Despite her youthful appearance, fans sometimes make hopeful claims like *"She's a chimera, so she's 15.5 in human years!"*, but her official age remains **31 years old** (see *Teto-san, 31 Years Old*).
- Even so, don't despair: she is infinitely younger than Kamui Gakupo's canon age of 468.
- While her twin drills supposedly spin upon seeing a mouse, they also spin whenever she gets overly excited, making the rule wonderfully inconsistent.
- Appropriately for her name *Kasane* (meaning "layered" or "overlapping sound"), she has an exceptional reputation for harmony and backing vocals.
- Her character item, the French baguette, occasionally drives around under its own motorized power.
- She is frequently mistaken for UTAU's bundled default voice, Utane Uta (commonly known as Defoko).
- People often misread the kanji of her family name (重音) as *Jūon*, *Omone*, or *Shigene*. It is pronounced **Kasane**.
- There is a prevalent community understanding that *"as long as we handwave it by saying 'she's a chimera,' literally anything goes."*

## Cultural Context & Terminology Guide

This section explains the 2channel, Niconico Douga, and Japanese internet subculture references that shaped Kasane Teto’s creation and history.

---

### 1. 2channel, News4vip, and VIPPERs
- **2channel (2ch / now 5channel):** Japan’s most influential anonymous textboard system, founded in 1999 by Hiroyuki Nishimura. It served as the birthplace of modern Japanese meme culture (and directly inspired Western imageboards like 4chan).
- **News4vip (VIP Board):** Established as an "anything-goes" chat sub-board, VIP rapidly evolved into the chaotic ground zero of Japanese internet pranks, flash mobs, slang, and collective creative trolling.
- **VIPPER:** A self-deprecating term for regular users of the VIP board. VIPPERs were notorious for orchestrating elaborate internet pranks (*tsuri* / 釣り, literally "fishing" or baiting) to fool mainstream internet users.
- **VIPPER Slang:** VIP developed its own bizarre dialect, including ending sentences with `お` (*o*), typing celebratory ASCII chants like `(^ω^)おっおっおっ`, and shouting sarcastic memes. The original website for Teto incorporated this slang throughout its fake sales copy.

### 2. The April Fools' "Fishing" Prank of 2008
- In late 2007, Hatsune Miku (CV01) took the internet by storm, followed quickly by Kagamine Rin & Len (CV02) in December 2007. Crypton Future Media announced that a third installment—**Character Vocal Series 03**—was in development.
- By March 2008, Vocaloid fandom was at fever pitch anticipating CV03. VIPPERs seized the opportunity to pull off an epic April Fools' troll: they decided to fabricate "the next official Vocaloid."
- **The "04" Blunder:** In their haste (creating the character in under 48 hours), the VIP thread thought Rin was 02 and Len was 03, mistakenly numbering Teto as **04**. After the prank ended, to avoid legal conflict or confusing buyers into thinking she was Crypton's product, her shoulder serial number was modified to **0401** (representing April 1st).
- Her fake website mirrored Crypton's layout, using the spoof domain `crvipton.jpn.org` (a pun on Crypton).

### 3. "Anka" (安価 / Anchor) Mechanics and Teto’s Absurd Lore
- **What is Anka?** On 2channel, replying to a post uses a number anchor like `>>50` (pronounced *anka*, which is a homophone for 安価, meaning "cheap" or "anchor"). In an "Anka Thread" (安価スレ), the thread creator lets a predetermined future post number decide the story or design (e.g., *"The new Vocaloid's age will be determined by post >>35"*).
- Because anonymous users intentionally post bizarre or conflicting ideas to derail threads, Teto ended up with surreal, contradictory attributes:
  - **Age: 31 years old:** While Vocaloids were marketed as teenage pop idols, anka decreed Teto was in her thirties. Fans playfully label her a "hag" or "cougar" (*Teto-san, 31 Years Old* / テトさんじゅういっさい), contrasting her youthful anime design.
  - **Gender: Chimera:** To reconcile absurd suggestions, she was deemed a chimera (part human, part bat/dragon), which fans used to excuse her having bat wings, a wallet-chain tail, and an unnatural age.
  - **Likes Baguettes / Dislikes DMC:** Completely arbitrary anka answers. Her baguette became her official handheld character item (mirroring Hatsune Miku's green onion and KAITO's ice cream). DMC refers either to the heavy-metal parody manga *Detroit Metal City* or Capcom's *Devil May Cry*.
  - **Special Skill: "Extending Rental DVDs":** Paying late return fees at video stores like Tsutaya—a mundane, slacker hobby for an anime android.

### 4. The Doraemon Gag & Mayo Oyamano
- On the fake teaser page, Teto’s voice actress was jokingly listed as **Nobuyo Ōyama (大山のぶ代)**, the legendary, raspy-voiced actress who voiced Doraemon for 26 years.
- Doraemon lore heavily infected Teto's early jokes:
  - Doraemon is terrified of mice; thus, Teto's "Second Form" triggers when encountering a mouse.
  - Her catchphrase, *"You really are an idiot, aren't you?"* (*Kimi wa jitsu ni baka da na*), is a famous, cold line Doraemon frequently directed at Nobita.
- When the VIPPERs decided to actually create a real voicebank, net user **Mayo Oyamano (小山乃舞世)** recorded the samples. Her pseudonym was an intentional anagrammatic wordplay tribute to Nobuyo Ōyama (*O-ya-ma-no Ma-yo* ↔ *Ō-ya-ma No-bu-yo*).

### 5. UTAU and the Birth of VIPPALOIDs
- **UTAU:** In March 2008, an independent developer named Ameya/Ayame released UTAU, a free Windows singing-synthesizer based on concatenative voice resynthesis.
- While the April Fools' joke could have died on April 2nd, the community fed Oyamano's recorded voice clips into UTAU. Teto became the first community-developed voicebank for the software, cementing her as UTAU's unofficial mascot.
- Following Teto's success, 2channel's VIP board repeated the prank formula in subsequent years, creating the **VIPPALOID** family:
  - **Yokune Ruko (欲音ルコ):** A 197-cm hermaphrodite / chimera with coffee and sleep obsessions.
  - **Namine Ritsu (波音リツ):** A cross-dressing 6-year-old boy weighing 25 tons equipped with missiles.
  - **Sukone Tei (健音テイ):** A yandere schoolgirl hopelessly obsessed with Len Kagamine.

### 6. Voicebank Technical Jargon
- **Tandokuon (単独音 / CV):** Traditional Japanese Consonant-Vowel recording (e.g., *ka*, *ki*, *ku*). Simpler to record, but prone to choppy, robotic transitions between syllables.
- **Renzokuon (連続音 / VCV):** Vowel-Consonant-Vowel recording (e.g., *-ka*, *a-ka*, *i-ka*). Developed by Ameya/Ayame in 2009, this drastically improved UTAU's vocal fluidity and realism.
- **Oto.ini Configuration (原音設定, *Oto-settei*):** The meticulous parameter-tuning file in UTAU that defines phoneme cutoff, pre-utterance, overlap, and consonant velocity. A well-tuned `oto.ini` is what makes an UTAU sound clear rather than garbled.
- **The "g-flag" & Kasane Ted:** In UTAU resamplers, the `g` parameter alters the formant/gender factor. Lowering Teto's gender parameter produces a masculine vocal tone. The community gave this male voice the persona **Kasane Ted (重音テッド)**, whom TwinDrill officially recognized as Teto's derivative counterpart.

### 7. Niconico Milestones and the "Fukkireta" Phenomenon
- **Niconico Hall of Fame System:**
  - **VOCALOID殿堂入り (Vocaloid Hall of Fame):** Videos surpassing 100,000 views.
  - **VOCALOID伝説入り (Vocaloid Hall of Myth / Legend):** Videos surpassing 1,000,000 views.
  - When Teto's duet *"Songstress of Lies"* hit 100k views, fans debated whether a non-Vocaloid qualified. This spurred the creation of the **UTAU Hall of Fame** (*UTAU殿堂入り*) and later **UTAU Hall of Myth** (*UTAU伝説入り*) tags.
- **"Fukkireta" (吹っ切れた):** In May 2010, an animator created a looping Flash animation of an anime girl swaying her hips and head side-to-side to Lamaze-P/Gojimaji-P's high-speed Teto song *"Ochame Kinou"* (おちゃめ機能). The infectious sway and song exploded across Niconico and early YouTube, sparking thousands of hand-drawn parodies from every anime fandom. It remains one of the defining viral memes of early Japanese web culture.

### 8. The "Chorogi" Donburi Gag
- In 2014, FamilyMart sold "Kasane Teto's Chorogi Don."
- **Chorogi (チョロギ / Chinese artichoke):** A small perennial tuber commonly pickled in Japan. It has a distinctive spiral, accordion-like shape that strongly resembles Teto's spiral drill twintails. FamilyMart turned this visual pun into a packaged meal for her fans.

### 9. Synthesizer V AI (2023) and Modern Resurgence
- For her 15th anniversary on April 27, 2023, Dreamtonics and AH-Software released **Synthesizer V AI Kasane Teto**.
- Shifting from the quirky, robotic freeware engine of UTAU to cutting-edge deep-learning neural synthesis transformed Teto into a mainstream powerhouse. She enjoyed a massive cultural resurgence, powering viral smash hits such as *Override* (オーバーライド) by Sasuke Haraguchi and *Favorite Side Dish Announcing Dragon* (好きな惣菜発表ドラゴン) by Frog96, proving her staying power 15 years after her birth as an April Fools' troll.
