# Kasane Ted (重音テッド)

**Kasane Ted** (重音テッド) is a derivative fan-made character originating from Kasane Teto. The name also functions as an affectionate moniker for Kasane Teto’s voice library when her vocal output is shifted into a masculine register using UTAU’s "Gender Factor" engine parameter.

---

## Overview

Kasane Ted was created by **Sakururi** (さくるり), also known as **Ted P** (who originally used the handle *Kanjuku Pine* [Ripe Pineapple] when the character first debuted).

He made his first appearance on October 4, 2008, in the video:  
**"Had Kasane Teto (g+30) sing Kichiku Megane"** (*重音テト（g+30）に鬼畜眼鏡を歌ってもらった* — *now deleted*).

In this video, Kasane Teto’s UTAU voicebank was adjusted with a Gender Factor flag of `g+30` to produce a deep, masculine singing voice, covering the song **"under the darkness"** (composed by C.G mix), the opening theme of Spray's 18+ Boys' Love visual novel *Kichiku Megane* (*Sadistic Glasses*).

*(At the time in October 2008, this song was considered an essential "mandatory test-piece" for male virtual vocalists, frequently covered by KAITO, Gackpoid, gender-bent Vocaloids such as MEITO and Hatsune Mikuo, and early male UTAU voicebanks).*

The creator retained Teto’s original 2channel *anka* lore designating her as **"Gender: Chimera."** However, her signature "drill twin-tails" were adapted into a single drill-shaped ponytail, her rounded, droopy eyes were sharpened into confident, upturned eyes (*tsurime*), and she was reimagined as a male character wearing a business suit and glasses in homage to the visual novel's protagonist. The author bestowed this creation with the tongue-in-cheek title: **"Chimera Second Form: Kasane Ted."**

The concept struck a chord with viewers, leading to a steady wave of videos utilizing the `g+30` Teto voicebank under the persona of "Kasane Ted." Setting the parameter to `g+20` produced a more youthful vocal texture, prompting some producers to refer to that variant as **"Boy Ted"** (*Shōnen Ted*).

Ted's arrival served as the catalyst for the broader phenomenon of **"g-change characters"** (*g-kae kyara*) across the UTAU community—a practice where creators altered an existing bank's Gender Factor to manifest an entirely new derivative character with an inverted gender identity (e.g., Nagone Mako → Nagone Makoto). Kasane Ted was the undisputed trailblazer of this UTAU gender-swap subculture.

For years, Ted remained an unofficial fan creation. However, on **October 10, 2011**, an official profile page for Kasane Ted was published on the official Kasane Teto website, establishing him as a fully recognized character in both name and standing.  
Simultaneously, it was announced that **TwinDrill**, the official management circle behind Kasane Teto, would serve as the formal contact and licensing desk for Kasane Ted. On October 21 of that year, official character guidelines were published, derivative submissions to Crypton’s *Piapro* platform were officially approved, and Ted’s very first official character artwork was unveiled.

### Subsequent Milestones

- **April 27, 2023:** AHS released **"Synthesizer V AI Kasane Teto."** Following this launch, creators began generating Kasane Ted singing videos powered by Synthesizer V's modern vocal synthesis engine.
- **March 14, 2024:** Ted's name was officially approved for direct artist registration on JOYSOUND’s *Utasuki Music Post* karaoke delivery service.
- **February 2015:** Sakururi withdrew from Nico Nico Douga, deleting the original ground-zero video alongside their personal Teto and Ted uploads. Despite this, a wealth of covers of "under the darkness" and original songs written specifically for Kasane Ted continue to be actively published by other community producers.

## Kasane Ted on Nico Nico Douga

Because Kasane Ted was born organically as a fan-created derivative, he possesses no single, rigid canon profile. Consequently, his portrayal varies depending on the video animator, producer, or illustrator:

- **Age:** Frequently depicted as either **31 years old** (matching Teto) or **61 years old** (derived humorously from 31 + g30 = 61).
- **"Is Teto a girl?" → "No, she's a Chimera."** *(A famous line from the original debut video).* Because of this, fans sometimes describe Ted not as a literal biological sex-change, but rather as her "Chimera Second Form."
- **Separate Existence:** Ted regularly duets alongside Teto, and has firmly established an identity distinct from her. Many fan works treat them explicitly as two separate individuals.
- **Gender-Swapped Ensemble:** He frequently co-stars alongside gender-bent Vocaloid counterparts (such as Hatsune Mikuo).
- **Nicknames:** Commonly referred to as **"Pony-Drill"** (*Ponidoriru*).
- **Glasses & Wardrobe:** True to his debut video, he is frequently depicted wearing spectacles. In his official artwork, however, he does not wear them on his face, but keeps them neatly folded in his suit jacket's breast pocket.
- **Military Attire:** While his official artwork features a standard business suit, community illustrators frequently depict him in stylized military or naval officer uniforms, featuring multiple color schemes. Following the release of *Synthesizer V Kasane Teto*, the diversity of these uniform designs expanded even further.
- **Character Numbering:** Because he lacks a commercial software box, he has no official numeric code on his shoulder. Most fans assign him **"0401"** (April 1st, reflecting his roots as Teto's counterpart). Others use **"1004"** (commemorating his Niconico debut on October 4).
- **Relationship Dynamics:** Depending on the creator's vision, Ted is alternatively depicted as Teto's older brother, her twin brother, or her romantic partner. When portrayed as lovers, the pairing is affectionately nicknamed the **"Self-Sufficient Couple"** (*Jikyū Jisoku Kappuru*—because a single vocal library supplies both parts of the duet).
- **Personality Shift:** Early works portrayed him as a suave, cool, and collected gentleman. Over time, however, he was increasingly relegated to the role of comic relief—a goofy, unpolished third-wheel (*sanmaime*) who frequently bears the brunt of physical comedy.
- **Honorary VIPPALOID:** Although he did not originate directly from 2channel's VIP board, he is universally treated as an honorary member of the **VIPPALOID** family.
- **Voice Classification:** Videos featuring the `g+20` pitch flag often carry the dedicated tag **"Boy Ted"** (*少年テッド*).
- **Archival Art:** The original illustration used in the debut video was hosted on Sakururi’s Pixiv account for a time, and went on to appear in celebrated community videos such as the UTAU cover of *"Hoshi no Kakera"* and an ensemble choral cover of doriko’s *"Cendrillon."*

## Cultural Context & Slang Guide: Kasane Ted

### 1. The Gender Factor (`g` flag) in UTAU & Vocaloid
In singing synthesis engines (Vocaloid, UTAU, Synthesizer V), the **Gender Factor** (commonly abbreviated as the **`g` flag**) manipulates the formant frequencies of a recorded voice. 
- Lowering the formant (`g-`) makes the voice sound brighter, smaller, and more childlike.
- Raising the formant (`g+`) broadens the simulated vocal tract, making a female voice sound deeper, heavier, and masculine without shifting the underlying musical pitch.

When early UTAU users realized they could shift Kasane Teto's bright, slightly nasal voice up by `g+30`, it produced an surprisingly natural, resonant baritone.

### 2. "Gender-Bent VOCALOIDs" (性転換VOCALOID)
In 2007–2008, before Crypton released official male vocals like KAITO into widespread prominence or Gackpoid hit the scene, the Vocaloid community satisfied the demand for male vocals through **gender-bending** (*seitenkan*):
- **Hatsune Mikuo (初音ミクオ):** Formant-shifted male counterpart to Hatsune Miku.
- **MEITO:** Formant-shifted male counterpart to MEIKO.
- **Kagamine Rinto / Lenka:** Gender-swaps of Rin and Len.

When Kasane Ted emerged in late 2008, he became the UTAU equivalent of Mikuo—the definitive male counterpart to the community's favorite virtual diva.

### 3. "under the darkness" and the "KAITO Mandatory Test-Piece"
*Under the darkness* by C.G mix was the theme song of the 2007 BL visual novel *Kichiku Megane* (*Sadistic Glasses*). In 2007–2008 Niconico culture, this song was celebrated as an essential rite of passage for virtual male vocalists. Because of its dark techno beat, sultry vocals, and melodramatic tone, users declared that any new male vocal bank had to prove its worth by covering this track. Debuting Kasane Ted with this exact song was a deliberate, pitch-perfect statement that Ted was stepping up to challenge the male vocal pantheon.

### 4. "Self-Sufficient Couple" (自給自足カップル - Jikyū Jisoku Kappuru)
Japanese internet slang used when an artist or producer creates a romantic pairing using derivative versions of the **same core source**. In Vocaloid/UTAU culture, pairing Kasane Teto with Kasane Ted is the textbook definition of a "self-sufficient couple": the music creator can produce a full male-female romantic duet using literally one free software voice library, simply by altering the formant parameters on the vocal tracks.

### 5. "Sanmaime" (三枚目) & "Yogore" (汚れ)
In traditional Japanese kabuki theater:
- *Ichimaime* (1st billboard) is the handsome male lead (*ikemen*).
- *Nimaime* (2nd billboard) is the suave romantic rival.
- *Sanmaime* (3rd billboard) is the comic relief.

Calling Kasane Ted a **sanmaime** or **yogore** (someone who takes the dirty, humiliating comedic roles) means that while he was designed to look like a sharp, handsome anime protagonist in a suit, the community affectionately uses him as the butt of jokes in MMD comedies—often getting slapped, ignored by girls, or panicking in ridiculous slapstick sketches.
