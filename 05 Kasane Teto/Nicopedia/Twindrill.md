**Kasane Teto Official Circle "TwinDrill"** (重音テト公式サークル『ツインドリル』) is the non-profit organization responsible for managing the intellectual property rights, licensing, and community guidelines surrounding Kasane Teto.

---

## Overview

The official Kasane Teto circle **"TwinDrill"** (hereafter *TwinDrill*) is a community-driven circle established to support Teto users, broaden her creative opportunities, and ensure she remains a cherished virtual character.  
Its formal registered title is **Kasane Teto Official Circle "TwinDrill"** (often abbreviated as **TD**).

The organization is anchored by the character's primary copyright holders: original visual designer **Sen** (線) and voice provider **Mayo Oyamano** (小山乃舞世), supported by a team of dedicated volunteers.

TwinDrill’s core operational responsibilities include:

- Free public hosting and distribution of the *Kasane Teto Voice Library*.
- Enactment, drafting, and updating of character usage guidelines.
- Formal corporate liaison and partnership with **Crypton Future Media, Inc.** (hereafter *Crypton*).
- Operational window for **One-Day Copyright Licensing** (*Tōjitsu Hanken*) applications at hobby conventions.
- Rights and trademark management for derivative character **Kasane Ted**.
- Hosting the **"Teto Tea Party"** (*Teto Chakai*), a monthly community text-chat held every second Wednesday.

*(Note: Technical development and audio engineering for the voicebanks are managed by an external working group: the **Kasane Teto Soundbank Team**, led directly by Mayo Oyamano).*

## Origins & Establishment of TwinDrill

Kasane Teto was originally conceived as an April Fool's hoax by anonymous users on 2channel’s VIP board. Once the joke was revealed on April 1, 2008, volunteers from the thread banded together with the original hoax voice provider to release her audio samples for the freeware synthesizer **UTAU**, transforming Teto into a functional singing character.

As countless creators published songs and videos with her, Teto’s popularity exploded. In April 2009, her signature song **"The Song of the Robot with Ears"** (*Mimi no Aru Robotto no Uta*) was selected for official commercial karaoke distribution on the JOYSOUND network.  
However, this milestone brought an unprecedented legal dilemma to light.

Because Kasane Teto had been created through crowdsourced anonymous board prompts (*anka*) parodying Crypton’s commercial *Character Vocal Series*, **nobody legally knew who owned the character's intellectual property.**  
Under Japanese copyright law, without a clearly defined corporate entity or legal author, third-party companies could not legally clear the rights to use the name "Kasane Teto" for commercial purposes—including karaoke indexing.

Up to that point, the community had operated purely on an honor system governed by unwritten community goodwill. But as Teto’s mainstream profile grew, relying on vague etiquette was no longer sustainable; formal, legally sound written terms became an urgent necessity.

Volunteers from the original 2channel production thread convened to untangle the legal knot. Legal review confirmed that intellectual property rights resided strictly with the two direct human creators:
1. **Sen**, who drew the original character design (translating text prompts into tangible art).
2. **Mayo Oyamano**, who recorded the vocal samples.

Both creators expressed a clear desire: rather than monopolizing control, they wished to manage Teto cooperatively alongside trusted volunteer community members to protect her legacy.

Thus, on **July 23, 2009**, **Kasane Teto Official Circle TwinDrill** was formally established.  
TwinDrill immediately initiated friendly dialogue with Crypton Future Media. In parallel, they drafted comprehensive usage guidelines and held an open community contest to redesign Teto’s official logo (removing direct stylistic homages that infringed on Crypton's trademarked commercial branding).

On **April 1, 2010**—exactly two years after her hoax debut—a breakthrough occurred: Crypton agreed to apply its **Piapro Character License (PCL)** framework to Kasane Teto, with Crypton acting as her formal corporate agent for commercial licensing. This historic agreement opened the doors to mainstream commercialization: major CD album releases, retail merchandise, scale figures, and video game appearances.

To this day, the members of TwinDrill continue to meet regularly to address community inquiries, oversee software ports, and protect Teto's creative ecosystem.

## Trivia & Tidbits

- **Legendary Mentors:** When TwinDrill was first established in 2009, two prominent early Vocaloid community designers served as formal advisors: **Smith Hioka** (creator of Akita Neru) and **CAFFEIN** (creator of Yowane Haku).
- **VIPPALOID Independence:** TwinDrill is strictly dedicated to Kasane Teto and Kasane Ted; it holds no administrative jurisdiction over rights bodies for other 2channel VIPPALOID characters (such as Yokune Ruko or Sukone Tei).
- **The Original Thread OP:** The anonymous user (`>>1`) who created the very first 2channel April Fool's thread back in 2008 has stepped back from administration and quietly watches over Teto's modern successes from afar.
- **Core Sound Engineers:** The *Kasane Teto Soundbank Team* includes prominent UTAU tuning masters such as **Maru@Loop** and **Yurahon'ya P**.
- **Global Outreach:** In addition to the soundbank team, TwinDrill maintains an external **English Translation Department** (*Eiyaku-bu*), responsible for translating notices and subtitles for the official Kasane Teto YouTube channel.
- **Volunteer Call:** Both the Soundbank Team and the Translation Department actively recruit volunteer community staff as needs arise.
- **First Major-Label Album:** On October 10, 2012, Teto’s first commercially distributed compilation album, ***0401 - The Best Days of Kasane Teto***, was released under the full creative production of TwinDrill.
- **Sapporo Snow Festival:** In February 2013, TwinDrill participated in the 64th Sapporo Snow Festival (Citizen's Sculpture Division), constructing public snow sculptures of **Kasane Teto** and fellow UTAU singer **Sekka Yufu**.

## Cultural Context & Slang Guide: TwinDrill

### 1. The Legal Paradox of 2channel Creations
When an internet meme is born on an anonymous forum like 2channel, it is typically produced by hundreds of faceless users contributing ideas in real time. Under traditional copyright law, this creates a legal nightmare: **who owns the rights?**
- If a character has no clear owner, legitimate companies (record labels, karaoke providers, game publishers like Sega) cannot touch it without risking lawsuits from anonymous contributors.
- If anyone can claim ownership, a bad actor could hijack the character and monetize it maliciously.

TwinDrill solved this dilemma through brilliant legal pragmatism: they established that under copyright statutes, abstract ideas (*anka* suggestions) are not copyrightable, but the **concrete visual execution** (drawn by Sen) and the **recorded sound files** (recorded by Mayo Oyamano) are legally protectable assets. By forming a non-profit circle around Sen and Mayo, they established a legitimate legal shield for the entire community.

### 2. Crypton, the Piapro Character License (PCL), and Piapro
**Crypton Future Media** (the creators of Hatsune Miku) could easily have sued the original creators of Kasane Teto for trademark infringement and deceptive marketing when the April Fool's hoax launched in 2008. Instead, Crypton took the exact opposite path:
- They recognized Teto as a testament to the passionate creative culture inspired by Vocaloid.
- In 2010, Crypton welcomed Teto into their **Piapro Character License (PCL)** system—a permissive copyright framework similar to Creative Commons that permits amateur creators to create fan art, doujinshi, and indie music without fear of copyright strikes.
- Crypton also agreed to serve as the official licensing bridge between TwinDrill and commercial corporations, allowing Teto to appear in commercial video games like Sega’s *Hatsune Miku: Project DIVA* alongside Miku, Rin, Len, and Luka.

### 3. "One-Day Copyright" (当日版権 - Tōjitsu Hanken)
In Japanese hobby culture, major events like **Wonder Festival** (*WonFes*) utilize a unique system called **One-Day Copyright**. Under this system, major IP holders grant amateur garage-kit sculptors legal permission to sell physical resin figures of copyrighted characters *exclusively on that single day at the convention venue*. 

TwinDrill maintains a dedicated administrative desk specifically to review and approve One-Day Copyright requests, allowing amateur sculptors around the world to legally manufacture and sell Kasane Teto garage kits.

### 4. Smith Hioka and CAFFEIN as Advisors
When TwinDrill was establishing its foundational legal framework in 2009, they turned to veterans who had already walked the path of turning fan-made Vocaloid memes into recognized icons:
- **Smith Hioka (スミス・ヒオカ):** Creator of **Akita Neru**, the yellow-haired tsundere phone addict created to parody forum flame-wars.
- **CAFFEIN:** Creator of **Yowane Haku**, the white-haired, drunken "Voyakiloid" created to represent frustrated users who gave up on trying to tune Vocaloid software.

Their advisory presence ensured that Kasane Teto followed the same healthy, sustainable path from internet shitpost to enduring cultural icon.
