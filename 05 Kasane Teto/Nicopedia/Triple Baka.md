**"Triple Baka"** (*とりぷるばか*) is the subtitle and popular community name for the original Hatsune Miku and Kasane Teto song **"Toriba ~Triple Baka~"** (驫麤～とりぷるばか～), composed by **LamazeP**.

Because the kanji characters in the primary title **"驫麤"** are extremely rare and difficult to type using standard Japanese keyboard inputs, users overwhelmingly utilize the hiragana tag **"とりぷるばか"** (*Triple Baka*) instead.

*(Embedded Content)*

Following the massive popularity of the original music video, this tag is also broadly applied to derivative videos featuring the comedy trio of **Hatsune Miku, Akita Neru, and Kasane Teto**.

## Overview

### About the Lyrics

The lyrics depict the everyday struggles, creative slumps, failures, and comebacks of an amateur content creator.

The following creator notes were included in the official lyric text file packaged within the author's distributed ZIP archive. Because these topics were frequently asked in the video comments, they are cited below:

> *"During Teto's part, the final 'Tetoteteto' is actually sung as **'Te-to-pe-te-so(n)'**.*  
> *The final 'Ryuuuuuu' vocal sound is genuinely just 'Ryu'.*  
> *The hourglass in the animation was drawn to signify 'completely running out of creative motivation in three minutes' (though I think the other interpretations posted in the comments actually fit much better!).*  
> *The first verse is about staying up late into the night working on a creative project, only to be struck by exhaustion and oversleep. Since you're already hopelessly late for school or work anyway, you just stay home and keep working.*  
> *The second verse depicts the lifestyle of a creator who takes pride in throwing together a rushed, slapdash video. He isn't fully satisfied with it, but he uploads it anyway. He keeps creating because everyone is waiting for his next work. That's the story."*

### About the Music

The full release is not merely an extended loop with a second verse tacked on; the underlying melody is subtly varied between the first and second halves.

Even in the initial short version, Kasane Teto’s backing chorus received immense praise. In the full version, she was given an extended solo bridge that proved wildly addictive (the video itself earned the tag *"Stupendous Addictiveness"*). This breakout performance foreshadowed LamazeP’s eventual status as a certified "Legendary Teto Master" (*Teto Masterpiece Producer*).

### Karaoke Availability

The song is officially indexed on major Japanese karaoke systems:
- **DAM:** Request Code `7601-65`
- **UGA:** Request Code `7901-16`

### About the Music Video (PV)

The hand-drawn music video features a distinct ensemble dynamic: **Hatsune Miku** handles lead vocals, **Akita Neru** provides electronic sound effects (tapping away on her mobile phone), and **Kasane Teto** contributes backing chants and chorus lines. (Miku and Teto's synthesized voices provide the actual vocal audio for their respective parts).  
The animation—where each character bounces and mouths along with their lyrics or sound effects whenever their track plays—was met with universal acclaim.

The full-length video includes a sequence in the second half showing LamazeP's digital drawing process captured at 2x speed.

### About the Nicoscript Comments

When the video was posted, LamazeP made extensive use of **Nicoscript** (Niconico's uploader scripting tool). Through these scripts, user comments were automatically converted into a synchronized wall of **`(^ω^) おっおっおっ`** (*"Ot-ot-ot!"*) emotes. While the script was later updated to allow regular comments, the historic barrage remains preserved.

Additionally, during special seasonal milestones, the uploader periodically added special script effects:
- **October 2008:** Special commemorative comment triggers.
- **April 1, 2009 (Kasane Teto’s Birthday):** As a tribute to "Teto-neesan," an overwhelming flood of celebratory Triple Baka comments was triggered across the screen.

## History & Milestones

### Short Version
- **June 28, 2008:** The short version of *"Toriba ~Triple Baka~"* is posted. It ranks #6 on the Daily VOCALOID Ranking (#139). On the revamped *Weekly VOCALOID Ranking #39*, it lands in 3rd place and is selected as the week's featured pickup work. After viewers noted that the white background made white comments illegible, comment colors were tinted via uploader script.

### Full Version
- **July 13, 2008:** The full version is officially uploaded. It achieves #7 on the Daily ranking (#154) and #2 on the Weekly chart (#41). On its launch day, an automated script that shifted comment colors accidentally caused over 500 comments to be dropped from the system counter; the color filter was promptly removed, leaving only the midway redirect command.
- At the time of upload, searching the tag "Kasane Teto" returned exactly 100 videos across the entire platform. The video was celebrated with the tag:  
  **"☆ Congratulations ☆ 100th Upload with the Kasane Teto Tag."**
- **August 30, 2008:** Included as Track 5 on the commemorative album *VOCALOMANIA SPEED* for Hatsune Miku's first anniversary. LamazeP reportedly experienced imposter syndrome and was quite overwhelmed upon finding his song included alongside industry titans.
- **November 6, 2008:** View count hovers around 80,000 views. However, once Roland’s full-motion animated PV was completed and entered the rankings, views accelerated exponentially.
- **November 10, 2008, 05:41 JST:** Surpasses 100,000 views, entering the **VOCALOID Hall of Fame** (*Dendō-iri*). The **UTAU Hall of Fame** tag is awarded simultaneously.

> *"My very first thought upon hitting 100,000 views:  
> 'Hey, me from last year... are you watching this? I've managed to bring Miku and the girls all the way to this stage...'"*  
> — *LamazeP, updated video description upon reaching the Hall of Fame*

- Approximately 30 minutes later (06:20 JST), LamazeP uploads a commemorative song titled: *"[First Hall of Fame] A Message of Gratitude to You All."*
- **December 16, 2008:** Spurred on by the viral "OK Series" remixes, the track surges back up to 14th place on *Weekly VOCALOID Ranking #63*. At 00:50 that night, Roland's animated PV surpasses 100,000 views, entering the **VOCALOID-PV Hall of Fame**.
- **January 29, 2009, 23:25 JST:** Surpasses 200,000 views.
- **May 4, 2020, 15:24 JST:** Surpasses **1,000,000 views**, officially earning **VOCALOID Mythical Status** (*Shinwa-iri* / Legend status).

## Cultural Context & Slang Guide: Triple Baka

### 1. The Title Kanji Riddle: What Does "驫麤" Mean?
The official Japanese title of the song is **驫麤～とりぷるばか～** (*Toriba ~Triple Baka~*). 
The two kanji chosen by LamazeP are visual puns based on the Japanese word for idiot, **馬鹿** (*Baka*—literally "Horse + Deer"):
- **驫** (*hyō* / *todoroki*): A rare kanji composed of **three horses** (馬).
- **麤** (*so* / *baka*): An archaic kanji composed of **three deer** (鹿).

Stacked triplicate kanji characters in Japanese typography are known as *shinji-yō* (品字様). Putting "three horses" together with "three deer" literally spells out **Triple Horse-Deer** → **Triple Baka**! 
Because almost no standard Japanese keyboard IME can convert these archaic characters easily without tedious Unicode lookup, fans gave up and searched using the phonetic hiragana tag: **とりぷるばか** (*Triple Baka*).

### 2. The Trio: Why Miku, Neru, and Teto?
In the early days of Vocaloid (2007–2008), the community organically formed a beloved comedy trio consisting of:
1. **Hatsune Miku:** The earnest, slightly airheaded green-haired diva (official commercial VOCALOID).
2. **Akita Neru:** The cynical, yellow-haired tsundere who refuses to sing and spends all day browsing forums and sending flame comments on her yellow flip-phone (Vocaloid derivative / fanloid). In *Triple Baka*, Neru does not sing; she rhythmically taps her cell phone keys to produce electronic 8-bit sound effects.
3. **Kasane Teto:** The energetic red-haired drill-pigtail chimera (2channel VIP April Fool's hoax turned UTAU singer).

Together, they represent the three distinct pillars of 2008 Niconico vocal synthesis culture: official software, community fan creations, and grassroots freeware.

### 3. Teto's Secret Lyric: "Te-to-pe-te-so(n)"
In LamazeP's liner notes quoted in the article, he reveals that during Teto's rapid-fire vocal breakdown, she isn't just babbling "teto-teto-teto"—she is chanting **"Te-to-pe-te-son!"**  
This is a direct tribute to the NHK *Minna no Uta* song ***Tetopepetenson***, which was the original 2channel *anka* namesake that gave Kasane Teto her name back on March 30, 2008.

### 4. Nicoscript (ニコス) and the `(^ω^) おっおっおっ` Meme
**Nicoscript** was a specialized command framework provided by Dwango that allowed video uploaders to insert hidden interactive commands directly into the video timeline. Creators could:
- Force regular user comments to change font color or size.
- Automatically redirect viewers to another video when playback finished.
- Replace specific user keystrokes with pre-set ASCII art.

LamazeP's signature online avatar is the smug cat-like text emoticon **`(^ω^)`**. In *Triple Baka*, he set a script that turned user comments into an unending, bouncing wall of **`(^ω^) おっおっおっ`** (*"Ot-ot-ot!"*), mimicking the infectious, bobbing rhythm of the animation.

### 5. Niconico View Count Milestones
The Vocaloid community on Niconico tracks view counts with prestigious ceremonial titles:
- **VOCALOID Hall of Fame (VOCALOID殿堂入り - Dendō-iri):** Awarded upon crossing **100,000 views**.
- **VOCALOID Legend / Mythical Status (VOCALOID神話入り - Shinwa-iri):** Awarded upon crossing **1,000,000 views**.  
*Triple Baka* achieved Hall of Fame status in late 2008 and crossed into Mythical status in May 2020.

### 6. The "OK Series" (OKシリーズ)
In the middle of the song, Miku stops singing and Teto launches into an upbeat, repetitive solo bridge ending with an emphatic declaration:  
> **"...Teto! OK!"**

The delivery was so charming that Niconico creators began cutting out this 10-second vocal loop and turning it into an entire standalone genre of looping dance remixes, known across Niconico as the **"OK Series."**
