# LOLI.COM

- [[#Overview]]
- [[#Related Articles]]

**Terada Mamo** (寺田まも) is a creator on Niconico Douga.  
He previously operated under the name **LOLI.COM**.  
His nicknames include Moroboshi Tou-tou, among others.

---

## Overview

**Terada Mamo** is the originator of the legendary **"Mirishira Series"** (dubbing anime without knowing a single millimeter about the plot).

He possesses an astonishingly versatile vocal range, capable of voicing male roles, female roles, foreigners, middle-aged men, and elderly grandmothers all by himself.  
His favorite character is Akira Kogami from *Lucky Star*.  
He is also the creator of "Mariah," widely known as "Ura-Melt" (Reverse Melt).  
Additionally, he pioneered a brand-new subgenre of live commentary where he gives real-time running reactions to anime while knowing absolutely nothing about what he is watching.

While his roots lie primarily in rap arrangements within the "Utattemita" (Singing) category (Nico-rap), he is a true multi-talented creative who handles singing, songwriting, musical composition, and computer programming entirely on his own—producing original Hatsune Miku tracks, mashups, and remixes.

His broad spectrum of creative antics includes distributing the "new model vocaloid" **Rorine Com** (conceived as a tongue-in-cheek rival to VOCALOID2 *Hatsune Miku*), as well as delivering rapid-fire radio-DJ-style monologues during anime commentary.  
His singing prowess, songwriting, and comedic lyric parodies (*kaeuta*) are widely acclaimed, earning him a passionate fan following.  
Conversely, his artistic ability in drawing and visual arts is notoriously non-existent.

Later in his career, he transitioned into a professional commercial J-POP songwriter and composer, providing tracks to artists such as Flower, Supernova (超新星), Gekidan EXILE, and Yuya Matsushita, before announcing his retirement in 2014.  
He currently goes by the moniker **Terada Mamo** and has uploaded a track titled *"At Least I'll Live Until 95, Zon"* (少なくとも９５歳まで生きるゾン).

- **Catchphrases:** *"Isn't that neat~!"* (いいじゃん～！), *"Now that's youth for ya~!"* (青春じゃん～！)
- **Surprise Reaction:** Overdramatically shouts *"Eeehhhh?!!"* at the top of his lungs.
- **Vocal Tic / Call:** *"Yansu"* (ヤンス)

In his video *"Tried Narrating Nico Nico Douga Ryuuseigun in a Radio DJ Style"*, his impeccable, breathless rapid-fire banter completely captivated listeners, earning him the community title **"The Strongest Machine Gun in Niconico History."**

- On July 8, 2009, he participated in the compilation album *LOiD-01 -LOiD's LOGiC-* released by the vocaloid label LOiD.
- On October 10, 2012, he released the album *Com-Nama CD* through For Life Music Entertainment. On the exact same day, the compilation album *No-iD.* featuring his work was also released.

## Cultural Context & Japanese Internet Slang Notes

### 1. The Mirishira Series (ミリしらシリーズ)
"Mirishira" is a contraction of **"1ミリも知らない"** (*ichi-miri mo shiranai*), meaning *"I don't know a single millimeter [the slightest bit] about this."*  
Created by LOLI.COM, this became one of the most celebrated comedic video genres on Niconico Douga. The creator takes an episode of an anime they have never watched, strips the audio completely, and improvises their own voice acting, sound effects, character names, and absurd narrative twists on the fly. 
Because LOLI.COM had incredible vocal range (jumping between old men, young girls, villains, and narrators in a single breath), his *Mirishira* dubs became viral sensations.

### 2. Jinriki VOCALOID (人力VOCALOID) and UTAU's Missing Link
*Jinriki* means "human-powered." Before consumer voice synthesis tools like UTAU existed, internet users who wanted characters other than official Vocaloids to sing had to manually chop spoken dialogue into tiny WAV syllables and pitch-shift them by hand inside digital audio editors.  
LOLI.COM took this concept and turned it into an April-Fools-style gag in late 2007: he recorded hundreds of individual syllables in his own voice across several octaves and handed them out as "Free Hatsune Miku." Because manually sequencing hundreds of raw WAV files on an audio track was mind-numbing labor, **Ameya-P** coded an automated cutting-and-pasting utility specifically to make working with LOLI.COM's voicepack manageable. Ameya-P continually upgraded this utility until it evolved into a fully fledged, standalone voice synthesis engine: **UTAU**.

### 3. "Victims of LOLI.COM" (LOLI.COM被害者の会)
A running community joke tag. Before Ameya-P built software to automate the process, anyone who took LOLI.COM's challenge to make a song using his raw audio files had to endure dozens of hours of manual audio editing. 
Uploaders who survived this grueling labor were jokingly commemorated with this tag.

### 4. Kugyuroid (くぎゅロイド)
"Kugyu" is the famous affectionate fan nickname for renowned anime voice actress Rie Kugimiya (known as the "Queen of Tsundere" for voicing characters like Taiga in *Toradora!* and Shana in *Shakugan no Shana*). 
In early 2008, creators sampled her spoken anime lines to build experimental Jinriki voicebanks dubbed "Kugyuroid," which Ameya-P used alongside Rorine Com to test the early UTAU engine.

### 5. AquesTalk and Defoko (デフォ子)
AquesTalk is a very lightweight speech synthesis engine developed by AQUEST Corporation (famous as the robotic voice used for *Yukkuri* commentary). 
When UTAU was released in March 2008, Ameya-P bundled a default voicebank synthesized using AquesTalk so users would have a voice ready out-of-the-box. 
The community affectionately nicknamed this bundled character **Defoko** (literally "Default Girl," formally named Utane Uta).

### 6. Nico-rap (ニコラップ)
A vibrant underground hip-hop subculture on Niconico Douga. LOLI.COM was a prominent figure alongside other legendary internet rappers like Tightson (タイツォン). They frequently uploaded original rap tracks, cyphers, and collaborative rap remixes of Vocaloid anthems.

### 7. "Ura-Melt" (裏メルト) / Mariah
supercell's 2007 Vocaloid anthem *"Melt"* (sung by Hatsune Miku) depicts an innocent, heart-pounding romantic outing from a shy girl's perspective. 
LOLI.COM produced a famous answer-song/parody rap titled "Mariah" (dubbed *Ura-Melt*, or "Melt from the Other Side"), delivering a gritty, humorous, and irreverent male perspective on the same date.

### 8. Nico Nico Douga Ryuuseigun (ニコニコ動画流星群)
*Nico Nico Douga Ryuuseigun* ("Supernova") was an iconic 2008 mega-popular musical medley compiled by Shimadoriru, stringing together dozens of viral anime themes, game tracks, and Vocaloid songs into a single continuous track. 
LOLI.COM's upload where he spoke over the fast-paced medley as a hyperactive, fast-talking FM radio DJ went viral, earning him the badge "The Strongest Machine Gun in Niconico History."

### 9. "Yansu" (ヤンス)
A stereotypical verbal tic (*gobi*) historically assigned in Japanese manga and anime to comical, brown-nosing lackeys, sidekicks, or mischievous characters. LOLI.COM adopted it as part of his internet persona and vocal gags.

### 10. The LOiD Label
LOiD was an early commercial record label established under Bouncy Records around 2009. It was one of the first mainstream labels dedicated specifically to signing Niconico-based Vocaloid producers, independent electronic musicians, and internet vocalists.
