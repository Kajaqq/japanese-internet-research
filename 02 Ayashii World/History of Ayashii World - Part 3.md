# A History of Ayashii World Part 3

Continuation of [[History of Ayashii World - Part 2]]
## 2003

**2003.01.05**
Ayashii World@Honten shelter (@本店退避), so-called Neko-ita (猫板, the cat board), started on a home server. Its weak point was that hardly anybody knew about it.

**2003.01.08**
Gashin Shoutan ヽ(\`Д´) @Takken and qualifications squad Strange Legal Mind (臥薪嘗胆 ヽ(\`Д´) ＠宅建・資格班 Strange Legal Mind) started.

> [!note]
> Gashin Shoutan (臥薪嘗胆, "sleeping on brushwood and licking gall") is an idiom from Chinese history meaning to endure hardship for the sake of a future goal. Takken (宅建) is the Japanese real estate transaction agent licence, a famously tough exam, so this was a board for people studying for qualification exams.

**2003.01.13**
Mr Sayaka (さやか), who also wrote the Ayashii name dictionary (あやしい人名辞典), released treeview.cgi, which shows the Kuzuha script in a tree view. Later he improved it, co-developed it with Mr Namaire (生入), and it was implemented at @Zantei. On 2003.09.24 the development base moved to http://saxyaka.dyndns.org/.

**2003.01.24**
Because all of the @Zantei administrators had disappeared, @Zantei (Zantei shelter, 暫定退避) was born. The administrator was Mr Namaire. The BBS implemented a lot of new functions which Ayashii World had never had before (tree view, trip, etc.), and actively did media-mix using net radio; its novel administration was not bound by the conventions of Ayashii World. Later the original @Zantei revived, but most of the users chose Mr Namaire's administration. Even though it called itself a shelter, it had far more users than the original.

> [!note]
> A trip, or tripcode (トリップ), is a hash generated from a secret password typed after the name, so that a user can prove they are the same person without registering. It is well-known from 2ch and futaba, but it was new to Ayashii World BBS at the time, where Handle Names could be faked freely.

**2003.01.30**
Mr Utada Raper appeared for the first time in half a year and officially announced that he withdrew from the administration of @Zantei. He also said he would leave Ayashii World itself. It is really regrettable because he was one of the representative figures of Ayashii World.

**2003.02.21**
Metropolitan Museum (めとろぽりたん美術館) started. The administrator was Mr Tamago (たまご), who also ran an uploader and so on. It was the actual successor of Smithsonian Museum, but because it was founded by Zantei users, there were no Honten topics at all. In 2003.07 the Louvre Museum (るーぶる美術館), a Mirai stream one, also opened, but it was short-lived and ended after a month.

> [!note]
> All of these log-summary websites were named after real museums: the Smithsonian, the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Louvre, and later "MOMA" (2003.10.26).

**2003.03.11**
Ayashii World@Kusanone (@草の根), which supported Telnet connections, started.

> [!note]
> Kusanone means grass-roots, a reference to the Kusanone BBS of the Pasokon-Tsushin era (see Part 1, 1995.08.15), which users also accessed through a terminal rather than a browser.

**2003.03.12**
Honten Magazine (本店マガジン) was first published. On 07.02 of the same year it was archived as "Honten Magazine Publishing".

**2003.03.20**
Ayashii World Tamashii (あやしいわーるど魂, later Z) started. Users who were dissatisfied with Mr Namaire's administration moved to it, and it produced the Ayachuu (あやちゅう) memes which have continued endlessly on Remix and other places. The administrator was Mr Shiganai (しがない).

**2003.04.06**
A Remix-like Thing@arena (Remix状の物＠arena) started as a plugin for the @Zantei shelter. The administrator was Mr Namaire.

**2003.04.26**
A place for piling the logs of @ds-Honten was born. It was run by the administrator of the @DDNS server, which also had an uploader and a shelter BBS.

**2003.04.28**
Someone exploited a server-side bug, and logs with IP Address leaked at @Mirai.

**2003.04.29**
Ayashii World@Jonathan the Seagull (@かもめのじょなさん) started, derived from a monologue board on T-CUP. Inspired by the Makkochou Dictionary, it also had a dictionary of terms and people of the Ayashii World. The administrator was Mr Midorisan (緑三).

> [!note]
> Kamome no Jonasan (かもめのジョナサン) is the Japanese title of Richard Bach's novel Jonathan Livingston Seagull. Mr Midorisan is the same person as Soudan-kun from the Soudan-kun incident (Part 2, 2000.04.11).

**2003.05.12**
Honten Uploader@Toro (ほんてんあぷろだ＠とろ) started as an uploader for the Honten stream.

**2003.05.17**
The original @Zantei revived for only one day by Mr Gesu Nuts. Mr Namaire closed his BBS once, but persistent random-string trolling stopped the original again. In the end, Namaire's Zantei continued to run as a shelter.

**2003.06.01**
Ayashii World@Honten (shelter) (あやしいわーるど＠本店(退避)) started by Mr Okayu (ォヵュ). He also ran a Honten mailing list and so on.

> [!note]
> ォヵュ is "okayu" (rice porridge) written with small kana, the same kind of "gyaru-moji" style as ぁゃιぃ for あやしい.

**2003.06.02**
To counter load attacks which abused the past log search, Mr Machi no Yousei completely outsourced the log search function of @Mirai to external servers. The responsibility was taken by "futurelog" (run by Mr Maronii, マロニー), which had worked as a place for piling logs since 2002.09.12 as the successor of @Miraaai (@みらぁ～い, run by Mr Chinen) born on 2001.04.01, and "mirailog" (run by Mr Araki), which had worked since 2003.06.14.

**2003.06.05**
Mr Shiba's "Nikki-chou" (日記ちょう) started on Hatena Diary. After that, blogs became popular among Ayashii World users, and creative users left the BBS more and more.

> [!note]
> Hatena Diary (はてなダイアリー) was one of the most popular Japanese blog services at the time, especially among internet-savvy users. A lot of former underground users moved their activity there, as you can see on 2004.02.06 and 2004.11.01.

**2003.07**
Ayashii World@Cocos Islands (@ココス諸島) started to be run by a volunteer. The administrator was Mr BulkHead. For reference, the free accounts were apparently going to be provided until 2006.01.31.

> [!note]
> The name follows the same joke as the Christmas Island: .cx is the country code domain of Christmas Island, and .cc is the country code domain of the Cocos (Keeling) Islands. See also 2005.08.16 and 2006.01.31.

**2003.07.03**
Ayashii World@the Christmas Island renamed itself @Maria-sama ga Miteru (and its characters). The images which appeared at the bottom right of the page (at first top right) were very unpopular as harassment toward users, and although nobody knows if it was the direct cause, the amount of logs gradually decreased and the BBS got even more depopulated. Together with Remix becoming more like 2ch, even the administrator himself frequently mentioned closing; it was in confusion. Actually, users who had supported the island in the early days put more weight on their individual activities. For better or worse, this drove out the fundamentalists who were, so to speak, the natives of the island.

> [!note]
> Maria-sama ga Miteru (マリア様がみてる), so-called Marimite, is a light novel series about girls at a Catholic school, which became an anime in 2004. The BBS name changed to the names of its characters, as if teasing the anti-anime fundamentalists who had gathered on the island since the @Wakana era (Part 1, 1999.09.26). See also @Puchiko (Part 2, 2000.08.06).

**2003.07.21**
Honten residents registration (本店住民登録) started. Later the administrator personally collected various templates in "Mainly for pasting at Honten" (主として本店に貼り付けるための), which made up for the weak points of Smithsonian Museum and became its successor.

**2003.Summer**
Around this time, the amount of logs of @Mirai went beyond ds-Honten and became the biggest in the Ayashii World. The average of logs each day was just under 5MB.

**2003.08.10**
The "Shimo uproar" (シモ騒動) suddenly broke out on @ML. Around 1300 mails flew around until it settled down, and during the two weeks of the uproar there was a lot of damage, like overflowing mailboxes and frozen accounts.

**2003.08.25**
The server of the @Zantei shelter moved from ge.ws33.arena.ne.jp to 533.pr.arena.ne.jp.

**2003.08.27**
"strange overview" (so-called SOV) started. On 2004.03.07, a shelter of it by another person also started.

**2003.10.26**
"Ayashii Museum of Modern Art" (あやしい近代美術館), so-called "MOMA", started as the successor of Metropolitan Museum.

**2003.11**
Ayashii Soccer@Nishigaoka vanished because of the hypermart trouble. The administrator was Mr Arikui.

**2003.11.03**
The original @Zantei (so-called Honke, 本家), which had been open but dormant, restarted with the return of Mr Gikoneko and Mr Gesu Nuts. Actually it was Namaire's Zantei shelter which had protected the name of @Zantei, so there was criticism like "what are you going to do now after leaving it for nearly a year?". They admitted that Mr Namaire's BBS was the actual @Zantei, and chose their own way. As a part of it, they showed an orientation toward the outside, like selling goods using Mr Gikoneko's character, and links to famous internet magazines. Also, the colouring of the BBS which started on 2003.11.29 surprised and impressed a lot of users.

**2003.11.11**
Mr MONMONMON, the creator of "Ayashii Flash" (ぁゃιぃ Flash), passed away after a long illness. My condolences.

**2003.11.12**
Rhizome, run by Mr Mana, closed except for some topics because of its data transfer volume.

**2003.11.27**
The first arrests over the file sharing software Winny. An idiot pasted the URL of ds-Honten into the related 2ch thread, and the participant display of ds-Honten went over 1600 people; it was abnormal.

> [!note]
> Winny was a Japanese P2P file sharing software developed by an anonymous 2ch user known as "47-shi" (47氏), and was hugely popular around 2002–2004. The first two users were arrested in Kyoto for copyright infringement. Over 1600 people at once was an enormous number for an Ayashii World BBS.

## 2004

**2004.01**
Because virtualave became a paid service, a lot of personal, specialized and shelter BBS of Ayashii World users vanished. The next month hypermart also became paid; a lot of people must have felt deeply moved by the end of the free servers which had silently supported the prosperity of the satellite BBS around Ayashii World.

**2004.01.16**
Ayashii World Z stopped running its BBS (when you accessed it, telnet started).

**2004.02.04**
Mr office, who was a researcher at Kyoto University, was arrested over the personal information leak incident of the Association of Copyright for Computer Software (ACCS). He used to visit Ayashii World in the Shiba era.

> [!note]
> In 2003.11 he demonstrated at a security seminar that personal information could be extracted from the ACCS website through a vulnerability, and showed some of the leaked data. The arrest was a big topic among Japanese hackers because the ACCS was the organisation fighting against file sharing and warez.

**2004.02.06**
"@Kotonoha Informal" (＠ことのはインフォーマル) run by Mr Hideaki Matsunaga (松永英明) changed its site design to imitate the Kuzuha script. A unique blog community, not based on BBS, was forming around it, mainly Ayashii World OB led by Mr Shiba and former Amezou remnants.

> [!note]
> Kotonoha was a well-known blog at the time. In 2005 it was reported that Mr Matsunaga was a former member of Aum Shinrikyo, which caused a controversy in the Japanese blogosphere. Strangely enough, this connects back to the very beginning of this history (Part 1, 1995.03.20).

**2004.03.03**
Mr Oguri, the administrator of @Makkochou, passed away. Rest in peace.

**2004.03.08**
@Salon started as a radio board exclusively for @Fashion users. It was run by Mr Inspector.

**2004.03**
Ayashii World II, whose decline reached its extreme with the amount of logs falling to one tenth of its heyday, relaxed its access restrictions. Its former pull had already gone, and the users who were unbanned reacted very coolly. Incidentally, BBS of the other streams treated strangeworld.org as a browser-crash page.

**2004.03.23**
Ayashii World@Maria-sama ga Miteru changed its name back to the Christmas Island, and removed the extremely unpopular images at the bottom right (shoujo manga style pictures and doll images). However, the situation did not get better; it showed that trust once lost is not easily regained.

**2004.03.24**
@Hiroshima Tarou was deleted. It seemed to be a calculated move, and the administrator showed little intention to revive it.

**2004.03.27**
Ayashii World@Honten Zantei (@本店暫定) started by Mr Yamato Nippon (大和日本). It was lively only on April 1st.

> [!note]
> April 1st is April Fools' Day, which has always been a big event among Ayashii World users. See also 2005.04.01.

**2004.04.09**
Ayashii World@Kabu (@株, stocks) started, based on "Ayashii Securities" (あやしい證券), which derived from Remix.

**2004.04.11**
@Hiroshima Jirou opened to the public. It was an homage to @Hiroshima Tarou, but the script was based on the Kuzuha script.

**2004.05.02**
Ayashii World@mixi was born as a community inside Mixi. The representative was Mr Ashokan.

> [!note]
> mixi was a Japanese social networking service which started in 2004.02, and it was invitation-only at the time. It became the biggest SNS in Japan in the mid-2000s.

**2004.05.10**
The developer of the file sharing software Winny was arrested on suspicion of aiding copyright infringement. Even though it was a tool made by 2ch, it became a big topic on Ayashii World too, which revealed the double standard of new users who were tied up in their own rules.

> [!note]
> The developer, Mr Isamu Kaneko (金子勇), was convicted at the first trial, but was acquitted by the Osaka High Court in 2009, and the acquittal became final in 2011.

**2004.05.13**
Mr Midorisan (Soudan-kun), the administrator of Ayashii World@Jonathan the Seagull, passed away. Why!

**2004.05.17**
@Haribemabe Island (＠ハリベマベ島), which recommended streaming broadcasts and foreign drama topics, started.

**2004.05.22**
Ayashii World@Gay squad (＠ゲイ班) started, derived from a topic on Remix.

**2004.05.23**
@INFO file uploader, which had got the domain name strangeworld.info, opened. It seemed to be Honten stream, and it was an uploader unlike any other, so it was worth keeping an eye on. The administrator was Mr Funabashi (船橋).

**2004.05.28**
"Ayashii-world survey maker" (ぁ界アンケート作成), using the Jian script, started. On the evendeeper account, its administrator also ran "@Proxomitron (former Honten Proxo)", "@Shien" (＠私怨, grudge), "Net Radio" (ネットラジヲ), "@Magajiso complement" (＠マガジソ補完), "Uploader" and so on, showing varied activity.

> [!note]
> Jian is the survey website from Part 1 (1999.04). Proxomitron is a filtering proxy software for Windows, popular among users who wanted to hide or rewrite their browser information. "Magajiso" is "Magazine" with ン swapped for ソ, the same joke as Meiso (Part 1, 1998.09.06).

**2004.06.02**
Ayashii World@Trivia (＠トリビア) started. The script was He-board, and the administrator was Mr Mana.

> [!note]
> Probably named after "Trivia no Izumi" (トリビアの泉), a popular Fuji TV variety show at the time.

**2004.06.07**
Some users flirting (クネクネ) on net radio, ignoring the feelings of most users, caused internal strife at @Fashion. Access restrictions were strengthened, and it developed into an abnormal situation where the current administrator, Mr Natsuiro, declared a break by name with Mr Kumozaru, the founder and previous administrator.

**2004.06.11**
@Hiroshima Tarou revived with a greatly changed script. The administrator said he would close it when he got bored.

**2004.07.05**
Mr Osamu opened "mostviewed", a list of uploader images, as a plugin for @Meiso.

**2004.07.16**
The name of the @Christmas Island BBS became a random display again, and a shocking countdown, "120 days until sinking" (沈没まであと120日), started, as if hinting at a closing date.

**2004.08.17**
strange overview (SOV) stopped updating. The SOV shelter became its actual successor.

**2004.09.18**
strange overview (SOV) revived with a new look and a different angle.

**2004.09.19**
Someone exploited a bug in the character limit of the name column at ds-Honten, and it stopped working around the evening. It had not recovered by the next day, so the refugees who had scattered followed the notices of volunteers and gathered at @Honten shelter (Neko-ita). On the 21st, ds-Honten finally recovered.

**2004.09.21**
@Honten Reproduction (＠本店転載) and the @Honten PR office board ended, and Honten administration information (本店管理情報) started instead.

**2004.09.21**
Ayashii World@Yuefan (＠ゆえふぁん, Twelve Girls Band) started. The administrator was Mr Matsunaga.

> [!note]
> Twelve Girls Band (女子十二楽坊, Joshi Juuni Gakubou) is a Chinese band playing traditional instruments, which had a big boom in Japan in 2003–2004. Yuefang (楽坊) is the Chinese reading of "gakubou".

**2004.09.29**
Because its administrators (Mr Gesu Nuts and Mr Gikoneko) disappeared, the original @Zantei stopped working again.

**2004.10.01**
Mr Mana published "Fuuin Sakuhin no Nazo" (封印作品の謎, The Mystery of Sealed Works) from Ohta Publishing under the name of Kenji Ando (安藤健二). Congratulations.

> [!note]
> The book is a piece of investigative journalism about Japanese works which have been withdrawn from circulation and are never re-released, such as the banned 12th episode of Ultraseven. He later became a journalist.

**2004.10.03**
Because Mr ds abandoned its administration, @Honten Reproduction revived under the administration of Mr Okayu.

**2004.10.05**
Because GeoCities changed its specification, Ayashii World@Wageo closed. Likewise, because the concept of streets disappeared, the "Ayashii Street" (Part 1, 1999.10.23) was actually wiped out.

**2004.10.15**
The scheduled sinking date of Ayashii World@the Christmas Island. However, actually nothing happened.

**2004.10.15**
Because of rumours that Remix would close with the sinking of @the Christmas Island (the cx domain), unofficial shelter BBS for each topic sprang up like bamboo shoots after the rain. As a representative one, ENERGON started.

> [!note]
> Hence "sinking": the domain strangeworld.cx belonged to the country code of Christmas Island, so the closing of the domain was called the sinking of the island.

**2004.10.21**
Ayashii World@msn, a.k.a. strgwrld@hotmail.com, started.

> [!note]
> Presumably a community on MSN Groups, which was tied to Hotmail accounts. The same goes for @MSN Clone (2005.02.22).

**2004.11.01**
Hatena Diary started to require accurate name, address and gender for user registration, so that people who disliked it started to move one after another. Mr Shiba's "Nikki-chou" also moved on 11.04. Following Mr Shiba's withdrawal from Hatena, Mr Matsunaga removed the Ayashii-style site design.

**2004.11.06**
Mr Gikoneko, who had been missing, finally showed up, and the original @Zantei revived for the third time.

## 2005

**2005.01.27**
Namaire's Zantei got trolled and stopped working. Refugees were divided between Mr Mana's shelter BBS and the original Zantei, but after the evening Namaire's Zantei revived with proxy restrictions, and the matter was settled.

> [!note]
> The original text says Kushi restriction (串制限). Kushi (串, skewer) is slang for a proxy server, so this means blocking posts from proxies. This is also where the name of Nazo BBS@Kushi-Benkei (Part 2, 2002.01.02) comes from.

**2005.01.31**
"Ayashii-world heritage shelter" (ぁ界遺産待避) started on the ug.gs domain. On 02.28, @Mirai shelter (＠みらい待避) also started to run.

**2005.02.01**
At @Honten Reproduction, "The original Ayashii Newspaper" (元祖あやしい新聞), which summarized the logs of @Honten, started its serialization.

> [!note]
> The name refers to Ayashii Shinbun, the log reading service from 1998 (Part 1, 1998.09.07).

**2005.02.22**
@MSN Clone, a.k.a. strangeclone@hotmail.co.jp, revived and restarted.

**2005.02.22**
Ayashii World@Mirai, the actual main BBS at that time, celebrated its 5th anniversary, but on the PR office page Mr Machi no Yousei made a shocking announcement of his retirement as administrator. On 02.27 he officially announced a call for successor BBS. la-kai heritage (ぁ界遺産), the representative uploader of the Ayashii World, also declared that it would stop running after @Mirai closed.

**2005.02.28**
@Mirai unofficial shelter (＠みらい非公認退避) by Mr Mana started. The BBS itself had existed since 2002.04.05. Someone who claimed to have been a Mirai fixed Handle Name user started Ayashii World@Mirai (＠みらぃ).

> [!note]
> Not to be confused with the original @Mirai (＠みらい): this one is written with a small ぃ.

**2005.03.01**
Ayashii World@Mirai II (run by Mr Tanai) started as a candidate successor of @Mirai. Also Ayashii World@qwerty (its administrator was an anonymous @Mirai user) started; candidate successors were already springing up one after another. Moving to existing shelters led by Mr Mana's, or to other main-class BBS, were also possible, and the situation was chaotic.

**2005.03.04**
Ayashii World II stopped sending users to browser crash pages, and changed it to an image of a doll with its head torn off.

**2005.03.04**
Ayashii World@Mirai (＠みらぃ), run by Mr Shoujo Maso, who had come out about his identity the day before, set up a separate BBS in a subdirectory, ぁゃιぃわーるど＠みらゐ, in case it failed to become the successor of @Mirai. Mr Tanai's @Mirai II also changed its name many times; the maneuvering over the successor continued.

**2005.03.07**
Mr Machi no Yousei officially announced on the PR office page that Ayashii World@Mirai would close on 03.13. The successor BBS run by Mr Tanai (omitted because the name changed so many times) got a function for reporting trolls.

**2005.03.08**
Mr Mana's Mirai unofficial shelter was renamed @Mirai Cside (＠みらいCside). Later it turned into a shelter for qwerty. The successor BBS run by Mr Tanai got a function for monitoring rapid consecutive posting (連アタ).

**2005.03.10**
@ds-Honten got load attacks, and as a countermeasure, entering a random number became necessary on the log retrieval page.

**2005.03.10**
The administrator of "Ayashii-world heritage shelter", who had run the @Mirai shelter, declared that he withdrew from the successor race. Aiming at independent running, he moved on the ug.gs domain and started Ayashii World ug (あやしいわーるどug). The next day the background colour became pitch black, and it turned Nazo-like. Would it become a new direction for Ayashii World together with Hiroshima Tarou?

**2005.03.10**
@Mirai (＠みらぃ) run by Mr Shoujo Maso vanished, and was unified into the BBS in the subdirectory. Its name and so on seemed to be still undecided. Also, Mr Tanai, who ran Ayashii World@Mirai Taira (＠みらい平, formerly Mirai II), said unofficially that he had no intention of being the successor. The situation was in confusion.

**2005.03.14**
Ayashii World@Mirai closed. Along with it, @Lubu Jingzhou, @Persona and other BBS under Mr Machi no Yousei's administration also closed. Only the @Extra shelter seemed to continue. The huge number of refugees scattered to candidate successor BBS, shelter BBS, other main-class BBS and so on, and it was expected that it would take a while to settle down. Thank you for your hard work, Yousei-san.

> [!note]
> The announced closing date was 03.13 (2005.03.07), but the original text records the closing on 03.14. @Mirai had been the actual main BBS of the Ayashii World since around 2003 (see 2003.Summer), so its closing was the biggest change since the Main BBS of Mr Shiba closed in 1998.

**2005.03.14**
Ayashii World@the Christmas Island hinted that it would accept refugees. A participant display was added.

**2005.03.14**
As promised, "la-kai heritage" put an end to its long history. Ura Mirai seemed to continue.

**2005.03.14**
In the evening, Mr Shoujo Maso, worried about the scattering of the refugees, practically declared his withdrawal from the successor race. He changed the name of the BBS to Ayashii World@HoshinoRuri (etc.), and converted it into a personal BBS. From 03.17, the old "Ayashii World Pink pair of boots which remained at the scene" (ぁゃιぃわーるど現場に残されたﾋﾟﾝｸのながぐつ) became its shelter.

**2005.03.14**
Ayashii World@qwerty, which overwhelmed the other candidates both in amount of logs and in number of participants, became the actual successor of @Mirai. There were a lot of unclear points such as who the administrator was and its policy, but it had no notable flaws, so it seems it got its support by a process of elimination. The reason why the successor was decided so surprisingly smoothly would be that the other candidates destroyed themselves. In other words, the reason it won might be that it only prepared the environment and did nothing else. Its administrator was anonymous, without any extra colour (personality) or self-display, which suited the temperament of the users at the time. Anyway, its true value will be tested from now on.

**2005.03.15**
"Ayashii-world heritage shelter" was renamed "From the window of la-kai" (ぁ界の窓から). At the same time, because of transfer volume and server load, it announced and carried out moving Ayashii World ug away from XREA, and separating the uploader server. On 04.06, Ayashii World ug opened up HTML tags completely except for some, becoming even more Nazo-like.

**2005.04.01**
Only for April Fools' Day, @Mirai and la-kai heritage revived for a day. They were very lively.

**2005.04.07**
Ayashii World ug closed, saying it had run out of material. The uploader was restricted by referrer.

**2005.04.08**
"Ayashii World@Mirai Taira", run by Mr Tanai, became unreachable. It revived on 04.11.

**2005.04.10**
Ayashii World ug suddenly revived as Ayashii World ug Alternative.

**2005.04.17**
Ayashii World@Lubu Bingzhou (＠呂布 并州) started on the www.strangeworld.jp server.

> [!note]
> Jingzhou (荊州), Sili (司隷, Part 2, 2002.12.17) and Bingzhou (并州) are all provinces of Han dynasty China, the era of the warrior Lu Bu (呂布) after whom the @Lubu BBS are named. Bingzhou is the province where Lu Bu was born.

**2005.04.19**
Ayashii World ug Alternative was renewed as Ayashii World@Railway (＠鉄道).

**2005.04.27**
Ayashii World@Morning Musume effectively closed. Did the remaining users take shelter at Remix?

**2005.05.09**
Remix was trolled by someone and sank. As an aftereffect, @the Christmas Island also stopped working. Someone calling themselves iRC claimed responsibility. experience also stopped working, but it is unknown whether it was related. Ayashii World@Remix (shelter) started. The islanders went to Mr Mana's shelter. Remix@strangeworld.jp (りみくす＠strangeworld.jp) started. Including the existing ENERGON and others, successors sprang up one after another.

> [!note]
> The map at the top of the original Japanese page describes "the artificial planet iRC" as "a troublesome planet with a lot of residents who love disturbances, who make a fuss on other planets every day". It probably refers to the users of !strange@IRC (Part 2, 2000.04.04).

**2005.05.12**
Ayashii World@the Christmas Island and Remix@the Christmas Island revived.

**2005.05.17**
The administrator of ENERGON started @Reproduction integration (tentative) (＠転載統括（仮）), promoting reproduction for each BBS.

**2005.06.20**
Again, the Christmas Island practically declared its closing. 116 days until the cancellation of WebARENA Suite.

**2005.07.22**
Entering a random number when retrieving logs at Ayashii World@Honten was removed, making it easier to use.

**2005.07.23**
Ayashii World@Past log search (＠過去ログ検索) started. It was run by Mr Shion (しおん), who also ran the SOV shelter.

**2005.08.16**
An HDD trouble occurred at strangewold.cc, and all of its services stopped. Along with it, various satellite sites (MOMA, ぁW and other BBS) vanished at once.

**2005.08.29**
At strangewold.cc, a notice of recovery from the major failure. More than half of the data was lost.

> [!note]
> The original text spells it "strangewold.cc" both times; presumably it means strangeworld.cc, a .cc (Cocos Islands) domain, like Ayashii World@Cocos Islands (2003.07).

**2005.09.10**
Honten Disk System? (本店でぃすくしすてむ？) started, together with Honten Oekaki (本店おえかき, drawing board). The administrator was Mr Kickback (きっくばっく).

> [!note]
> Probably a pun on the Famicom Disk System.

**2005.09.10**
"From the window of la-kai" started a shelter uploader, which was renamed "Noto Penis!!(;\`Д´)" (能登ﾍﾟﾆｽ!!(;\`Д´)) on 09.14.

**2005.09.11**
Ayashikatta World@Omanchin Railway (あやしかったわーるど＠おまんちん鐵道) started as the successor of Ayashii World ug.

> [!note]
> Ayashikatta is the past tense of ayashii, so the name means "the world that was strange".

**2005.09.13**
Kyoko no Haru@Meiso (虚構の春＠メイソ) vanished, because its rental server ended its service.

**2005.09.14**
Under Mr Osamu's administration, Katei no Koufuku Meiso BBS (家庭の幸福メイソ掲示板) started as the official shelter of Kyoko no Haru. Namiteba and Pikachu also revived. On 09.17 it finally called itself Ayashii World Meiso (あやしいわーるどメイソ).

> [!note]
> Kyoko no Haru (虚構の春, Fictitious Spring) and Katei no Koufuku (家庭の幸福, The Happiness of the Family) are both titles of short stories by the novelist Osamu Dazai (太宰治), whose given name is the same 治 as the administrator's.

**2005.09.14**
"From the window of la-kai" and "Ayashii World@Railway" vanished. It was announced that their administrator had passed away.

**2005.09.15**
As the successor of "From the window of la-kai", "Noto Penis!!(;\`Д´)" was renamed "Sanzen la-kai" (三千ぁ界).

> [!note]
> A pun on Sanzen Sekai (三千世界, "three thousand worlds"), a Buddhist term for the whole universe, with sekai (world) replaced by la-kai (ぁ界).

**2005.09.29**
@the Christmas Island and Remix strengthened their access restrictions. It was practically a relocation.

**2005.10.20**
Ayashikatta World@Omanchin Railway was renamed Nazo@Omanchin Railway (なぞ＠おまんちん鐵道). On 10.22 it was renamed again, to Ayashii World ug@Omanchin Railway (あやしいわーるどug＠おまんちん鐵道).

**2005.10.23**
Mr Funabashi of "strangeworld.info" founded Ayashii World@IT Dokata BBS (＠ITドカタ掲示板).

> [!note]
> IT Dokata (IT土方, "IT construction labourer") is Japanese internet slang for programmers working long hours under harsh conditions at subcontracting companies.

**2005.11.07**
Ayashii World@Hinata (＠ひなた) belatedly raised its flag as a successor of @Mirai, for refugees who were tired of the tension at @qwerty or had been caught up in its bans, and absorbed refugees.

**2005.11.18**
An uploader, "Kokage" (木陰), was set up exclusively for Ayashii World@Hinata. The administrator was Mr Hinata. On 11.21, @Hinata stopped working because of trolling by someone. For now the entrance seemed to be restricted by cookies. Its amount of logs was already comparable to II. On 11.25 it stopped the cookie restriction, and returned to normal operation.

> [!note]
> Hinata (ひなた) means a sunny place, and Kokage (木陰) means the shade of a tree.

**2005.11.27**
@Fashion changed its background colour to white. Furthermore, on 11.29 it changed it to `#007f7f` and renamed itself The first experience of @Fashion BBS (はじめての＠ふぁっしょん掲示板). Back to its roots? On (2006.)02.17 it changed back to `#004040` again.

> [!note]
> `#007f7f` is the colour of The first experience of Ayashii World, and @Fashion itself first started as Hajiaya@Fashion with that colour (Part 1, 1999.05).
