# A History of Ayashii World Part 1

This is an English translation of [Ayashii World no Rekishi](http://f16.aaacafe.ne.jp/~stwalker/) (あやしいわーるどの歴史; The History of Ayashii World), enhanced with extra links, archives, and more.

## 1995

**1995.03.20**

The so-called Tokyo subway sarin attack committed by Aum Shinrikyo occurs.

**1995.04**

Shiba (しば) establishes a music-oriented PATIO (a forum-like service provided by [[NIFTY-Serve]] named *musicaholic*, which covers his personal music interests (UK Rock, Techno).

**1995 Summer-Autumn**

Shiba is impressed by *Kasumigaseki* (霞ヶ関), a game based on the Tokyo subway sarin attack and a pioneer in the genre of *Unscrupulous games*  (不謹慎ゲーム), and he starts a [[NIFTY-Serve|NIFTY Home Party]] (a simple anonymous BBS service) named *Ayashii World* (あやしいワールド; Strange World). It closes soon afterwards.

After a brief period of inactivity, and as the drastic shift from the earlier forms of Pasokon Tsūshin to the Internet occurs, Shiba begins to look at the possibilities that lie in pedophilic images. He wonders if pedophilic images, which are strictly regulated in other countries, could be an underground culture that is unique to Japan.

> [!note]
> "Nifty-Serve" one of the online service (パソコン通信, Pasokon Tsūshin) provider. The Nifty-Serve had provided since 1987 to 2006 officially. The service was not exactly same as the Internet because they provide their own servers, and users access to the servers. Around 1995, Nifty-Serve was the biggest provider at the time and had a million of users. See also: [Pasokon Tsūshin(JP Wikipedia)](http://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%91%E3%82%BD%E3%82%B3%E3%83%B3%E9%80%9A%E4%BF%A1)
>
> "Home Party" is like BBS script. The function was only UserID, Title, and 20 line sentence limits. It is very primitive, isn't it?
>
> "Kasumigaseki" is a game based on Sarin gas attack on the Tokyo subway. The game was released underground and being popular among underground BBS comunity. After the fad of the game, some media reported the game, so that it was actually well-know game in the end. [地下鉄サリン事件](http://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9C%B0%E4%B8%8B%E9%89%84%E3%82%B5%E3%83%AA%E3%83%B3%E4%BA%8B%E4%BB%B6)

**1995.08.15**

[Kowloon Kurosawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kowloon_Kurosawa) launches the [grassroots BBS](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%8D%89%E3%81%AE%E6%A0%B9BBS) *Tokyo Isoternet* (東京イソターネット). Later, he starts *Kokuchi-kun* (告知君; Notice-kun), a BBS where the content is called *Kaibunshou Hozonkan* (怪文書保存館; The Archive of Anonymous Defamation Documents). This would have a great influence on future log archival sites.

> [!note]
> "Kusanone BBS" unlike Pasokon-Tsushin, is a free BBS system. Nifty-Serve or the providers charged users a fee to connect the server, but Kusanone BBS is free, which means costed only phone bill.
>
> "log preservation website" can be recently found a lot of 2ch log websites. [2ちゃんねる系ブログ](http://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/2%E3%81%A1%E3%82%83%E3%82%93%E3%81%AD%E3%82%8B%E7%B3%BB%E3%83%96%E3%83%AD%E3%82%B0)
>
> Surprisingly Kowloon Kurosawa has still held the website on the Internet since 1997 though Original Grass-Root BBS was formed in 1995: [Kokuchi Kun](http://www.hehehe.net/users/kowloon/), [Kaibunsho Hozonkan](http://www.hehehe.net/library/).

## 1996

**1996.04**

Aisuta (あいすた) launches *Atarashii Kaze* (新しい風; New Wind). In June 1996, the name is changed to *Ayashii World* (あやしいわーるど; Strange World), but it is said to be very unpopular because whenever anything mildly risky is posted, it is swiftly deleted.

Aisuta eventually resigns from his managerial role, and asks if anyone wants to use the board's name as they wish. There's a theory out there that says Shiba inherits the Ayashii World name this way, but the truth is completely unknown. 

> [!note]
> Actually according to the timeline, Mr. Shiba already used the name in 1995, but I have no idea which is earlier. There are no records of this available besides memories of hearsay.

**1996.04.29**

Pedo Koushaku (ペド侯爵), also known as Marquis de Pedo, launches *Nihon Lolicon Graphics* (日本ロリコングラフィックス; Japan Lolicon Graphics), which has a strong influence on Shiba.

> [!note]
> I guess the name, Japan lolita complex graphics, was inspired by [SGI Japan](http://www.sgi.co.jp/), at time the name was "Japan Silicon Graphics" (日本シリコングラフィックス) because "Lolita Complex" often abbreviates "Loli-Com" (ロリコン) in Japanese.

**1996.05.02**

[Web Urawaza Rescue](https://www.rescue.ne.jp/) (Web裏技レスキュー; Web Tricks Rescue, later known as CGI Rescue) begins distributing his [MiniBBS](https://www.rescue.ne.jp/cgi/minibbs/) bulletin board script, also known as KaniBBS (簡易BBS; SimpleBBS).

**1996.06.01**

[Kawakami Ichirou](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B2%B3%E4%B8%8A%E3%82%A4%E3%83%81%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC) (河上イチロー) launches *Der Angriff* (The Attack). It functions as a counter to existing media.

> [!note] Translator's note
> Der Angriff was the name of a [newspaper operated by the Nazi party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Der_Angriff) in Nazi Germany.

**1996.08.06**

Vladimir launches the Japanese version of [*InfoVlad*](https://web.archive.org/web/19970410065820/http://www.infovlad.com/) (previously only available in English). On 1997.08.17, the [*HACK JAPONAISE RING*](https://web.archive.org/web/19970410071120/http://www.infovlad.com/underground/asia/japan/) [webring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webring) is launched on InfoVlad.

**1996.08.21**

Shiba notices that [Pedo Koushaku's bulletin board](https://web.archive.org/web/19961229153737/http://www.digitaleden.com/h10/a922.htm) makes use of a free and anonymous rental service. By using [PostOne](https://web.archive.org/web/19961218232843/http://post1.com/) 's free email forwarding service, [Tripod](https://web.archive.org/web/19961023234728/http://www.tripod.com:80/) 's free web hosting, and [Digital Eden](https://web.archive.org/web/19961229104124/http://www.digitaleden.com/) 's free bulletin board service, ==*[Ayashii World](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%81%82%E3%82%84%E3%81%97%E3%81%84%E3%82%8F%E3%83%BC%E3%82%8B%E3%81%A9)* (あやしいわーるど; Strange World) finally makes its way onto the Internet. This is where the history of Ayashii World as an anonymous bulletin board begins.==

Since Digital Eden's security is nonexistent, Ayashii World is immediately christened with bulletin board trolls, and so it is off to a turbulant start (it appears that Tanai (棚井) is among the attackers \*laughs\*). The main topics of discussion are *pedoneta* (ペドネタ; pedophilia-related stories/material) and mainstream subculture trends, which reflects Shiba's intentions. Photographs of Shiba participating in offline events are released publicly, and there seems to be an idyllic atmosphere.

> [!note] Translator's note
> The URL for [Ayashii World's Digital Eden room](https://web.archive.org/web/19961229120749/http://www.digitaleden.com/h6/a575.htm) is known, but unfortunately the service closed several days before the earliest archive available was created. However, the [page displaying Ayashii World as being listed on Digital Eden](https://web.archive.org/web/19961229104240/http://www.digitaleden.com/h6/daicyou.htm) is available.

> [!note]
> Pasokon-Tsushin required user ID, so that administrator could identify who posted easily. The Internet, however, is rather difficult than Pasokon-Tsushin at the time.

**1996.09**

On Ayashii World's bulletin board, [Asada Takuya](https://ohta.at.webry.info/201204/article_1.html) (あさだたくや) conceives the idea of a truly liberty-oriented newsgroup on [Netnews](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%8D%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%E3%83%8B%E3%83%A5%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B9) (see: [Usenet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usenet)) in response to the feudalistic and hard-headed administration found on the *fj.\** heirarchy (fj stands for *From Japan*). As a result, many Ayashii regulars of this time participate in the newly-formed *[japan.\*](http://usenet.trigofacile.com/hierarchies/index.py?see=JAPAN)* hierarchy.

Tanai, in addition to spreading mp3 files around the world (as the creator of [*YunaSoft MP3 Encoder*](https://web.archive.org/web/19981212034259/http://www.yunasoft.gr.jp/)), is also the founder of [*japan.fetish.lolita*](https://groups.google.com/g/japan.fetish.lolita). Following this:

1. *japan.binaries.pictures.lolita* is created.
2. *japan.fetish.lolita* is abolished.
3. *japan.binaries.pictures.erotica* is created.
4. *japan.binaries.pictures.lolita* is abolished.
5. *japan.binaries.pictures.erotica.lolita* is created.

...which brings us to the present day. Due to conflicts with distributors over restrictions on erotic material, Tanai pulls out of the project halfway through, but he later becomes a leading figure in the proliferation of pedophilic images on japan.\*.

> [!note] Translator's note
> [Archived posts regarding Tanai](http://strangewalker.web.fc2.com/tanaisan.htm).

**1996.11.03**

[*Ueno Haruki no HoMePaGe*](https://web.archive.org/web/19981206045146/http://home.intercity.or.jp/users/ytera/ueno/index.html) (うえのはるきのほめぱげ; Ueno Haruki's Homepage) is launched, which gives birth to the legendary delusional character [DD no Tetsu-san](https://web.archive.org/web/19981201034318/http://home.intercity.or.jp/users/ytera/ueno/tetupro.html) (ＤＤの哲さん).

> [!note]
> The original text says denpa-character(電波キャラ), which means literally a radio wave character, but turned to be crazy character. [電波系](http://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/電波系)

**1996.11.30**

*Chikadou Iriguchi* (地下道入口; Underpass Entrance), Japan's first fully-fledged UG (underground) site created in 1995.05, is closed. In the aftermath, Chikadou refugees pour into Ayashii World, and hacking/cracking-related topics take the center stage. Incidentally, a site that pays homage to Chikadou Iriguchi named [*Gesuidou Iriguchi*](https://web.archive.org/web/19981207064817/http://members.tripod.com/~popochan/gesuido.htm) (下水道入口; Sewer Entrance) is launched.

> [!note] Translator's note
> The only [archives of Chikadou Iriguchi](https://web.archive.org/web/19990128163534/http://www.yk.rim.or.jp:80/~jun/ug.html) that exist were made *after* the site's closure, leaving only the final message from the admin.

**1996.12.23**

==With Digital Eden's free service coming to an end, Ayashii World moves to *Kenjinkai* (県人会) which is run by Tome (とめ).== It uses a so-called "ikkou keijiban" script (一行掲示板; single-line bulletin board), and in addition to Ayashii, many other UG-style bulletin boards also utilize these. Among them is *Guess Who Keijiban* (ゲスッ掲示板; Guess Who Bulletin Board), which will be created in 1997.02.

Incidentally, by the time of Digital Eden's end, Ayashii World's hit counter has exceeded 400,000 hits.

> [!note] Translator's note
> None of the URLs for Kenjinkai's sites were archived as far as I'm aware, but they were said to be as follows:
>
> - www.kenjin.com
> - skyport.com/keinjin/

> [!note]
> Still you can see how the Guess BBS at Kenjinkai was: [ゲスッメモリアル](http://museum.scenecritique.com/home/guess/gesubbs/kenjinkai/312.html). Probably the Ayashii World BBS at Kenjinkai was also very similar.

**1996.12.26**

Shiba seperates AyashiiWorld@Kenjinkai (あやしいわーるど＠県人会) into *97* for general topics, and *2000* for technical topics.

On 2000 in particular, some of the regulars who will later go on to become members of *Guess Who* (ゲスッ) are openly discussing raiding other bulletin boards. Kenjinkai gets flooded with complaints and is forced to shut down temporarily.

It could be said that Ayashii World 2000 founded the notorious bulletin board destruction group Guess Who, but Shiba himself consistently declares that he opposes bulletin board trolling.

> [!note]
> Often you can see "Guess", but actually this is the moment of birth of Guess in a sense.

## 1997

**1997.01.27**

Legendary hacker KKC makes a post to his bulletin board, *Hackerz BBS*, that continues to get passed down to this day. He writes " あまりに簡単すぎる英語で書きました。 You is a big fool man. Hahahaha." ("I wrote it in extremely simple English. You is a big fool man. Hahahaha.")

> [!note] Translator's note
> A [manual archive of KKC's post](https://web.archive.org/web/20010107214500/http://www.nurs.or.jp:80/~nspixp/kkc/index.html) and the posts surrounding it is available. ([mirror](https://hackerzbbs.tripod.com/))

> [!note]
> Hacker in this meaning is actually cracker. At the time, Japanese underground users confused the definition although still a lot of people who are not familiar with computer technology misunderstand or mix both of terms.

**1997.02.20**

*Idol Neta World* (アイドルネタわーるど; Idol Material World) is launched. It's commonly referred to as AiNeta (あいねた).

**1997.03**

The members of *2000* who instigated the bulletin board trolling move to the aforementioned *Guess Who Keijiban* (ゲスッ掲示板; Guess Who Bulletin Board), and thus the infamous bulletin board destruction group *Guess Who* (ゲスッ) is formed.

Upon accepting a trolling request, they will use any means possible to render the target bulletin board unusable. They famously post *Aum Song* (オウムソング; songs produced by the Aum Shinrikyo religious cult) on the [*Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Agriculture,_Forestry_and_Fisheries_\(Japan\)) ' (農水省; nousuishou) bulletin board, as well as attack the bulletin boards of [*Soka Gakkai*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soka_Gakkai) (創価学会; a Japanese Buddhist religious movement) and [Fuji TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuji_TV) (フジテレビ; Fuji Terebi).

According to Alice (アリス・リデル; Alice Liddel, a famous member of Guess Who), over 900 bulletin boards were rendered unusable by 1998.05. At a later date, Alice becomes hostile towards Ayashii World.

> [!note] Translator's note
> [Archived posts regarding Guess Who and Alice](http://strangewalker.web.fc2.com/alice_4.htm).

> [!note]
> Guess BBS was not only discussing/doing trolling, but oddly accepted requests from normal users: Reasons to troll were mostly personal enmity, or actually could be any reasons.

**1997.03.25**

*AyashiiWorld@jcat* (あやしいわーるど＠jcat) is launched.

**1997.03.26**

Kenjinkai's service comes to an end. There's a theory that says that Ayashii World is the cause, but Shiba refutes it.

**1997.03.31**

*AyashiiWorld@K&T* (あやしいわーるど＠Ｋ&Ｔ) is launched. Longer posts are now possible.

> [!note] Translator's note
> The URL for K&T's service was said to be as follows, but there are no archives of the site to the best of my knowledge: http://www13.pair.com/ktsoft/

**1997.04.01**

AyashiiWorld@jcat is closed.

**1997.04.17**

Diary site [*17-sai Mizuka no Sonzai ～Kokoro no Kagami～*](https://web.archive.org/web/19971211004932/http://www.kumagaya.or.jp/~mizuka/mizuka-hp.htm) (17歳 みずかの存在 ～心の鏡～; 17-year-old Mizuka's Existence ～Mirror of the Heart～) is launched. She becomes a forerunning [net idol](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%8D%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%E3%82%A2%E3%82%A4%E3%83%89%E3%83%AB) (ネットアイドル).

> [!note]
> The author had written a blog until 2007 presumably: [17のセレンディップ ～ふとした偶然をきっかけに、幸せをつかむこと～](http://ameblo.jp/mizuka17/entry-10032851442.html). In this article, she said she was 17 years old at the time and was normal girl, unlike other Net-Idol. Later she even wrote an article about this experience on a book, Computer Evil manual 3 ([コンピュータ悪のマニュアル〈3〉](http://www.amazon.co.jp/コンピュータ悪のマニュアル〈3〉-backrs-All-networkers/dp/4887185421)). It was mentioned by a book which is about the Internet culture and history of general Japanese underground scene, A hisory of the Internet scene in Japan which is not being written on a textbook ([教科書には載らないニッポンのインターネットの歴史教科書](http://www.amazon.co.jp/教科書には載らないニッポンのインターネットの歴史教科書-ばるぼら/dp/4798106577)). Moreover, Ayashii people were involved attacking her website probably, and the book, Computer Evil manual 3, was written by some Ayashii users for sure.

**1997.05.01**

[*Mr.K Ikari no Homepage*](https://web.archive.org/web/19980109045040/http://www.angry-web.com/) (Mr.K 怒りのホームページ; Mr.K's Angry Homepage), the headquarters of criticism/blasting-oriented bulletin boards, is launched.

**1997.05.27**

In the Suma ward of Kobe, a series of elementary school murders known as the *[Sakakibara case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_child_murders)* (酒鬼薔薇事件; Sakakibara jiken; also known as the Kobe child murders) occurs.

**1997.06.29**

==The alleged real name of the suspect in the Sakakibara case (a 14-year-old boy at the time) is posted to Ayashii World 2000 (K&T). As a result, the bulletin board falls into chaos and goes offline.==

**1997.07.02**

[*Hando!*](https://web.archive.org/web/19991003055649/http://plaza10.mbn.or.jp/~fragile/hando!/sindex.html) (反動！; Reaction!) is launched in response to all the online uproar regarding the Sakakibara case. [Mana](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%AE%89%E8%97%A4%E5%81%A5%E4%BA%8C) (まな) is the administrator.

> [!note] Translator's note
> Mana is also known as Tsubasa Shibahime (芝姫つばさ), and his real name is Kenji Ando (安藤健二).

**1997.07.05**

Similarly to Ayashii World 2000 (K&T), the alleged real name of the suspect gets posted to *AyashiiWorld@Hihihi* (あやしいわーるど＠ひひひ), and the board gets deleted. The administrator of this bulletin board is unknown.

> [!note] Translator's note
> A site containing [screenshots of AyashiiWorld@Hihihi and several other bulletin boards that were shut down around the time of the Sakikibara case uproar](https://debagame.tripod.com/bbs.htm) is available.

> [!note]
> hihihi (ひひひ) is an expression of laughing similar to hehehe.

**1997.07.05**

*AyashiiWorld@interactia* (あやしいわーるど＠interactia) is launched.

**1997.07.07**

*UGTOP*, an influential hacker group in Japan's UG (underground) world, is formed. It is organized by [Hideharu Ishikawa](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%9F%B3%E5%B7%9D%E8%8B%B1%E6%B2%BB) (石川英治), a man who will later go on to become chairman of [ARTEMIS Inc.](https://www.artemis-jp.com/) (株式会社アルテミス). Many members of UGTOP regularly use Ayashii World as their hangout, as well as ROOtZerO of the hacker group *DEFCON ZERO*. At this time, the mental image that *Ayashii* conjures up in the minds of those who have heard of it is probably a den of super hackers.

> [!note]
> Although the [UGTOP](http://www.ugtop.com/) website says it has been run since 1996.

**1997.07.20**

[*Uganiku no Homepage*](https://web.archive.org/web/20010404012612/http://home.raidway.ne.jp/~okada923/) (ウガニクのホームページ; Uganiku's Homepage), famous for its [*Onanii Nikki*](https://web.archive.org/web/20010409053554/http://home.raidway.ne.jp/~okada923/diary.htm) (オナニー日記; Masturbation Diary), is launched.

**1997.08.01**

[*Ougonsekai! Ougontaiken!*](https://web.archive.org/web/20000619033436/http://fate.ug.to/) (黄金世界!黄金体験!; Golden World! Golden Experience!), which hosts the PO Box site [*Yuganda Unmei*](https://web.archive.org/web/20010302100329/http://yuga2000.bank-sakura.com/cgi-bin/list.cgi) (歪んだ運命) (nicknamed *YugaUn* (ゆがうん)), is launched.

> [!note]
> A PO Box is a BBS which is able to create threads by users freely. According to a chart of the Internet scene, [インターネットの歴史年表](http://websitemap.michikusa.jp/webhistory.html), the script was based on Yui board.

**1997.08.05**

Amezou (あめぞう) launches his bulletin board link collection site (a pioneer among those that would popularize underground culture).

> [!note] Translator's note
> The original URL was http://home.onestop.net/amezo/index.htm, and there is [a manual archive available](https://web.archive.org/web/20200125032210/http://history.amebbs.com/1997/1997_0805_n002main-ame.htm) demonstrating how it looked.

> [!note]
> His website from the beginning was only BBS portal, without thread floating BBS.

**1997.08.10**

[*Teacup*](https://www.teacup.com/), a MiniBBS-based bulletin board rental service, is launched.

**1997.08**

*Licentious Notice Board* (commonly known as *LNB*), a staple among image-attachment bulletin boards, is launched. In addition to being home to many creators of collages (コラ; composite images; photoshops (usually pornographic)), the site is blessed with abundant material and high-quality residents, and is a great bulletin board of this era. However, once the original LNB managed by fujinami closes, it will rapidly lose momentum due to the userbase splitting into various factions and spreading out to many different places.

Many popular attachment boards will derive from LNB, such as [*Yuumeijin Talk*](https://web.archive.org/web/19981203113220/http://www.tcup.com/03/torizoku.html) (有名人トーク; Celebrity Talk) and [*Rutika no Kobeya*](https://web.archive.org/web/19981202155501/http://www.enjoy.ne.jp/~makochan/cgi-bin/hime.cgi) (るてぃかの小部屋; Rutika's Little Room). Many LNB refugees (such as TAK) will also pour into Ayashii.

> [!note] Translator's note
> The original URL for fujinami's LNB was http://cgi.osk.3web.ne.jp/~fujinami/NoticeBoard.cgi, but there is no archive available as far as I'm aware. You can find this link listed on the [archive of Amezou's link site circa 1997](https://web.archive.org/web/20000620053835/http://taitirou.virtualave.net/cgi-bin/bbs.cgi), with the following description:
>
> > There's lots of idol collages
>
> There was also [a later location for Yuumeijin Talk](https://web.archive.org/web/20000620053835/http://taitirou.virtualave.net/cgi-bin/bbs.cgi).

> [!note]
> I personally think LNB was the genuine original founder of imageboard culture in Japan. according to the book, A hisory of the Internet scene in Japan which is not being written on a textbook ([教科書には載らないニッポンのインターネットの歴史教科書](http://www.amazon.co.jp/教科書には載らないニッポンのインターネットの歴史教科書-ばるぼら/dp/4798106577)). The book says "since 1998, using imageboard.cgi and uploading images on the server has been common instead of posting a URL link of images(do you understand the difference?), so that I rarely found that type of BBS. You just need to understand for the present that porn picture images BBS which you can find a lot on the Internet in present can be traced back to its origin, LNB users' enthusiasm. "
>
