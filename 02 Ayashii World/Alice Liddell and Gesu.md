# Alice Liddell and Gesu'

**Alice Liddell** (アリス・リデル; also Alice P Liddell, ありす, and the older handle **DUKE**) was a pseudonymous figure in Japan's late-1990s underground bulletin-board scene. Alice is linked to the board-raiding group **Gesu'** (ゲスッ), to the flooding script **PerlDUKE**, and to the pressure widely blamed for Shiba leaving [[Ayashii World Hub|Ayashii World]].

How large Alice's role in Gesu' really was is disputed, and much of the confusion comes from Alice. The surviving posts deny, half-admit and joke about the same events at different times. This article sets those versions side by side instead of choosing one.

> - **Alice's own posts:** most of D1 and D2, the 2002 diary in D3, and the posts signed アリス or アリス・リデル at the top of D4 (lines 14–119). Alice says impersonators were common (D1:799, D2:422, D3:728), so even these are not certain.
> - **Other people's claims:** most of D4, including hostile profiles and fan histories.
> - **Parody:** the Gesu' "Empire" bulletins, the "Gesu' police" news items and the *Evangelion* skit (D3:850–914). These show how people saw Alice, not what happened.
> - **Dates:** most D4 posts and the first D3 posts give only month, day and weekday. The years here (1997/1998 for D4, 2002 for D3) are inferred from weekdays.

## Who Alice was

The handle comes from Alice Liddell, the girl behind Lewis Carroll's *Alice* books. Asked in 2000 whether they were the "real" Alice Liddell, Alice simply replied with the historical Alice's birth and death dates (D1:687). A hostile 1998 profile also lists DUKE, HolyDragon, Maple and other handles (D4:491–495). The Encyclopedia confirms Alice "a.k.a. DUKE", and a 1998 parody roster was corrected for listing Alice and DUKE as two people (D4:575).

By 2000 Alice wrote in a playful style, ending sentences with the verbal tic りゅん, and described a life far from the underground. Alice worked in small-town video production, shooting bridal videos and cable-TV jobs, and built PCs for local customers on the side (D1:20–140, D1:223–233, D2:397). Alice insisted the internet had become a minor sideline, about 5% of their life (D1:183–190, D2:860). By 2002 the りゅん had gone, and Alice called themself an ordinary salesman (D3:734).

These details are also unreliable. Within about six weeks in 2000, Alice wrote of owing a bank ¥32 million, of having no debt at all, and of having borrowed ¥45 million (D1:426, D2:840, D2:921). Alice's stated philosophy explains why: much of what they wrote was *neta* (ネタ, material or a bit), and in their view anyone who took it literally had fallen into a trap (D2:939, D3:742–758).

## What Gesu' was

Gesu' grew out of the regulars of **Ayashii World 2000**, the technical board Shiba split off on 1996-12-26. A Gesu' board opened on Kenjinkai in February 1997, and the group formed around it the following month (Encyclopedia; Part 1). It raided boards on request. Its best-known attacks hit the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (flooded with Aum Shinrikyo songs), Soka Gakkai and Fuji TV (Part 1).

The main methods were automated post floods, booby-trapped HTML tags and mail bombs, often sent through proxies. A smaller number of members also ran "social attacks", which were stalker-like campaigns to wreck a target's personal relationships (D4:449–460). Well-known names included:

- **あほあほ / REBON:** the Gesu' board admin (D1:991, D3:974, D4:562)
- **黒マグロ (Kuromaguro):** known for offline social attacks (D1:665–669, D3:938)
- **笹川良い子 (Sasagawa Yoiko):** ran the Abunai board (D3:926)
- **bfm:** credited with writing most of Gesu's scripts (Encyclopedia)

Gesu' was less organised than its legend suggests. Asked what Kuromaguro's role was, Alice said there were no roles: people turned up when bored, trolled if they felt like it, and went to bed (D1:659–663). The "Gesu' Empire", with its fleets and commanders (D4:350, D4:557–590), was a running joke. The Encyclopedia says Gesu' disbanded after infighting in October 1997, yet people went on using the name: there was talk of a "new Gesu'" in 1998 (D4:277–322), and sites named gesu2 and gesu9 were active in 2000.

> [!note] Names that get confused
> - **ゲスッ (Gesu')** is the group and its board. English sources also write Guess, Guess Who or GUESS, and gloss it as "scum".
> - **Ayashii World 2000** is the board the group came from, not the Gesu' board itself.
> - **下水道入口 (Gesuidō Iriguchi)**, the "Sewer Entrance" site, and **下衆ナッツ (Gesu Nuts)** look related but are not part of the group, as far as these sources show.

## Alice's part in Gesu'

**Tools.** A comment quoted in the Encyclopedia places Alice among the Ayashii 2000 crowd and credits Alice (as DUKE) and Magi with laying the foundations of the image-tag attacks. Alice's own 1998 history describes using the 1184 chat as a test bed for attack tags, log tampering, WinNUKE and PerlDUKE, purely out of boredom (D4:79–84). Alice later claimed the final "pure" versions of the PHASER flood tools, VisualPHASER-SE and MillenniumPHASER (D1:160–163).

**Gesu' Memorial.** Alice's site ゲスッメモリアル ("Gesu Memo") was the scene's public face, and a poster in 2000 remembered it advising raiders to start a quarrel first (D2:710). HighwayBLOOD, the high-school cracker in the January 1998 PonPonNet break-in, posted updates on its board (Part 1; Encyclopedia). The site closed around July 1998 (Encyclopedia). One anonymous poster blamed the closure for what followed: displaced Gesu' teens flooded Ayashii and wore Shiba down (D4:264–273).

**Spokesperson.** Alice was the Gesu'-linked person the press went to. Alice appeared on NHK on 1998-02-27 in connection with PonPonNet (Part 1). They said a news organisation's city desk checked information with them (D4:114), and they attended a meeting of ANSOC, Gesu' figures and reporters from outlets including NHK, Yomiuri and Nikkei (D4:98–101, D4:205–208). ANSOC was the anti-net-stalking group that became Gesu's main public opponent. Its head appears as Midorikawa in Alice's account and as Midōoka elsewhere. Parodists gave Alice the title of "media PR officer" (D4:574). At the same time, Alice ran a board-security site, and critics dismissed it as self-promotion (D4:155, D4:505).

**Standing.** Outsiders called Alice a leader (D4:167), a founder (D3:919) and, in jest, an emperor (D4:350). Alice's own June 1998 history counts them among the Gesu' executives, and it says the "propaganda unit" was another name for that group (D4:71–72). Three months later, Alice wrote "I am not a central figure in Gesu'" (D4:42). In 2000 Alice said they had only played there for a while and that あほあほ had started it (D1:989–991).

## The break with Ayashii World

In March 1998 Alice released PerlDUKE, a client that used sockets to flood outside boards with posts (Encyclopedia). Part 1 says it was tested on Ayashii World. Alice apologised to Shiba, but the backlash was severe: Ayashii users circulated "neck cracking" edits of Alice's NHK footage and exposed their personal details on the boards. "Alice trolling" became a pastime in its own right.

Opinion was split at the time. One board admin called PerlDUKE an experiment that exposed real weaknesses in board software (D4:642–667). Shiba, who had known DUKE since the Kenjinkai days, disagreed: DUKE simply loved attacking boards, even Gesu's internal ones, so the security talk was beside the point (D4:677–681). An underground bulletin reported that Alice had also angered "M" (apparently Magi, see D4:597), a respected figure known as the father of the IMG-tag attack, by shrugging off warnings about the script (D4:623–638).

After May 1998 it emerged that Shiba was personally involved with the Alice-trolling crowd. Alice was furious and, according to one theory, threatened a lawsuit (Part 1). The events that followed moved quickly:

- **1998-09-03:** Shiba suddenly announces Ayashii World's closure (Part 1).
- **1998-09-04:** Alice posts that they are pursuing complaints against Shiba and Shiba's publisher over a *Hacker Japan* article, but had nothing to do with the closure (D4:16–24). Alice explains they are denying it now so that silence can't be used against them later (D4:33–37).
- **Autumn 1998:** Shiba officially apologises after contact by phone and on the board (Part 1).
- **1998-12-07:** Shiba asks that no boards use the Ayashii World name, apparently under an agreement with Alice (Part 1; [[Ayashii World|Wikipedia]], note 7). Magmania, Shiba's personal site, closes the next day, and Shiba disappears from the web.
- **1999-01-24:** Honten drops the name, and accounts agree that Alice knew or had obtained its admin password (Part 1; [[2ch History Timeline]]).

Part 1 notes that Alice denied applying pressure. However, a post signed by Alice on **1999-07-14** complicates that denial. Alice says the only target they personally went after was Shiba, and blames the other attacks on impersonators. They add that their first planned social attack was aimed at the Ne-ken (ネットワーカー研究所) admin, and that when that person disappeared the target moved to Ayashii (D1:902–910). This is the closest thing in these sources to an admission, although it doesn't describe what Alice actually did.

The damage to Alice's reputation lasted. Boards that kept the Ayashii name faced vandalism, and long-time users treated Alice as a villain, though Part 1 says this faded with later generations.

## After Gesu'

Alice announced a retirement from the underground (D1:1054, D3:921), then kept reading and posting on @Mirai and Remix. Their later activity falls into a few threads.

- **The AGStar feud.** AGStar, who ran major Ayashii boards, replied "ex-convict" to Alice's posts (Part 1) and banned them (D1:735). Alice answered with mock costings of how much it would take to track AGStar down and intimidate them, then called it all *neta* (D1:697–736, D2:855–858).
- **The 2000 war with 2channel.** In August 2000 Ayashii users and Gesu' attacked 2channel's servers with tools including Phaser, until Hiroyuki apologised ([[Ayashii World|Wikipedia]]). Alice claimed no part in it and said they were on nobody's side (D1:927–943, D1:1021–1027). But Alice had told two of the newer Gesu' figures that they had no objection and could go as far as they liked (D1:635–636). They had also sent the entire contents of a site to whoever asked, and said they didn't care if unreleased attack scripts were mixed in (D1:631–633, D1:1007–1013). Alice's link to the gesuN sites is murkier. They posted files to gesu9.virtualave.net in June 2000, and they could have deleted the gesu2 board and had sent out that account's contents (D1:337–341, D1:609, D1:620–622). Gesu9 also appears as a separate person, and how that handle relates to the site isn't clear (D1:629–635).
- **Ne-ken again.** When a page about Alice was raised on Remix in October 2000, Alice called it 80% lies and predicted that it would vanish within about a month (D2:931–933). Two days later its maintainer apologised publicly and removed the personal details (D2:948–966).
- **Later *neta*.** In 2000 Alice posted a trap aimed at warez users on an upload board, and they gave advice on sabotaging a vehicle to someone with a grudge (D1:316–345, D2:637–681). In 2002 they told a story about their boss paying them ¥10,000 to take down an idol poll, wondering if this made them the world's first hired troll (D3:397–410). How much of this is true can't be determined.

Alice's fullest self-explanation is a long 2000 reply in a thread with someone signing as Hiroyuki. It describes wanting kindness from people, being treated as repulsive offline and mocked as a fake woman online, and turning to board destruction to vent that frustration (D1:1037–1059). Its polite style is closer to Alice's 1998–99 posts than to the 2000 りゅん voice, so it may be an older text reposted. It reads as sincere, but it comes from a writer who warned readers not to take them at their word.

## Errors in other vault sources

The diaries contradict or complicate several statements elsewhere in the vault:

- **"Guess BBS's administrator" / "Guess sysop":** [[4chan Pre-history]] and [[2ch History Timeline]] give Alice this title. Alice did run boards, including the Gesu' Memorial board and the PerlDUKE board (Part 1; D4:65, D4:625), but the original Gesu' board on Kenjinkai was あほあほ's (D1:991, D3:974). These English sources seem to merge the two. Part 1's "central member" is safer, and "famous member" safer still.
- **"2000 became the Gesu board":** the [[2ch History Timeline]] and [[4chan Pre-history]] treat these as one board, and [[The First Channel - Ayashii and Amezou]] places a Gesu board "within Ayashii". Part 1 and the Encyclopedia show the group coming out of 2000 but setting up its own board on Kenjinkai.
- **"Honten was hacked by Alice himself":** [[4chan Pre-history]] says this. Part 1 and the [[2ch History Timeline]] say only that Alice knew or had obtained the admin password, not how.
- **"Jan 24, 1998":** the Honten entry in the [[2ch History Timeline]] should read 1999 (Part 1).
- **"Alice denies pressure":** Part 1 is accurate, but it should be read alongside the 1999-07-14 post above.
